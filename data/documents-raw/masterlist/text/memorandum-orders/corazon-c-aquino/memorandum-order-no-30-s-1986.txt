MEMORANDUM ORDER NO. 30
August 20, 1986

=

DECLARING THE OFFICE OF THE VICE-PRESIDENT
AS A CRITICAL AGENCY OF THE NATIONAL GOVERNMENT

The Office of the Vice-President is hereby declared a
critical agency. :

The Compensation Plan for the Office of the President
(Proper) authorized under E.0. No. 530 dated March. 31, 1979
shall apply to all permanent positions in the Office of the
Vice-President effective July 1, 1986.

The Ministry of Budget and Management is directed to+
implement this Order.

By authority of the President

fon P. ARROYO
