Office of the President of the 3bhilippine:

MALACANANG, MANILA

MEMORANDUM ORDER NO. 318

The National Bureau of Investigation is hereby directed to conduct
a formal inquiry into the reported unscrupulous practices both in the
private and public sectors which cause the shortage of rice and corn in
the market.

The NBI shall submit its report and recommendations to the Office
of the President, through the Office of the Executive Secretary, not later
than November 30, 1995.

By Authority of the President:

D. TORRES ‘

Manila, October 25, 1995
