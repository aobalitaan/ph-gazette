Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the Recognition and Awarding Ceremonies of Military Personnel involved in the Anti-Terrorist Campaign and other Operations

[Delivered at the Heroes’ Hall, Malacañang, January 24, 2007]

Thank you.

Thank you very much, General Esperon.

Members of the Cabinet; Ambassador Kenney; Undersecretary Hughes and the members of the American delegation — thank you for coming here to congratulate our Filipino soldiers — bereaved families of our soldiers killed in action; our gallant men of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, congratulations!

I salute you for your courage and devotion to duty. You are the soldiers of the people in the finest tradition of the uniform. Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.

I also commend the leadership of the Chief of Staff, General Esperon and all our field commanders. Congratulations to all of you!

As we weaken the terrorist cells and bandit hordes and drive the revolutionary left to strategic defeat, we will continue to consolidate the Filipino people in the greater fight against poverty, disease, ignorance and injustice.

Kaya yung ginagawa ninyo, nilalabanan ang kalaban para mapayapa tayo, malaking bagay yon para sa ating laban kontra sa kahirapan. Kaya maraming salamat din doon.

With the all-out support and vigilance of every Filipino, we can add on to our victories and ride the tide of the people’s confidence and hope, shore up the economy and work with our neighbors and allies for a safer world.

At magagawa nating lahat ito dahil sa inyo.

You have laid and secured the road to peace and prosperity. You have repulsed the armed tormentors of the people by the gallant shield of the Filipino soldier.

And I’m very happy that tonight, we have a personal representative from President Bush, to join us in this celebration.

Our joint military training exercises with the United States constitute a spearhead against the spread of terror in the region. At the same time, we are closely coordinating with our neighbors in stepping up vigilance along our common borders to close the dragnet on the Abu Sayyaf remnants on the run. Remnants na lang sila. Retaso na lang sila dahil sa inyo. Nakuha ninyo yung mga ulo nila.

At itong ginawa ninyo, itong ginagawa ninyo, lahat itong mga inyong tagumpay na binigyan natin ng mga promotion ay malaking bagay hindi lamang sa Pilipinas kung hindi sa buong mundo.

The security of the Asia-Pacific lies in a terror-free Southeast Asia. It lies in stability in the Korean peninsula. It lies in a stable environment in the South China Sea. That is your role not only in the country but in the region and in the world.

And in the recent ASEAN meeting, we tackled these issues to provide a comprehensive umbrella of peace and security to facilitate trade and development in the region.

The Philippines is committed its share of the work.

Marami sa inyo na binigyan natin ng promotion — mga bata, mga platoon, mga bata. I want to give a special message to our young soldiers spearheading the fight against terror, because you are spearheading the fight against terror, in the same way that our young professionals and young entrepreneurs are leading the fight against poverty while helping build excellence in the entire region. Your generation will wrap up the fight against terror and poverty in no time at all and lead our nation to the ranks of the first world in the coming decades.

Pag kayo ay edad na namin sana nga nasa first world na tayo. Pero hindi natin magagawa yon kung hindi mapayapa ang ating bansa. Kaya ang unang-unang pangangailangan ay yung ginagawa ninyo. Kaya tayo’y nagkakaroon nitong pagdiriwang kasi gusto ko sa ngalan ng buong sambayanang Pilipino, magpasalamat sa inyong pagkabayani.

Kaya ginawa natin itong pagdiriwang sa Heroes’ Hall kasi kayo mga heroes kayo lahat.

To all of you, once again, congratulations! And carry on.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
