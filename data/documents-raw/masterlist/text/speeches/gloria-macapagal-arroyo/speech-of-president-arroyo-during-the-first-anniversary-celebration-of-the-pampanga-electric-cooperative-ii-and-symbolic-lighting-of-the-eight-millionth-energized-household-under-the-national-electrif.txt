Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the first anniversary Celebration of the Pampanga Electric Cooperative II and symbolic lighting of the eight millionth energized household under the National Electrification Administration’s electrification program

[Delivered at the Lubao Gymnasium, Lubao, Pampanga, August 24, 2009]

Congratulations!

Congratulations! to Father Paking, Edith Bueno, NEA, Samar Electric Cooperatives sa 8 millionth household electrification. Keti naman king Pampanga, congratulations PELCO II  king rehabilitation yu!

At keti naman kaluguran kung kabalen keti Pampanga, pung Lubao y Vice Governor Yeng Guiao, dening Congressman tamu y Mikey Arroyo ing chairman ing Committee on Energy. Y Dong Gonzales, Anna Bondoc, ampu ing congressman ning BUTIL y Pitung Guanlao. Y Secretary Ed Pamintuan ing kekatang CORD, ing kekatang host keti king Lubao y Mayor Dennis ampo ding aliwang opisyales keti, ding aliwang mayors a bisita tamu ngening aldo, and our General Manager of PELCO, General Manager Amador Guevarra at dati kanu y Ka Basil, congratulations! Kaluguran kung kabalen karing opisyales, reng pamuntuk… karing opisyal deng ali-aliwang balen, yung iba-ibang mga electric cooperative dito sa Central Luzon, congratulations sa lahat ng mga ginawa natin deng kooperatiba king kuryenti. Congratulations to all of you!

At king PELCO II king pamamye elektrisidad kareng metung a what? 177 barangay keti king Pampanga, congratulations!

And Fr. Paking Silva, kung ano ba yung ginawa namin ni Edith at ng NEA, ikaw ang talagang inspirasyon dahil nandoon ka pa sa electric cooperative outstanding ka na nung araw pa, congratulations for the 8 millionth households to be electrified!

At isang napakalaking rason kung bakit ginagawa natin itong celebration dito sa Lubao, pwede naman gawin sa ibang bayan ng Pampanga yung PELCO, pwede namang gawin doon sa Samar yung 8 millionth cooperative — and we congratulate Samar — dahil ang anak ng Lubao, ing kekatang dating Presidente Diosdado Macapagal ing megupisa ning Electrification Administration.

At ining aldong ini naalala natin ang isang taong ang pangarap ay bigyan ng elektrisidad ang pinakamalayong mga baryo ng Pilipinas. Kaya nandito pati si Bishop Parang ng Mindoro. Hello! Bishop nice to see you here.

At tayong lahat na nandito tinutuloy natin ang kaniyang panaginip, and as you have seen, thank you Father Silva and Edith for this charts that explain themselves our administration extended the biggest ever financial assistance to the electric cooperatives, five billion pesos to enable you our cooperatives to do your missionary work even in the so-called non-viable areas. And I am pleased to see that most of you have delivered, congratulations sa inyong tagumpay!

Ngayon sabi ko na kay Edith Bueno saka yung Department of Energy, dapat by the end of this year ikumpleto na natin na lahat ng barangay ng Pilipinas ay meron ng elektrisidad. At kalupa ning PELCO, I’m also pleased na yung mga electric cooperatives ay ipinag-ibayo ang inyong performance, including kalupa ning PELCO, their rate of collection, and handa na kayo ngayon sa kumpetensya sa power sector industry.

Samantala, we are accelerating our power and energy development program while developing more sources of indigenous power. The country has already attained 58 percent self-sufficiency in power. And we anticipate an even higher percentage with the full implementation of the Biofuels Act and the Renewable Energy Law, na ing principal author king House ya y Congressman Mikey Arroyo.

The full implementation of EPIRA, which we passed in our administration, will also start next year, after the power purchase agreement that we inherited will expire. This is projected to bring down power rates especially for small consumers.

Kunswelo para sa akin yung narinig kong report ni Father Paking na nagtutulungan ang mga electric cooperative para magbibay ng magandang serbisyo. Yang Hunyo inutos ku king NEA a dinan kuryente ing Bgy. Mawacat na malapit king kabundukan ning Pampanga, Bataan at Zambales nung nokarin makatuknang ding alus 200 a pamilyang Aeta. Kilub ning apulong aldo, pitung libong kilometro linya ing mikabit kapamilitan na ning Task Force Kapatid. Congratulations, Task Force Kapatid! Misaup-saup la ding PENELCO, PELCO I and II, PRESCO, TARELCO I and II, ZAMECO I and II king programang aini dakal a salamat. Maraming salamat sa inyo.

At keti naman king Pampanga kapamilatan na ning PELCO II, dakal lang negosyu ing karelang asopan kalupa na ning Mekeni Food Products, Jomafer Supermarket, Makro at ding gagawang muebles king Betis at Guagua. Ngeni sisiguradwan na ning PELCO II na ding atlu pang mitatagan a barangay midinan na la mu namang serbisyu king malaguang panaun.

Samantala y sundu ning gubyerno ing pamamye serbisyo kareng tau lalu na king kasalukuyang problemang pang-ekonomiya king mabilug a yatu. Ding kalulu dinan tang prioridad. This means that electric coops must work hard to bring light and power to the farthest barangays where the most marginalized of our people reside.

Growth is achieved only through hard work and perseverance. And so today, on the first anniversary of PELCO’s rehabilitation, on the 8 millionth household electrification, I once again enjoin the electric cooperatives continue to be a vital contributor to the steady economic growth that we enjoyed in the past eight years where I’m sure this barangay electrification helped us enjoy an unprecedented 33 quarters of uninterrupted growth. And with your help, let us carry the steady growth forward into the future.

Salamat Father Paking, salamat Edith, salamat sa NEA, salamat sa Electric Cooperatives for helping me put flesh to my father’s dream of development for our people — a dream that the rest of our countrymen share as well.

Once again, congratulations! Mayap a oras pasibayu king PELCO. Dakal a salamat kang Ging y Dennis, ampo ding aliwang nga opisyales keti king Pampanga ding Congressman tamu.

Congratulations and Mabuhay sa ating lahat! Thank you.
