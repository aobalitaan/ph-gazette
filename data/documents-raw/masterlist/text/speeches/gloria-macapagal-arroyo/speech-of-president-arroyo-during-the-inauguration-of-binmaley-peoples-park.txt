Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the inauguration of Binmaley People’s Park on the occasion of the Town’s 415th Foundation Anniversary and Fiesta in Honor of Our Lady of Purification

[Delivered at the Binmaley People’s Park, Pangasinan, February 2, 2005]

Mrs. Gina de Venecia, the First Lady of the House of Representatives; Governor Agbayani Tan amin ya opisyales diad Pangasinan; Congressman Espino; Mayor Rosario; lahat ng aking mga Cabinet members; Vice Governor Lambino; Vice Mayor Jettie Delos Angeles, Amin nga opisyales diad Binmaley; the different Pangasinan mayors who are here today san saray kabaleyan diad Binmaley; masantos a kabuwasan, Congressman Tulagan of the next district, please join us here congressman; masantos ya fiesta tan salamat ed sikayon amin ed tulong yo ed siyak ed eleksyon nen 2004.

Masasanting so insabi na fiesta ed Binmaley, amay-amay so nagawaan natan ya blessings amin. Unaan la dia so balita ed ekonomiya natan. Lumalakas ang piso. Millions of dollars are coming in. ‘Yung Supreme Court sabi puwede tayo mag-mining and all of these other things are helping our economy. ‘Yung calpers, nagdesisyon na itutuloy ang investments sa Pilipinas. ‘Yung Supreme Court, final na ang kanyang desisyon. All of these should seal confidence in the economy.

The average Filipino will soon reap the fruits of confidence in the economy, which will not only bring in more investments and jobs but drive the overall agenda of urgent reforms. These positive developments don’t happen by accident. They are the result of hard nosed assessment by the objective analysis of the great potentials of our country and excellence and hard work of our people. These assessments are not limited to the economy but extend to the gains we’ve made in unifying our political institutions to put our fiscal house in order: in fighting corruption, terrorism and crime; in waging peace; and in delivering our pro-poor agenda.

Philippine stability is dawning upon the whole range of national affairs and cutting across the regions and sectors. We must stay the course, seize the opportunity for reform, and close ranks behind a higher future.

And I can’t think of no better place to make this call than among the wonderful, friendly and supportive people of Binmaley, Pangasinan.

Balbaleg ya salamat ed sikayon amin.
