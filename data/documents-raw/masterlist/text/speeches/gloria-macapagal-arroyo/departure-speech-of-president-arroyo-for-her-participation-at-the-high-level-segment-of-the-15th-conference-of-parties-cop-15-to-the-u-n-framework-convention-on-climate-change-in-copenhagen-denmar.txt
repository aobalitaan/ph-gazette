Departure speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines For her participation at the High Level Segment of the 15th Conference of Parties (COP-15) to the U.N. Framework Convention on Climate Change in Copenhagen, Denmark

[Delivered at Terminal 2, NAIA, Pasay City, December 16, 2009]

Sinasagot ng ating bansa ang hamon ng bagyong pandaigdigang ekonomiya, ngunit may higit na mabagsik na bagyo ang humaharap sa atin: the devastation of Mother Nature accelerated by the mistreatment of the fragile environment by human beings all over the world.

The long-term security and prosperity of our nation and, indeed, the world, relies on coming to grips with the man made devastation that is warming our planet. Everyday we delay is a day the glaciers melt, the waters rise, food supplies are threatened and the poor suffer. That is why the Global Warming Summit in Copenhagen is vital for our nation, for the future of our children and for the fate of the world. Let us pray that progress will be made at the Summit.

Ilang buwang lamang nakaraan ang mga pag-asa para sa Copenhagen ay lumalabo. But today, developed and developing nations alike seem closer to assuming their burden and doing something positive about solving one of the great issues of our time.

In recent weeks, we have been meeting with our Global Warming Task Force in preparation for the talks in Copenhagen. We want to make inroads in Denmark based on science that shows that our nation is being battered by global warming even if we ourselves have a very good carbon footprint. In fact, with my meeting with the multilaterals the other day, the World Bank country representative pointed out that, in fact, we are where we want the rest of the world to be. But our carbon footprint is already there. We are doing our part for the environment. And yet, while we are not a climate maker — because we are not a source of climate change — we are a climate taker.

The typhoons are becoming more horrific; the rains more violent; the seas more savage. Global warming is affecting our agriculture, our fishing stocks, our forests and our cities. Kailangang magtrabaho tayo kasama ng buong sangkatauhan para makipagbalikat, gumawa ng mabibigat na desisyon, at palitan ang direksyon upang sagipin ang Inang Kalikasan at sa ganoon, sagipin ang sanlibutan.

Pupunta tayo sa Copenhagen na may patunay kung paano ang pagpapalit ng klima ng mundo ay negatibong inaapektohan ang Pilipinas. Gaya nang sinabi nating maraming beses na, the greatest human tragedy that will arise from climate change will be the migration that will be forced by sinking coastlines and in some cases, such as Tuvalu and the Maldives, sinking countries. As more and more sites are rendered inhospitable to human life and economic activity, it is estimated that as many as one billion people may be affected globally. Kalahati sa kanila ay manggagaling sa Asya. Habang nagkakalat ang mga pamilya sa mga matataas na lugar sa iba’t-ibang bahagi ng mundo, climate change will spawn global change. Ito ay isang bagay na kailangan sama-samang tugonan ng mga bansa ng mundo. Bilang bansang may higit pitong libong pulo, ito ay bagay na malaking abala sa Pilipinas. Kailangan linawin din natin ito sa mundo.

Panahon na para ang mga bansa ng mga mundo ay angkinin ang ating mga tungkulin gaya nang sabi ko ang Pilipinas inaangkin na natin ito kaya nga maganda ang ating carbon footprint. Ang ating trabaho ay ipasulong at ipagtanggol ang interes ng Pilipinas. Ang paglutas sa problemang ito ay kakailanganin ang maraming taon, ngunit ibig nating tiyakin na simulan natin ang proseso ngayon, upang sa ganoon ang mga sumusunod ay maaaring ipagpatuloy na tulungan ang ating bansa ngayon at sa darating na mga taon.

Ito ay lalong importante dahil sa panahon ng paghihirap ng pangkabuhayan, the pressures are greatest to abandon the obligation to the environment or to loosen the rules and put economic objectives above environmental interests. This is precisely why at this point in time we must call on the world community to be even more vigilant in advancing efforts to tackle the global challenge of climate change.

Para sa Pilipinas, we will not go to Copenhagen looking for a handout. We will go to join hands with other nations to band together to solve this problem imposed by man. Lagi nating sinasabi na para sa makatarungang resulta, it is essential for developed countries to lead in reducing emissions, in accordance with the principle of common but differentiated responsibilities. A financial mechanism must also be established to meet the costs of adaptation for developing countries and for an effective development and transfer of technology.

Ngunit habang ang mas mayayamang bansa ay may higit na responsibilidad at pasanin, iyan ay hindi dahilan para ang mga umuunlad pang bansa ay iwasan ang kanilang responsibilidad. Kailangan gawin nating lahat ang ating bahagi at yan ang ating pakay para sa ating summit sa Copenhagen.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
