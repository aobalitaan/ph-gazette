Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the International Earth Day Celebration 2007

[Delivered at the Bulwagang Rizal, Malacañang, April 23, 2007]

Happy Earth Day!

Kahapon ang Earth Day pero ngayon ang working day, kaya natin ipinagdiriwang ang Earth Day dito sa ating pamahalaan. Itong Earth Day na ito, ano ang kanyang konteksto? Ang konteksto ng ating Earth Day ay iyong ating pagsisikap na walang tigil upang magtayo ng modernong bansa na lalahok balang araw sa hanay ng mga bansang mayaman at maunlad. Iyon bang first world in 20 years. Matipuno ang aking pag-asa sa hinaharap ng sambayanan at sa pag-angat ng mamamayan. Our national agenda can transform our economy and lift up the poor.

Ngunit, dapat nating gamitin ang bawat kasangkapang hawak natin; edukasyon, teknolohiya, pautang sa maralita o microfinance upang makaahon ang ating mga kababayang mahihirap at matamo natin ang pag-unlad ng para sa lahat at para sa kalikasan.

Kaya ako ay nasisiyahan dahil narito si Secretary Ding Panganiban, namumuno ng NAPC, narito si Ed Pamintuan ang ating kampeon ng Luzon Urban Beltway, nandito ba ang Flying V? Yes, Flying V. Nandito ang Rotary District 3800, yes, di ba? Nandito kayo pati iyong Rotary Club ng Cosmopolitan San Juan, nandito rin iyong Philippine Confederation of Grains Association. Gusto kong magpasalamat sa inyo sa inyong kasunduang magtutulungan upang maibsan ang kahirapan sa pamamagitan ng pagkakaloob ng feeding and skills and values training sa mahihirap na benepisyaryo sa ilang prayoridad na lugar sa Luzon Urban Beltway, maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.

They are part of the economic engine to drive us to first world status in 20 years. But even as they do so, even as we do so, we must not do so at the back of the poor, nor at the expense of the environment.

No nation can aspire to become modern without thinking of our environment. Napakahalagang tutukan natin ang kaunlarang makakalikasan. Kaya bawat April 22 ng bawat taon, we have marked this every year as Earth Day in the Philippines.

Mayroon tayong isa pang Cabinet member na napakaimportante sa ating programa para sa kalikasan. Bukod kay Secretary Reyes, kay Secretary Ding Panganiban at saka kay Secretary Pamintuan, malamang sa susunod na taon kasama na natin ang Department of Education dahil itong araw na ito, I am directing the Department of Education to incorporate subjects dealing with global warming and climate change into the public school Science curriculum subjects. We want our young to be aware of global warming and other climate change phenomena.

Because we want to press ahead up to their generation with a massive Green Philippines Environmental Plan, that is comprehensive in scope and thorough in execution. Well, our officials from the DENR are here, I would just like to make it clear to them and to all what is this Green Philippines Plan, although I’ve been saying it over and over again it’s time to say it again this time. This plan includes; first, reforestation; second, preservation of our reefs and waterways; third, scrubbing the land and air of pollutants that dangers the health of our citizens and fourth, energy independence.

On reforestation, we must take back our forest and replenish fallen trees with new protected forest to protect our land and our people. We must reforest that land and bring back the beauty of the Philippines, we all loved and want.

Itong darating na Mayo, ang inyong lingkod ay pupunta sa New Zealand. Kung naalaala ninyo many years ago, ang New Zealand ay tumutulong sa ating forestation sa Bukidnon. Naalaala ko noong ako ay bagong Senador noong 1992 nagtanim ako ng puno doon. Noong bumalik ako bilang Presidente ang laki-laki na ng puno. And this is New Zealand’s continuing support for the Philippines, as to have expanded it to eco-tourism.

Kaya noon pumunta si Prime Minister Helen Clark dito sa Pilipinas ang kanyang attending secretary si Secretary Reyes, dib a Angie? Dahil kalikasan ang aming pinag-usapan at bumisita siya doon sa kanilang proyekto ng New Zealand doon sa Bohol, iyong mga dolphins and whales sa Pamalican Island.

Ngayon, sa pagpunta ng inyong lingkod sa New Zealand, isa sa mga tulong na ihahandog ng New Zealand kaya pupunta rin si Leonard Gibb at Narda Camacho doon ay tulong muli sa ating reforestation’s program, number one program ng ating Green Philippines.

On the second program, item mode of program, first of the environmental plan, preservation of our reefs and waterways and designation of national parks and wildlife refuges. I want to turn our coral reefs and beaches after all the other wonder of the world. I want to turn them into national parks and international sanctuaries for eco-tourism and scientific research. And so as part of this, today, I thank the DENR, Angie and his officials for working very quickly so that we can have this proclamation signed today. What is this proclamation? It is a proclamation establishing a critical habitat and eco-tourism area within the coastal lagoon of Las Piñas and Parañaque and ensuring the maintenance of necessary ecological services from peripheral areas. Ito ang ating proclamation bilang pagdiriwang sa araw ng Earth Day at pipirmahan ko ngayon.

Iyong pangatlo, iyong anti-pollution. We want to tap the enthusiasm of young ang old alike, to clean up our environment. We need to rid our barangays and cities of trash. We need to build up more urban parks, and recreation areas, we need to purify our water and we need to clean up industrial sites that is an eye sore and are unhealthy. Unilever, thank you very much.

Kaya itong araw na ito ako ay nagagalak dahil kasama ng ating pagdinwang iyong ilang mga barangay na namumuno sa pangangalaga ng kapaligiran at kalikasan sa kani-kanilang lugar. Saan dito iyong mga outstanding barangays sa ating paglinis ng kalikasan? Nandito ba sila, Narda? Okay.

Narito rin iyong ilang negosyante, iyong mga nag-oath taking kanina. Kabilang na iyong mga junk shop owners, nasaan iyong mga junk shop owners natin? Na ginagawa ang lahat para matiyak na ang kanilang negosyo ay nakakatulong sa kapaligiran at kalusugan ng mamamayan.

At kasama rin natin ang green army ng DENR, Angie? At iyong ating mga eco-aide, na matamang nagbabantay laban sa mga umaabuso ng ating kapaligiran at kalikasan.

And finally, energy independence, ito naman ay trabaho ng Secretary of Energy. The role of innovation in this context is vital along with political will. We need to develop a larger manufacturing base along with our booming service economy to create jobs. Pero kasabay nito, kailangan tayong mas umasa sa sarili at maging matipid sa paggamit ng enerhiya. There is no reason we cannot do so through, we cannot do both manufacturing and energy independence. There is no reason we cannot do both through innovation, proper quality planning and united political and civic leadership such as what we have in today’s hall.

Halimbawa, naglunsad tayo ng programa sa mga bio-fuel, iyong punong batas ni Congressman Migz Zubiri, para maging Senador. Bio-fuels gaya ng ethanol at coco-biodiesel sa gawa ng pananim. Our bio-fuels program will rely on investment and innovation to create jobs, skills and self-reliance while keeping our environment clean. Dito hindi lang Secretary of Energy ang kailangan, pati Secretary of Agriculture because of the raw materials. Sa pagsisikap nating ito huwaran natin ang bansang Brazil na siyang nanguna sa bio-fuel, because we must continue to learn around the world and in our region.

May pagkakataon tayong matuto sa karanasan ng ibang bansa at magbukas ng mga bagong landas tungo sa kaunlarang maka-kalikasan.

We must think outside the box. Gagamitin natin ang teknolohiya at innovation upang wakasan ang taung-taong pagdepende sa dayuhang langis at paglabag sa kalikasan.

Nagmula ang ating kabuhayan sa Inang Kalikasan. Kaya sa inyong lahat na nag-aalaga sa kalikasan maraming salamat sa pangangalaga ninyo at paglilingkod bilang huwaran ng paglingap sa ating kalikasan at kapaligiran.

Mabuhay ang lahat ng nagmamahal at nag-aalaga sa kalikasan!

Happy Earth Day muli sa inyong lahat!
