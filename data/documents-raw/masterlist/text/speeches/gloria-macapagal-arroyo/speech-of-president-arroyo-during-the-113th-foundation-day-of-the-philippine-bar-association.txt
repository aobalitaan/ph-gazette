Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the 113th Foundation Day of the Philippine Bar Association

[Delivered on April 16, 2004]

I would like to greet the members of the Board, the past presidents, the different VIPs, the presiding justice of the Court of Appeals, the Associate Justices of the Sandiganbayan, the members of the Judicial and Bar Council, most of whom, if not all, were my appointee.

Well, I am glad to see all of you here tonight. An I am glad to see the faces of may of the Philippine Bar Association, leaders and members, so that, in my next term with your support, I know whom to appoint in the Supreme Court. Excellencies of the Diplomatic Corps — I should have mentioned also the RTC judges — the recipients of the award, distinguished guests, ladies and gentlemen, a few days ago, I unveiled my pro-poor agenda to the next six years to end poverty and unite the nation.

In the nest six years, I pledge to triple loans to self-employed and small business owners. I pledge to build 3,000 new schools, grant college scholarships to every qualified poor family, and give a computer in every school. I pledge to (COVERAGE CUT) — rogue and abused in our government.

I further pledge to continue to fight for self-sufficiency in rice production. To fight for new roads, bridges and highways to link our great nation. And to fight for more property rights and more legal rights for our people.

Legal rights. Ang katarungan ay ang bukal ng isang demokrasya. Justice is a bedrock of democarcy. Without justice, there can be no domocracy. Ang isang lipinang kulang sa hustisya ay isang diktatorya, isang sistema ng pasismo.

In my short presidency, with the support of may of you — kanina nga nabanggit ang halos lahat ng aking presidential appointees, pero hindi pa nabanggit si Nonong Cruz at si Joey Peñafrancia, is Attorney Acosta, and I remember to mention them because they are among those who have been helping me in my administration’s making a strong part in judicial reform. Thank you, Ding, the filing of cases against corrupt judges and prosecutors, but more in general and more positively, the adjustment of the judges and prosecutors’ pay. The package of institutional strenthening of the judiciary funded by the World Bank and the Mediation Law.

Sa susunod kong administrasyon, sa tulong ninyo, we will build on this strong start. Kasama ng Korte Suprema, lilinisin pa natin ang ating hudikatura, ang ating mga korte at ang mga taong nagsisilbing anay sa loob at labas ng hukuman. Gagamitin ko ang aking impluwensiya upang ang ating hudikatura ay may pantay-pantay na paglapat ng hustisya sa lahat ng Pilipino, mahirap o mayaman. Hahabulin natin at ipapakulong ang mga bumabaluktot sa batas sa loob at labas ng hukuman. Lilipulin natin iyong mga sinabi ng ating mga kasapi ng diplomatic corps who I am greeting tonight, iyong tinatawag nga nilang mga hoodlums in robes, na sila ang nagiging sagabal sa investments na pumunta rito.

At kayo namang magagaling na abogado ng Philippine Bar Association, hihimukin ko ang mga abogadong nagmamalasakit sa bayan, kagaya ninyo, na palawigin pa ninyo ang inyong pro-bono service para sa kapakanan ng mga dukha at walang kapangyarihan sa buhay.

If my complete term, as this one — it is only half a term — I also intend to share political power as broadly as possible. I will reach out to all parties, to all groups, to all communities to identify their best, brightest and most honest to serve in our government. Sa ilalim ng susunod kong daminstrasyon, magkakaroon nang tunay na national unity government.

Aanyayahan ko ang oposisyon, ang kontra partido, ang naging kalaban na kalimutan na ang pulitika at ipadala ang kanilang pinakamagaling na kinatawan sa ating pamahalaan kung saan ay may naghihintay na mataas na tanggapan sa kanila.

Ang aking pamahalaan ay magiging isang tunay na salamin. Kapag itinaas ninyo ang salamin na ito at makikita ninyo ang larawan ng bansa.

Ang aking pamahalaan ay isang tunay na pamilya ng lahi. Kasama rito ang mga kinatwan ng kabataan, kababaihan, cultural communities, senior citizens, partido ng oposisyon.

It is time we detoxify our political system. Let us rid politics of its pernicious poison.

It is time we employ the blessing of civility to heal a wounded society. Civility is the glue that will bind Filipinos together.

Tulungan ninyo akong magkaroon tayo ng bagong pagkakaisa. Mabubuhay muli ang puwersa, ang disiplina sa batas kung mangingibabaw ang pambansang interes sa sariling kapakanan.

Members of this oldest lawyer’s association, you are the cream of the crop of our legal profession. Kung mayroon mang makakatulong sa akin sa mga hamon ng reporma at rekonsilasyon sa darating na panahon, kayo na nga iyon. Tulungan ninyo akol

Maraming salamat.
