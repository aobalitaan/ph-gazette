Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the 46th National Council Meeting of the Boy Scouts of the Philippines

[Delivered at the Dusit Hotel Nikko, Manila Hotel, May 16, 2002]

Maraming salamat Secretary Raul Roco sa iyong pag-imbita at sa iyong pagpahayag ng patuloy na suporta ng Department of Education sa Boy Scouts of the Philippines. Dahil alam natin na nagkaproblema yung boy scouts nung ipinatupad ni Raul ‘yung batas na dapat walang collection. Kaya kanina pinag-usapan namin dito sa mesa at siguro ‘yung nawala sa boy scouts dahil sa pagtigil ng collection during enrollment ay pwedeng manggaling sa special education fund. Iyon ang ating gagawin para hindi malugi yung boy scouts.

Mayor Jojo Binay, ang National President ng Boy Scouts of the Philippines. At pinaalala n’ya yung utang ko bilang pangulo, na one million pesos para sa Boy Scouts of the Philippines, pandagdag na rin ‘yon; Congressman Butch aquino; Congressman Teddy ‘Boy’ Locsin; Vice Mayor Mercado, ang Conference Chairman nitong ating meeting ngayon; Mr. Kim Kyu Young, Cam Saha Meda, and we thank you and we congratulate you for being here. And congratulations as you end your tour as Regional Director of the World Scout Bureau; si Jun Aniag, ang Senior VP ng Boy Scouts of the Philippines, former Congressman, binabati ko rin; si Vice Governor De Jesus ng Misamis Oriental, Chief National Commissioner ng Boy Scouts; si Dr. Pedro San Vicente, ang member Emeritus ng Boy Scouts; si Frankie Roman na member Emeritus din ng boy scouts — saan si Frankie? Ayun; si Secretary General Escudero, ang ating Master of Ceremonies at saka si Secretary General. Monsignor Laban, member ng Executive Board ng Boy Scouts pero siya ang Malacanang Chaplain kaya ‘pag nagsi-simba ako araw-araw, siya ang nagse-sermon sa akin; Obet Pagdanganan, BSP past National President and now, kasi tapos na ‘yung one year, Presidential Adviser on Cooperative Development; Congressman Del de Guzman; and si Dr. Destura, ang Vice President for the Visayas; at ‘yung aking kababayan sa Iligan, si Jimmy Semana, ang Vice President for Mindanao.

Binabati ko rin yung mga members ng executive board, officers and members of the Boy Scouts of the Philippines. May I also greet Mr. Nazmul Haque Nazu from Bangladesh; Commander Aftab Qureshi from Pakistan; nandito rin si Congressman Cho Roco at saka si Congressman Harry Angpin; si Governor Petilla ng Leyte na ininduct ko bilang Boy Scout din doon sa Leyte; ay, mabuti nadaanan ko si Governor Aurora Cerilles dahil wala sa listahan, Governor Cerilles; at iyung inyong counterpart naman sa kababaihan, si Cynthia Sagala, President ng Girl Scouts — naku, nalaman na niyang narito ako mapipilitan akong pumunta ngayon sa Girl Scouts Conference din; other Government Officials; distinguished guests; ladies and gentlemen:

Greetings, my fellow scouters. I come to this council with best wishes for your safety, security and happiness.

Ako ay natutuwa na, bilang inyong pangulo, ako ngayon ang Chief Scout ng Pilipinas. Tunay na ito ay isang napakalaking karangalan, dahil alam naman nating lahat na kayo ay isang samahan ng mga huwarang kabataang kilala sa gawa at hindi puro salita. Hindi ba ang motto natin sa boy scouts, sa mga scouts ay “laging handa”, always ready. Laging handa sa pagtulong sa ating clean-up drives, sa traffic assistance — hindi ba ‘yung image ng boy scout always helping an old lady cross the street? — sa anti-drug campaign, sa community service.

Mataas ang aking paggalang sa scouting. Ang aking mga anak naging boy scouts din noong panahon nila. At mula nung matatag ang Boy Scout of the Philippines noong 1936, milyun-milyon na ang kabataang pilipinong nakinabang dito at nakaiwas sa masasamang bisyo.

In fact, I am very happy to learn that the boy scouts is number three in the world. But that is in terms of absolute numbers. Sabi nga ni Jojo, if you talk about percentage of the population being boy scouts, lalo pang mas mataas — siguro baka pinakamataas na nga — at ang isang dahilan nga ay dahil dito kaiba-iba ang kostumbre ng boy scouts, naka-integrate sa mga gawain ng mga eskwelahan. And so it is truly important that we continue this tradition because it leads our youth into the good path away from the bad path.

The practice of self-reliance, the attitude of taking the initiative to achieve goals and the skills of emergency preparedness have inculcated in countless young Filipinos the values of discipline, diligence and perseverance. Through the path of scouting, many respectable and responsible Filipinos of today have acquired the sturdy character and firm conviction to selflessly serve god, country, and fellowmen. It is largely because of scouting that many of them, many of you have become good citizens and outstanding leaders.

At ‘yon na nga, sana ay, tayo ay, dahil marami sa mga leaders dito sa boy scouts ay leaders din ng pulitika sa iba’t-ibang mga lugar sa ating bansa, sana nga ‘yung mga political leaders na leaders din ng boy scouts ay manguna sa asal na huwag na muna tayo masyadong mag-alitan sa pulitika, gawin muna ang trabaho para sa ating pamayanan.

At dahil nga sa aking mataas na paggalang sa scouting, noong 1997, noong ako ay senador pa, tumulong ako sa pagpapatayo sa Nueva Ecija ng isang training center dahil hiningi ni Jojo. Ngayon ay ginagamit na ring pook-sanayan ng mga boy scouts. Meron din akong ilang na napondohan na Girl Scouts Centers — alam ni Mrs. Sagala ‘yan — noong ako ay senador. Kaya sabi ni Jojo, pero iyung contribution bilang pangulo, iba naman ‘yon. So, “okay, Jojo,” kaya sabi ko. Kaya si Jojo ‘pag nag-uutos, mahirap tanggihan.

Tulad ng aking nasabi kanina, ang boy scouts ay isang samahan ng mga huwarang kabataan. Kilala sa gawa at hindi puro salita. Scouts are not faultfinders, empty critics or complainers. Kaya ‘yan ang hinihingi ko sa mga politicians na scouts. Scouts have an active concern for and a determination to resolve problems affecting the community.

Scouting in the Philippines is also a symbol of hope, because it springs from the youth. From this hope flows the strength to confront and overcome challenges, the tenacity of spirit to reach out for the best, the noblest, the most sublime of human aspirations.

But hope alone, without substance, cannot meaningfully alter or improve the human condition. Hope must be transformed into decisive action and resolute initiative. This is true in confronting two great challenges today — terrorism and poverty.

Ang laban kontra sa kahirapan at laban kontra terorismo ay hindi nagkakalayo, magkambal nga sila. Poverty and the sense of powerlessness that it brings are the spawning grounds where terrorism can easily spread its evil ideology.

Kaya nga ang ating laban sa kahirapan ay binubuo sa apat na bahagi:

Una, sa pagpapairal ng sistema ng ekonomiya ng bagong siglo, kinakailangang magkaroon ng magandang macroeconomic environment para magkaroon ng trabaho ang bawa’t mamamayang Pilipino.

Pangalawa, dahil karamihan sa ating mga mahihirap ay nasa kanayunan, kinakailangan ang agricultural modernization founded on social equity.

Pangatlo, ang mga programang direktang nakakatulong sa mahihirap tulad ng pabahay at Medicare.

Pang-apat, ang pag-angat ng moralidad sa ating pamahalaan at sa ating lipunan.

Ano ang ibig sabihin ng mga ito sa ating kabataan? Edukasyon. Dapat nating bigyan ng kaalaman at kakayahan ‘yung mga kabataan upang sa paglaki ang mga kabataan ay maging responsableng mamamayan na kayang bumuhay sa mga sarili.

Our national hero, Dr. Jose Rizal, once said that an uneducated people cannot be a free people. We have seen places in the world where innocent children are tutored in hatred and utter disregard for human values. They are programmed like robots along a single path of behavior, to obey authority without question. And worse sometimes, the authority is an authority that foments hatred.

We are all witness to how an uneducated citizenry can be controlled by extreme ideologies, by contrived fears, by irrational causes. They become vulnerable to dictators, to rabble-rousers, to unscrupulous leaders.

The education and enlightenment we need to propagate is the responsibility of all and everyone in our society.

This annual meeting of the national council sets the direction and the pace of the BSP. The BSP is the largest youth organization in the country. The deliberations and decisions made in this council have far-reaching effects on the Filipino youth.

As your chief scout, allow me to pose three questions in guiding your deliberations:

First, are we doing the right thing? Second, are we doing enough of the right thing? Third, are we doing enough of the right thing with the required urgency?

The country and each of your communities wait upon your leadership and participation to make life better for our people.

The world we are living in today needs more peace, but it is filled with strife. The global community needs more cooperation, but it is surrounded by conflict. Humanity needs more solidarity, but we hear the voices of hate and belligerence all around.

There is an urgent need for our children to be in the company of good, decent and sincere people. Without doubt, they should be in the company of the boy scouts.

Ang scouting ay simbolo ng pagkakaisa. Pinagsasama nito ang mga muslim at kristiyano, mga tagalog, mga bisaya, mga chinese, mga kastila, mga mayaman, mga mahihirap, mga pinuno, mga tagasunod. Sa boy scouts, palagay ko, lahat ay pantay-pantay.

Indeed, let scouting be a shining example of what a common vision plus strong unity can achieve. Let scouting pave the way towards team spirit, self-reliance and the excellence of youth.

Sa ating hangaring masugpo ang kahirapan at mabalik ang dangal ng ating bansa ngayong siglo beinte-uno, kailangan natin ang tibay at dignidad ng mga boy scouts.

Kailangan natin ang lakas ng loob ng mga boy scouts. Higit sa lahat, kailangan nating ipamalas ang magagandang bagay na natututunan sa scouting.

Ako ay umaasa na hindi ako bibiguin ng ating mga kasamahang scouts. Lubos ang aking tiwala sa inyong kakayahan na tulungan ang ating bansa na masugpo ang kahirapan sa dekadang kasalukuyan.

At dahil nga ang national president ng boy scout ay mayor din ng pinaka-mayamang lungsod sa ating bansa, iyung boys world’s scout headquarters ay sana nga, sa sipag ni Jojo Binay, ay matayo dito sa Makati City.

Maraming salamat at mabuhay ang scouting sa Pilipinas.
