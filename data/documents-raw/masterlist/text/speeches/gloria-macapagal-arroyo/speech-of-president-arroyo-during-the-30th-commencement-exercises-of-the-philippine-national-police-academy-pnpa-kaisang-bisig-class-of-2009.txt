Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the 30th Commencement Exercises of the Philippine National Police Academy (PNPA) “Kaisang-Bisig” Class of 2009

[Delivered at the PNPA Campus Grandstand, Camp Castañeda, Silang, Cavite, March 26, 2009]

Thank you very much Secretary Puno for your introduction.

Tikas pahinga.

Vice President de Castro, members of the Diplomatic Corps, Dr. Cojuangco, Police Chief Superintendent Abarzosa, PNP Chief General Versoza, Head of our Bureau of Jail Mangement and Penology Dian, Acting Chief of the Bureau of Fire Protection Bandilla, Mayor Tolentino and other local government officials, other national government officials, PNP ’Kaisang-Bisig’ Class of 2009:

Congratulations! Lalo na kay Cadet Roan Marie Bascugin, class valedictorian, congratulations! (Applause)

Pinagmamalaki kong kababaihan ang ating valedictorian itong taong ito. (applause)

Sa mga magulang, kaanak at kaibigan ng mga nagsisipagtapos, congratulations din! (Applause) Dahil sa inyong pagsisikap, nakapagtapos sa Akademya ang inyong anak, kapatid, kaibigan. At special na congratulations sa ama ni Cadet Roan Marie Bascugin, si Police Superintendent Bascugin. (applause) Talagang 30th Commencement Exercises na ito dahil ang ama ni Marie Basgucin ay Class ’84 ng PNPA. (Applause) Kaya second generation na itong ating mga nagtatapos ngayon.

Ang ‘Kaisang-Bisig’ ang napili ninyong pangalan ay nagbibigay ng pagkakaisang kailangan para sa kapayapaan at kaunlarang makakamit lamang sa pagtutulungan ng ating kapulisan, mga bumbero, at mga jail officers.

And together with Secretary Puno who inducted you, who received you into the service of the Department of Interior and Local Government, I welcome you, the country’s newest police, fire and jail inspectors, to permanent government service. (Applause)

Yung ginamit ni Secretary Punong salitang ‘permanent’ ay napakamakabuluhan sa mga panahong ito sa buong mundo. At mabuti naman at sa ngayon may mga bakante pa ring mga naghihintay sa mga kwalipikadong aplikante sa sektor ng kapulisan, ng military at ng edukasyon.

We must continue to focus here at home on creating jobs as well as keeping prices low. Salamat na lamang na nakaya ng taong-bayan ang mga presyo ng bilihin ng pagkain kaya malaking binaba ang hunger rate ng ating bansa itong Enero. (Applause) But the government must provide incentives to help the private sector to keep and create jobs. The government must also continue to come to the rescue of the poorest among us with targeted subsidies of food, fuel and rice.

How do we fund all these? The answer is: “Cut government waste.”

In challenging economic times, the government must tighten its belt and live within its means, just like the average Filipino household. To that end, the government is taking a series of actions to stop unnecessary and wasteful spending. The taxpayers deserve no less from their government and public servants. Kaya ipinag-utos ng inyong lingkod ang belt-tightening measures sa lahat ng ahensiya ng pamahalaan, kasama na rito ang DILG, PNP, BJMP, BFP at PNPA. Mabuti na lang at noon pa natin itinaas ang sweldo ng mga pulis. Sa loob ng nakaraang walong taon, limang beses itinaas ang sweldo o allowance ng ating mga pulis. (Applause)

Ngayong dapat magtipid, nilagdaan ko ang Executive Order upang bawasan ang mga ahensiya ng pamahalaan ang Maintenance, Overhead and Operating budgets ng 1.5 percent. Aabot ng 7 billion pesos ang salaping matitipid.

I have directed electricity and fuel saving measures. Our targets: Bring down consumption by 25 percent of fossil fuels; reduce electric consumption by at least 10 percent; turn off airconditioners at 4:30 except those with 24-hour work.

Ipinag-utos ko rin na gumamit ang lahat ng ahensiya ng fluorescent bulbs imbes na incandescent upang makapagtipid tayo sa inaangkat na kuryente; and convert 20 percent of our government vehicles in major cities to non-fossil fuels. At dapat umpisahin na nating gawin ito, Ronnie, sa ating mga police vehicles.

Government vehicles not on official trip, including police cars and fire trucks should be garaged in the agency office and the keys kept by the guards during weekends and holidays. Government vehicles used by officials below Cabinet rank may not have security plates unless there is imminent danger to the lives of the officials involved.

This coming Independence Day, we will forego the annual parade which costs 30 million pesos. Instead, we will hold a mega-jobs fair so that our 45,000 workers displaced by the global crisis can find employment and livelihood.

The government, including the DILG, PNP, BJMP and BFP and PNPA, must pour all savings and revenue into programs that help the poor and the middle class.

Savings from MOOE shall be allocated to emergency employment for the next six months. Secretary Puno has been designated as Emergency Employment Champion for the Province of Rizal, while Secretary Jun Ebdane of Public Works has been designated as Emergency Employment Officer for the Province of Cavite; and in both cases, in fact in all cases, with priority given to displaced workers in the export industry.

Helping the poor and the middle-class also includes more and more investments in education, healthcare and infrastructure. Kaya sa edukasyon kasama na doon ang PNPA. Thus, I directed the Secretary of Interior and Local Government to provide more slots for PNPA next school year. (Applause)

Itong klase ninyo — I think, 315 or 14 kayo nagsimula — at sabi ni President Cojuangco next year ay 380 ang pwedeng makapasok. Sabi ko sa kay Secretary Puno dagdagan yun at gawing 400 ang tatanggapin. (Applause)

Inatasan ko rin ang upgrade ng PNP hospital at mga clinics — meron ba kayong clinic dito? O, i-upgrade din natin ang clinic dito. Gawin nating primary hospital kung clinic-clinic pa lang siya (applause), para makapaglingkod na rin sa kapaligiran sa pamayanan. At hindi lang dito yun din ang dapat — sinabi ko na kay Ronnie noon — na gawin sa malalayo at mahihirap na lalawigan.

Working on infrastructure also means more road repair, where we can use OYSTER (Out-of-School Youth Serving Towards Economic Resiliency) at importante ito para sa inyo dahil ang recruitment officer ang dapat ang chief ng police station.

And all agencies must support programs for providing meals for our children in school and job and training opportunities for our young graduates.

Yung kanina nung nakaupo kami at sinabi ni Ronnie he’s accepting you in the permanent service, yun nga sabi ni Vice President Kabayan yan trabaho rin yan. Kaya mapalad kayong mga nagga-graduate dahil meron nang trabahong nagihihintay sa inyo. (Applause) Kaya bilang sukli, I am instructing all police camps, including the PNPA, to spend also part of your savings for providing breakfast to the day care, preschool and Grade 1 children in the school nearest your camp para sa kinabukasan ng susunod naman na henerasyon.

We must tackle many challenges at once to keep our economy and way of life going strong. On the part of government, we must live within our means and run a lean, efficient government. This includes continued fiscal prudence on the part of every agency including PNPA as our national government officials and economic managers undertake efforts to work towards a balanced budget. All the while, we must also simultaneously invest in key programs for our people, and certainly one of these key programs is public safety.

Natutuwa ako at tuloy-tuloy ang naging pagbaba ng ating ibat-ibang crime rates. Halimbawa, malaki ang naging pagbaba ng mga bank robberies at mga insidente ng kidnapping, hindi ba General Versoza? Tama yun. But there are still challenges we have to address. We must bring political killings to zero, including assassinations of government officials and media personalities.

This morning I talked with General Versoza and I said that Davao broadcaster Elgin Damasco should be offered police protection, and the texts threatening him should be investigated.

Kaya maraming nagawa ang inyong mga predecessors, mga Class 2009, at marami pa ring kailangang gawin ang inyong klase pagpasok niyo sa police, sa jail management at saka sa fire protection.

Our people have given you the rare opportunity to develop your potential. Go now and give back what you received by keeping the people safe. And as you keep the people safe, you will be able to carry out through your careers your theme today of, “Justice, Integrity and Service: Essential Values Towards a Strong and Globally Competitive Republic.”

Sa inyong lahat, congratulations! Good luck! And thank you. (Applause)
