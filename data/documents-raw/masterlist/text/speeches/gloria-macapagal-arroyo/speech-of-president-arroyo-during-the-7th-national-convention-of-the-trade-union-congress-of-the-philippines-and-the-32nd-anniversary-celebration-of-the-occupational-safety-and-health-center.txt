Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the 7th National Convention of the Trade Union Congress of the Philippines and the 32nd Anniversary Celebration of the Occupational Safety and Health Center

[Delivered in North Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City, December 14, 2007]

At gusto kong batiin si President Democrito Mendoza, hindi lamang siya’y Spiderman o Superman, pwede pa sa High School Musical.

Hindi ko na babatiin isa-isa ang mga narito kasi I have to conserve my voice. May pharyngitis ako and this is my last speaking engagement for the next three days, kinansel ko na lahat ang iba. Pero TUCP hindi ko pwedeng ikansel.

Binabati ko kayo sa inyong kumbensyon. Imagine 32 years old na kayo since 1975. Pinakamalaki sa Pilipinas at pinakamatagal and long lasting and long surviving. Congratulations! At nakakatuwang pakinggan yung talakay ninyo sa pagpapalawak ng mga benepisyo para sa ating manggagawa at kanilang pamilya. You want to go beyond the traditional services that a regular labor organization renders.

Nais kong bigyan ng matinding pansin ang paghahanap ng trabaho, ang pakikipag-ugnay sa mga trade unions sa ibang bansa para sa proteksyon ng ating manggagawa doon sa ibang bansa, pagtatayo ng training centers at pagbibigay ng scholarships sa mga anak ng unyonista.

Dito sa training center gaya nang sabi ni Manong Mocring kaagapay ninyo ang TESDA sa pagbibigay ng pagsasanay, at dahil dito lalo naging competitive ang mga manggagawa ng TUCP dito at sa ibang bansa. Bahagi ang TUCP sa apat na milyong Pilipinong naka-enroll sa ibat-ibang programa ng TESDA at mga accredited voc-tech schools.

Maraming programa ay dumadaan dito nga sa TUCP, congratulations! Nais ninyong bigyang pansin ang pagtatayo ng grocery at tindahan kung saan mas mura ang bilihin. Alam ko na ayun pa yun ang ginagawa ni Boy Herrera, kumukuha ng mga suppyly sa ibang mga trade union sa ibang bansa. Pero maganda rin na meron tayong paraan kung paano mag partner dahil nag tatayo ng mga Tindahan Natin kung saan nakabili ng murang bigas, noodles at pwede nating ipatayo and mga ito sa mga lugar ng bansa kung saan nakakaranas ng gutom. At ngayon na may halos na walong libong Tindahan Natin sa bansa, magbalikatan tayo, maglagay tayo ng TUCP Tindahan natin outlets sa mga tindahan ni Boy Herrera.

Nais din niyong bigyan pansin ang paglikha ng negosyo para tumaas ang pondo ng inyong samahan at pagtulong sa mga miyembro sa pagtatayo ng kani-kanilang negosyo.

We already see signs of the current benign, stable growth conditions sparking a wave of entrepreneurial activity in the Philippines. And it will not only help you raise money for your union, trade union, it will also help you become the vibrant middle-class we need, if we are to join the ranks of first-world in 20 years. In several key areas we have promoted a new entrepreneurial class. One is in microfinance, where the system has extended more than P80 billion loans to low income men and women who want to start their own business. In addition, through our Central Bank, we have created a series of investment incentives and programs for foreign remittance to be invested into capital enterprises.

Sa micro at SME sector, na yung dati kasama si Cerge Remonde na kasama ko na ngayon ay nakalikha tayo ng mahigit dalawang milyong trabaho mula 2004. Nakapagpautang ng P190 billion sa mahigit tatlong milyong micro at SME, at marami dito ay manggagawa.

Siguro Art Brion, abogado ka naman, pag-aralan mo kung papano ang pwede nating gawing microfinance conduit ang TUCP. Yan ay isang magandang negosyo.

Nais din niyong bigyan pansin ang pagtatayo ng murang pabahay, sabi ni Manong Mocring, at tulad ng gusto ninyo, ginagawa nating abot kaya ang mga programang pabahay para sa ating manggagawa. Ibinaba ang interest rate ng pag-ibig housing loans sa 6 percent na lamang, noong araw 9 percent. Ang interes naman para sa may 300,000 pesos and above ay bumaba na sa 9 percent mula 10 percent. At pinahaba rin ang repayment period hanggang tatlumpung taon. Magandang negosyo na ang TUCP pumasok sa housing for the workers.

Nais niyong bigyan pansin ang pagkakaloob ng mga tulong pang-kalusugan. Katugma nang inyong programa an gating Philhealth program. Over the next three years, we will invest in our country and our people to improve services like health insurance for ingredient families. Ngayon, 63 million na ang mga Pilipinong kasama sa programang ito. Hindi lang mahihirap, lahat ng Pilipino kasama sa programang ito. Hindi lang mahirap, lahat ng Pilipino. Out of the 85 million Filipinos 63 are already covered by health insurance. At doon lubhang mahihirap na kasapi ng TUCP pwede nating tulungan bayaran and first premium ng mga kasapi ninyo para makatulong at umaangat ang kanyang kalusugan.

Yung mga Botika ng Bayan at Botika ng Barangay, nakakabili ng gamot na kalahating presyo. Meron na tayong almost 10 000 Botika ng Barangay. Ang Botika ng Bayan naman ay may more than 1,000. Yung isa over the counter, yung isa kasama ang prescription. Umaasa tayong maipasa na ng kongreso ang batas ng cheap medicine para kahit na wala sa botika ng barangay ay pwedeng makabili ng kalahating-presyong gamot. Pero in the meantime, pwede rin magkaroon ng TUCP Botika ng Bayan. Magandang negosyo rin yun ng TUCP.

Over the next three years, we’ll also invest to improve our infrastructure. Kasi sabi niyo gusto niyong makahanap ng trabaho sa mga walang trabaho at kayo ang magiging trabaho niyo yung jobnet. Ang trabaho naman ng pamahalaan is to create an environment in which businesses feel confident to expand and employ more people because more jobs means less poverty.

A strong infrastructure network is the backbone of a modern nation. That’s why this year, we’re spending 50 percent more on infrastructure than in 2001 when our administration took over. And we’re able to do that because of the additional resources generated by our economic reforms. This is part of our 1.7 trillion-peso plan to invest in social and physical infrastructure which includes billions of pesos in education, health care and training along with billions in new bridges, roads and ports to upgrade the competitiveness of the Philippines. This will attract more investment and create more jobs gaya nang sabi ninyo. Mas maraming company ang maglilista sa Jobnet.

Our economy has reached a new level of maturity and stability with one of the strongest macroeconomic fundamentals in 20 years. In 2001, six years ago, no one thought we could get more revenues, cut down on our tax cheats, strengthen the peso, move the stock market. No one thought we could bring our consolidated public sector deficit budget into balance, or lower our debts and raise employment, but we have. Meron na tayo yatang sa ating consolidated national sector deficit ay 62 billion na yata ang ating surplus. At yung ating national government din ay bumababa dahil lumalakas ang piso.

Employment in April 2007 Tama ba ito , Art? – increased by 3.1 percent with around one million new employed workers. Employment was raised to 33.7 million from 32.7 million in April last year. So, we have kept up with our average of one million jobs a year, the highest in any administration.

Today, the Philippines is on a path to permanent economic growth and stability. Investments are pouring in and the peso is strong. I know this is a two-edged sword and we want to help our overseas workers cope with a strong peso. Our stock market has reached historic heights and we have created six million jobs in six years. And the world is taking notice, I believe because we have such productive workers and such rational and reasonable trade unions like TUCP, the Philippines offers one of the best values in Asia for investors. Marami doon ay dahil sa TUCP.

As I said earlier, we want to join the ranks of first-world nations in 20 years. As I said earlier, we’ll need a robust middle-class world class, world class infrastructure, strong institutions of government. Yet in the near term we must address the issues of hunger and poverty. We can t say we have moved forward if we don’t lift up the poor and the hungry among us. Let’s be measured by our ability to live our faith.

Patuloy tayong nakikibaka sa kagutuman. Noong isang taon, bumalangkas tayo ng plano para labanan ang kagutman. Nakasentro ang plano sa murang pagkain, tulong sa nagugutom pagtrabaho sa kalsada at kabukiran, gulayang pampamilya, microfinance, at dagdag sahod sa ilalim ng mga Regional Wage Boards.

At ako ay nasisiyahan at ipinagmamalaki ko na sa Regional Wage Boards halos lahat ng employees representatives ay galing sa TUCP. Nakabase ang hunger mitigation program sa pagkikilala na may gutom: una, dahil kulang ang pagkaing abot-kaya; at pangalawa, walang pambili sa pagkain. Sa ganun, kailangan paramihin ang murang pagkain – supply side; at palaguin ang kita at hanapbuhay – demand side.

Sa supply side, bahagi ng plano: palakasin ang produksiyon ng pagkain; pahusayin ang sistema ng paghatid sa pagkain. On the demand side: kailangan dagdagan ang kita ng tao; ituro ang wastong pagkain; palaganapin ang natural family planning.

Upang palakasin ang produksiyon ng pagkain, isinusulong natin ang mga programang manukan, babuyan, bakahan, pagsasaka, patubig, gulayan ng masa, at pagpapalago muli ng mga kakahuyan at palaisdaan sa dagat. Dito pwedeng pumasok ang TUCP, pwedeng tulungan ng pamahalaan.

To enhance efficiency of logistics and food delivery the program consists of: barangay food terminals. Pwede ring maglagay ang TUCP ng bagsakan ng pagkain aside from Tindahan Natin, kaya makakatulong kayo rito; roll-on/roll-off ferry routes. Meron na kayong mga pinapatakbong pantalan ba’t hindi kayo magpatakbo ng roll-on/roll-off ferry boat mismo? ; farm- to-market roads; efficient local transport in the provinces; the food-for-school program; an NGO network to support feeding programs; farmers option to buy back their produce. Ito ang paraan upang maging mas mabilis ang pagdala ngpagkain.

On the demand side of hunger mitigation, putting more money in people’s pockets, the program includes: more aggressive micro financing. Kaya kabalikat kayo ; aggressive training . Kabalikat din kayo.

To provide good nutrition, the program includes: social marketing; promoting exclusive breastfeeding; nutrition education in schools. And to promote natural family planning, responsible parenthood programs are being done, kasama ang birth spacing.

It’s really time, I’m glad that TUCP is reinventing itself, because it’s really time for the nation to focus on issues that are important to our future: creating more jobs, keeping prices down, providing affordable housing, providing access to education for our children.

Malaking tulong ang pagtatayo ninyo ng mga eskwelahan para maibsan ang ating pangangailangan para sa edukasyon . Education is a core value of Philippine society and life. The hardworking men and women of this nation put their family first. And the best gift any family can give to a child is access to a good education.

Education is the foundation of economic prosperity and individual liberty. That’s why we will continue to invest in new school construction at the elementary school level and to bolster our college scholarship program for schools ready for higher education. Pwede din tayong tumulong art sa pagbigay ng scholarship sa mga anak ng TUCP members. Ikaw na lang mag-aayos sa CHED.

Teachers are the backbone of our educational system. Without these selfless men and women our children would fall short of their dreams and aspiration. We’re indebted to their hard work and dedication. Their work is noble. They deserve the praise and respect of every Filipino.

Noong araw dahil ang teachers ay napakarami, half a million, pag nagbibigay ng extra bonus ang gobyerno hindi kasama ang teachers. Pero sa ating administrasyon sinasama natin ang mga guro sa ating extra Christmas bonus.

At sana yung PGEA, na kasama ng TUCP, ay mag-recruit ng mga guro bilang kasapi ninyo para mas marami tayong kabalikat sa pagpaplano ng kung anu-ano ang maganda sa mga guro.

Lubos ang aking tiwala na magtatagumpay kayo sa inyong bagong balakin. Sana rin ay ipagpatuloy ninyo ang suportang laging binibigay sa inyong lingkod.
