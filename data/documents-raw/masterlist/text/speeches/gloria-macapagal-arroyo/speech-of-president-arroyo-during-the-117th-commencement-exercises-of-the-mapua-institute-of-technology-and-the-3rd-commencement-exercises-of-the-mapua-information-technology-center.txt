Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the 117th Commencement Exercises of the Mapua Institute of Technology and the 3rd Commencement Exercises of the Mapua Information Technology Center

[Delivered at the Plenary Hall, PICC, Roxas Blvd., Pasay City, January 29, 2004]

Thank you very much.

Thank you, Dr. Vea; Ambassador Yuchengco, thank you for this wonderful honor. But more than that, thank you for bringing honor to our country because when you were ambassador to the United Nations, my Ambassador to the United Nations, you were the one who got our votes to get into the U.N. Security Council of 2004; Mrs. Helen Dee, thank you, too; Prime Minister Virata; Secretary Navarro; Dr. Torres; Mr. Lim Sim; Mr. Santos; Dr. Biglete; Dr. Valisno; deans and officers of the administration; parents; graduates; ladies and gentlemen:

I am happy to be here with the Mapua of the 21st century. Mapua has long been synonymous with quality engineering. It is among the institutions that has done us Filipinos proud. Aside from being the choice of Ambassador Yuchengco for his educational innovations for the 21st century, Mapua is also the alma mater of Dado Nanatao, the Filipino Bill Gates.

And so it is fitting today that among the many graduating students of Mapua that we congratulate, we also congratulate the first batch of computer science graduates. May there be more Dado Banataos among you. And may there be Alfonso Yuchengcos among you also in the future.

I congratulate you for graduating. There are many young people, other young people who are unable to graduate. Baka sa mga under classmen ninyo may mga nanganganib na baka hindi maka-graduate. Hindi dahil kulang sila sa utak kung hindi baka kulang sila sa pondo. Marami pa ngang napipilitang tumigil sa pag-aaral, kung kailan patapos na, dahil sa kakulangan ng pera.

In my going around among the people, I have found that many students and their families spend time and money for college, especially a good private college like Mapua, only to run out of resources on the final lap. Sa karaniwang Pilipino kailangan matugunan muna ang ilang pangunahing pangangailangan ng pamilya, tulad ng pagkain at iba pang gastos. Kaya madalas nahihirapan tustusan ang pag-aaral sa kolehiyo, lalo na kung kukonti na ang natitirang pondo.

Nauunawaan ko ang problemang ito. Ito ay kasaysayan ng aking ama, si dating pangulong Diosdado Macapagal. Siyanga pala, si Diosdado Macapagal ay isang matalik na kaibigan ni Don Alfonso Yuchengco. Every fifth jubilee of my father, Ambassador Yuchengco would give a dinner, a birthday dinner in his honor. Halimbawa, 65th birthday, 70th birthday, 75th birthday, 80th birthday. Minsan, nagkamali ako, nasabi ko kay Dr. Yuchengco na mag e-eighty five na ‘yung aking tatay. So, he gave him a dinner. Mag e-eighty four palang pala, nagkamali. But it was providential, he gave a dinner on his 84th birthday but on the 85th birthday of my father, he was already too sick to have been the guest of honor in yet another jubilee celebration. And so it was providential. And I’m very grateful that on the last possible birthday my father could celebrate physically, Ambassador Yuchengco was his host.

Pag-usapan ko ‘yung aking tatay. Siya ay nagsimula mag-aral sa abogasiya. Kagaya ng marami sa inyo nakapag-aral at nagtapos sa karera. Nagsimula siya sa abogasiya, mahirap lamang siya kaya siya ay woking student. Ngunit dahil mahirap siya, mahina ang kanyang katawan. Working student, nagtatrabaho, nag-aaral, naglalakad mula sa eskwela hanggang trabaho o trabaho hanggang eskwela hanggang tahanan. Nagkaroon tuloy ng tuberculosis. Sabi ng kaniyang doktor, “kailangan tumigil ka magtrabaho o tumigil ka mag-aral. Hindi pwede ‘yung dalawa.” ngunit papano naman siya mag-aaral kung titigil ng trabaho dahil mahirap lamang sila. Wala nang pang-tuition, kaya tumigil nagtrabaho, tumigil rin mag-aral. Mabuti na lamang ay may Don Honorio Ventura na isang mayaman pero bachelor. Nalaman ang kalagayan niya, binayaran ‘yung kaniyang pagpapatuloy ng pag-aaral. Kaya naging bar topnotcher siya tuloy at narating ang kaniyang tagumpay sa pagpupunyagi niyang matapos ang pag-aral.

Eh hindi lahat ng nakakapos ang estudyante ay nakakahanap ng kanilang don Honorio Ventura. Ni hindi lahat ay nakakahanap ng kanilang Don Alfonso Yuchengco. Kaya nagpasya ako na kumilos upang ang mga problema ng mga estudyanteng ganito ay malutas.

Napag-alaman ko na totoo may scholarship program at student loan program ang CHED, ngunit hindi ito nakaka-abot sa lahat ng mga nais nating tulungan. Siguro merong ilan sa inyo ay natulungan, hindi ko alam dahil alam ko meron mga 400,000 students ang natutulungan sa scholarship. Bukod dito, ang kanilang mga… ‘Yung CHED ‘yung naipagkakaloob ay hindi sapat para sakupin lahat ng gastos ng college education. Baka mabayaran ang tuition, baka hindi mo mababayad ‘yung ibang miscellaneous fees. But now in the 2004 budget, because we have been able to manage our budget deficit ceiling, we’ve been able to do appropriate fiscal policy, we’ve been able to generate more revenues than we targetted, the 2004 budget will give the biggest funding support for the education of deserving students.

Ngayon sa 2004, sa pamamagitan ng interest-free student loan program, ang mga kinakapos ng 3rd year, 4th year at graduating students ay hindi na mag-iisip kung saan kukuha nang pantustos sa kanilang pag-aaral. Dahil maaaring umutang kada semestre kapag kapos ang isang estudyante hanggang 8,000 pesos, at ito ay magsisimulang bayaran two years after graduation. Kung ang utang ay menos sa 4,000 pesos ito ay payable sa loob ng limang taon. Kung ang utang naman ay mas mataas sa 4,000 pesos hanggang 8,000 pesos ito ay sampung taon para bayaran.

And I have already released the initial 200 million pesos for the student loan program. Eto ang tunay na “Study Now, Pay Later.” get your degree, get a job within two years, then repay your loan. This is an important component of our fight for education for the average Filipino.

We Filipinos have always valued education. I’m sure the parents here are testimony to that. I’m sure many of your parents have sacrificed so that you can be in this hall today graduating. And thanks to your parents concern. Thanks for the value that the average Filipino gives to education, our country is ranked very highly. It is ranked number one in terms of the number of knowledge workers being produced. Many of these knowledge workers involved engineering, many of them involved information technology. And I congratulate Mapua under the leadership of Ambassador Yuchengco for this thrust of strengthening engineering but also computer science and information technology. Thus, the Mapua of the 21st century occupies a pivotal role in the knowledge society that our country is becoming known for. Information technology and its related industries, they are the growth sector of our future.

We have invested in the infrastructure here. During my administration, we were able to improve the bandwidth in the interconnectivity. So that today, it is only one-third of the cost of what it used to be before i was president.

And with Mapua producing graduates as what we are honoring today, we are also working on the human resources. Thus, we are producing the infrastructure and the human resources necessary for the development of information technology as the growth sector of our future.

Call centers and other back office operations which are — if you talk about I.T. in the range of skills, where on the one hand, you have software development at the highest range, and you have call centers at you would say relatively the lowest requirement of skills.

Let’s talk about call centers alone and back office operations. They grew from 2,000 jobs before I became president to now unexpected 40,000 jobs this year in 2004. And these jobs are growing every year.

This is not by accident. At the start of my tenure, I declared that technology will be the basis of our development. And it is becoming true in such a short tenure of my presidency. We can make technology work for and serve the average Filipino.

And I am very proud to be here and honored to be here to acknowledge that Mapua is playing a leading role in creating a modern and progressive Philippines.

To you, the fortunate products of Mapua, and its role in the knowledge society of the 21st century, congratulations!

Congratulations to this great institution. And congratulations to you, our graduates — the hope of the Philippines for the 21st century.

Thank you.
