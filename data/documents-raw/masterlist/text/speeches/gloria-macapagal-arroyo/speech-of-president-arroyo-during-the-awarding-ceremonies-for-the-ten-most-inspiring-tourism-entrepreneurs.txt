Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the awarding ceremonies for the Ten Most Inspiring Tourism Entrepreneurs

[Delivered at the Music Hall, SM Mall of Asia, Pasay City, March 3, 2007]

Maraming salamat, Secretary Cerge Remonde.

Noong si Cerge ay ilinagay natin bilang coordinator ng Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, ang tanong niya, ano ang aking marching order sa kanya. Sabi ko, basta suportahan mo ang ‘Go Negosyo,’ you won’t go wrong.

The inspiration and moving spirit of Go Negosyo, Presidential Consultant on Entreprenuership, Joey Concepcion; Vivienne Tan, who’s also a great leader of Go Negosyo; our Cabinet members Peter Favila, Secretary of Trade and Industry under whom we have the Small and Medium Enterprises Department and Secretary Arty Yap of Agriculture promoting entrepreneurs and agri-business; and representing Secretary Ace Durano who is based in Central Philippines, in Cebu, so here in Manila representing him is Oscar Palabyab.

I would like to congratulate and acknowledge Lray Villafuerte who was recognized today for his wakeboard tourism attraction in Bicol. And we have two other governors here from Central Philippines, which includes Bicol, Gwen Garcia of Cebu and Sally Perez of Antique. And we have the governor of peace and development from arms to farms — I think now he wants to include Patikul beach among our attractions — Governor Ben Loong. I hear that Governor Evardone is here. There you are, governor, who of course the landlord or the overseer of the Guiuan surfing tourism; our inspiring tourism awardees; ladies and gentlemen.

Talagang nasa diwa ng pagiging entrepreneur ang positibong pananaw sa kinabukasan, gaya ng magandang hinaharap ng ating ekonomiya. Sa ganito ring paningin, binabati ko itong ating mga inspiring tourism entrepreneur na pinararangalan natin ngayon sa kanilang mga nagawa.

Siyempre, diyaan sa mga yan merong mga malalaking puhunan ang kailangan. Yung mga mega resorts like Villa Escudero, yung mga airlines like Asian Spirit, yung mga hotel chains like Microtel. Pero meron ring maliliit na negosyo, yung adventure tourism ni Tony Carag raw nagsimula sa 50,000 pesos, yung souvenirs in Mr. Aldeguer ay nagsimula sa 20,000 pesos, at yung gumagawa ng ating map halos walang puhunan, nakihiram lang muna ng printer nung kanyang kapitbahay. Kaya marami talagang paraan kung papano maging entrepreneur, maging milyonaryo ka, marami kang minana o maging nagsisimula ka lamang. Kaya pinupuri ko rin yung ating mga teenpreneur awardee bilang sagisag ng maagang pagsisimula sa negosyo na marapat palaganapin.

Palakpakan din natin ang Philippine Center for Entrepreneurship, si Joey, si Vivienne at lahat ng ating mga young entrepreneurs, sa pagsulong nitong Tourism Negosyo Exposition.

The strength of the Philippine economy goes beyond transient political events like the forthcoming May elections. We have already laid the fundamentals of growth — a sturdy fiscal house, a solid program of good governance, a robust social payback to the people, mounting gains in the security front, and a democratic system in full bloom. The business community is on the right track in placing their bets on this nation and government, the excellence of the Filipino and the spirit of enterprise that lives in our society.

And let me say that as soon as the sun rises, we shall have clean, credible and peaceful elections in May. There should be no doubt that the Filipino will make this happen. We are a nation that cherishes democracy and freedom as deeply as we cherish enterprise and competition. Investors should go for the gold in the midst of the Philippine bull run.

Itong taong ito, gusto natin magkaroon ng higit tatlong milyong turista. Ang dami na ngang pumupunta dito, ang kulang natin mga kwarto. Kaya doon sa nanalo sa Bohol na masarap na restaurant, sabi ko, maglagay ka nga ng bed and breakfast. Yun nga ang kanyang ginagawa. Sa kabuuan ng ating ekonomiya, itong ating adhikain itong taon — mag-invest sa taong-bayan upang makamit ang walong biyaya sa 2008. Investing in the people so that we can achieve 8 by 08: job creation o hahapbuhay; cost of living o abot-kayang bilihin; strong peso o matatag na piso; more investment, maraming investment; Pro-poor education, edukasyon para sa mahihirap; pro-poor health care, housing, anti-hunger, paggamot, pabahay at pagkain para sa mahihirap; green Philippines, makakalikasan at berdeng Pilipinas. Binanggit nga ni Oca Palabyab, sabi niya, bakit berde ang uniporme ni Joey. Maliban sa siya ay tiga-La Salle, ang berde ay pag-asa, hope at ang berde ay eco-tourism for green Philippines; isa pang adhikain nating strong anti-terror, malakas na kampanya kontra-terorismo para sa ganun yung beach ng Patikul ay maraming pupunta doon, magbibisita kay Governor Ben Loong.

At para makatulong sa mga entrepreneur gaya ng sabi ko siyempre may malalaking negosyong malaki ang puhunan pero may maliliit na negosyo sa tourism. Doon sa Philippine Tourism Authority meron sampung milyong piso na microfinance fund ang Philippine Tourism Authority ng DOT para sa microfinance para sa programang Grassroots Entrepreneurs for Eco-Tourism or G.R.E.E.T. — greet.

Kaya inaanyayahan ko ang GO Negosyo makipagpartner sa PTA para itong nahahanap ninyong ating mga nagsisimulang maliliit na negosyante sa eco-tourism ay makahanap ng pansimulang pondo sa Philippine Tourism Authority. Sana makatulong ito sa Go Negosyo. Sabi nga ni Ace Durano, bawat isang turista makakalikha ng isang trabaho kaya kung malalampasan natin ng three million plus ang ating tourism itong taong ito — kasi kahit na halos three million umabot noong isang taon — ay dadami ang trabaho sa tourism lalung-lalo na sa ating mga entrepreneurs.

Sa inyong lahat, congratulations! Go Negosyo!

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
