Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the Opening Program of Technical-Vocational Education and Training with Arabic Language and Islamic Values Education at the TESDA-DepEd Jobs Fair for Muslims

[ Delivered at Hall A & B, Philippine Trade and Training Center, Pasay City, September 3, 2008 ]

Thank you for being here. At sa inyong lahat, ating mga kapatid na Muslim na naririto, sa lahat ng Muslim ng Pilipinas, lahat ng Pilipinong hindi Muslim, Assalamu Alaikum and Blessed Ramadhan!

Congratulations sa mga kabataang Muslim na nakatapos ng mga training sa ilalim ng programang ALIVE. Ang ganda naman ng programa ninyong ALIVE, buhay na buhay or Arabic Language and Islamic Values Education ng DepEd na sinundan ng skills training.

New and better collection has put the Philippines on a path to permanent, sustainable economic growth and stability. We are committed to consolidating the gains in new revenue by making long overdue investments in physical and human infrastructure. This includes along with billions of pesos for new bridges, roads and ports billions of pesos for education, healthcare and training.

In training halos walong milyong katao ang nakatapos sa iba’t-ibang training program sa ating administrasyon , double the number in the previous 14 years. Hangad natin kayong mga kabataan na matuto nang kasanayang magagamit para sa inyong kinabukasan. Pinaggastusan natin ng iba’t-ibang training programs kabilang ang maikling pagsasanay na pagkakakitaan agad tulad ng pananahi—may nakita akong dressmaking dito, sabi ni Boboy maganda ang mga opportunity, o pag-ayos ng buhok, sana maganda rin ang opportunity doon. Yung pini-phase out na natin yung caregiving kasi sobra na ang supply. Kaya yung mga hindi makakuha ng trabaho sa caregiving ngayon pumasok kayo muli sa ibang klaseng training dahiI marami pang iba.

Yung mga mas mahahabang programa tulad ng welding, automotive, cell phone repair, na malaki ang demand sa labas ng bansa, at mga kursong vocational o technical kabilang na ang skills upgrading. Eh, eto alas diyes tayo nagsimula ng programa pero anong oras nagsimula itong jobs fair? 8 o’clock. Eh, sabi ni Secretary Lapus sa akin, ilan na diyan ay nakakuha na ng trabaho. Diyan sa call centers meron nang nakakuha ng trabaho.

This is an auspicious way of celebrating the first days of Ramadhan. Kasisimula ng Ramadhan, and I want to use this opportunity to deliver to all of you our deepest prayer na sana’y maging daan ang Ramadhan towards achieving peace and quiet in the whole nation.

I would like to take this opportunity to assure you that the highest respect shall be accorded to our Muslim brothers and sisters during the month of Ramadhan.

I would like to assure you that the law enforcement activities in Mindanao shall give the utmost consideration to the unhampered observance of Ramadhan by peace loving and law abiding Muslims.

Marami sainyo ay kabataang Muslim ng Metro Manila at kapaligid, Cavite hanggang Pampanga, at marami sa inyo o magulang ninyo ay lumikas mula sa Mindanao . Mindanao continues to feed you even if you are here in Manila. Mindanao is a food basket of the Philippines, but the sad irony of Mindanao as food basket is that it has some of the highest hunger in our nation. Yun siguro ang isang dahilan kung bakit kayo, pamilya ninyo ay lumipat dito .

Mindanao has the large fields of the highest productivity, yet it also has the majority of our ten poorest provinces. The prime reason is the endless Mindanao conflict. A comprehensive peace has eluded us for half a century. And we thank our Muslim neighbors, we thank Malaysia for helping us in our search for that comprehensive peace in Mindanao. But, we must bring stability to the region and regain order before we can effectively reactivate the peace process in earnest.

In the light of the recent violent incidents committed by lawless MILF groups, the government will not sign the Memorandum of Agreement on Ancestral Domain. The peace process — and we’d like to assure you and our neighbors and continue to convey our gratitude to Malaysia — the peace process must proceed. But, only after we have expanded the number of stakeholders for consultation.

We have refocused all peace talks from one that is centered on dialogues with rebels to one of authentic dialogues with the communities. Our commitment is to peace, the constitutional process, and the rule of law.

Let me take this opportunity also to be very clear to those elements who seek disruption and destruction: There will be no peace gained through violence. No peace agreement can or will be reached through intimidation or the barrel of a gun. The way to peace isn’t through the barrel of a gun rather it begins with providing a person a job, food on the table and human dignity.

Central to that success is to invest in people, in education like ALIVE, in healthcare, roads, bridges and training such as TVET. And we are making those investments as we see in this affair, this jobs fair today. Bukod tangi ang programang TVET with ALIVE sapagkat ito ay nakalaan lamang para sa mga kabataang Muslim habang binibigyan kayo ng mga alternatibong kaalaman at livelihood training, binibigyan din kayo ng mga karagdagang kaalaman na sang-ayon sa mga panuntunan ng Islam.

Sa programang TVET with ALIVE, ipinagsasama ang programa ng Madrasah at ang pagtuturo ng kasanayang pangkabuhayan. Batay sa ginawang pagsangguni sa mga kabataan sa tatlumput walong bayan na karamiha’y Muslim, napili ang mga kursong tumututok sa mga pangangailangan at hinahangad na trabaho ng mga kabataan na Muslim.

Nitong nakaraang limang buwan, halos isang libong Muslim na out-of-school youth mula sa Metro Manila at Pampanga ang binigyan ng ALIVE training kasama ng pagsasanay para sa kabuhayan o TVET. At sabi ni Jing Lapus, ang target ay 25,000 Muslim youth to be able to benefit from TVET with ALIVE. At sa pamamagitan nitong jobs fair, inaasahan nating makapaghanap kaagad, kayong mga kabataang Muslim ng trabaho kaya natin idinaos itong exclusive na jobs fair para sa inyo .

Peace is a central ingredient to the nation’s future. It is a central ingredient for stability in our region. And we’d like to thank again our Malaysian brothers and to assure them that peace is a path we remain firmly committed to achieving, and this includes working to achieve a permanent peace in Mindanao.

With all of us working together, mga Muslim, mga Kristiyano, mga nandito sa Metro Manila, mga nandoon sa Mindanao , and all our brothers in the Muslim world and all our brothers and sisters outside the Muslim world, if we all work together, peace will happen. Samantala, sa ating jobs fair ngayon, hangad natin ang tagumpay ng kabataang Muslim.

Blessed Ramadhan at maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
