LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 8
TO    :   
The Secretary
Department
of Labor
Manila
Pursuant to Proclamation No. 1081 dated September 21, 1972, and General
Order No. 1 dated September 22, 1972, you are hereby directed to
implement immediately the wage levels established by the Wage
Commission for sugar workers.
Done in the City of Manila,
this 24th day of September, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred
and seventy-two.
