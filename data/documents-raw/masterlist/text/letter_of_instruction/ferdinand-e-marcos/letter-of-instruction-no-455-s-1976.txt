LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 455
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 455
TO   
:    The Secretary of Tourism
In view of the unchecked activities of vendors and hawkers of souvenir
items in motorized bancas which mar the pleasantness of "shooting the
rapids" in Pagsanjan, Laguna, you are hereby authorized to extend the
Philippine Tourism Authority's control and administration over the
Pagsanjan Gorge Tourist Zone as authorized under Proclamation No. 1551
dated May 31, 1976, over Pagsanjan River from the Falls downstream to
the vicinity of Pagsanjan Rapids Hotel, including the river banks.
