LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 679
TO:   
:     The Secretary, Department of Trade
The Chairman, Textile Export Board
To avoid disruption of garment exports, implementation of LOI No. 676
dated March 2, 1978, is hereby deferred pending formulation of the
implementing guidelines by the Department of Trade in consultation with
the private industry sector and approval thereof by the NEDA Board.
Done in the City of Manila,
this 10th day of March, in the year of Our Lord, Nineteen Hundred and
Seventy-eight.
