LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 822
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 822
TO   
:    The Minister Ministry of Natural Resources
In line with the government's effort to promote the proper management,
utilization, protection, rehabilitation and development of forest
resources, effective immediately, permits to operate sawmills
previously granted to those without any timber license, logging
concessions, or authorized sources of logs are hereby cancelled.
For immediate compliance.
