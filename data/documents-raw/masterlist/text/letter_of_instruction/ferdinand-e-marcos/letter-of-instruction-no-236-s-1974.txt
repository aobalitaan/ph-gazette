LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 236
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 236
TO   
:      The Secretary of Finance
The Director General, NEDA
The Commissioner of the Budget
The Chairman, Career Executive Service Board
You are hereby directed to implement effective December 1, 1974 the
following directives:
1.   
Adjust the salaries of department secretaries and
officials of equivalent rank to P50,000 per annum.
2.    Adjust the
salaries of department undersecretaries
and officials of equivalent rank to P38,000 per annum.
Done in the City of Manila,
this 24th day of December, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred
and seventy-four.
