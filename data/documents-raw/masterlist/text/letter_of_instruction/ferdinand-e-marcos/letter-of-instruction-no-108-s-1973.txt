LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 108
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 108
TO   
:      Brig. Gen. Prospero Olivas
Chief, CIS, PC
THRU    :    The Chief of Constabulary
Conduct an investigation to ascertain responsibility for the loss of
three hundred forty-four (344) hogsheads and the pilferage of the
contents of two hundred (200) other hogsheads of PTVA tobacco stored in
the warehouses of Farmers' Virginia Tobacco Redriers at Bauang, La
Union.
Initiate the preparation of appropriate criminal charges against the
party or parties responsible for this loss of government property.
Inform this Office of your findings and the action taken thereon.
August 2, 1973
