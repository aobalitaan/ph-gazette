LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 1249
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 1249
TO   
:    All Members of the Cabinet
Henceforth, any availment from the National Security Fund or any
Confidential or Intelligence Funds shall be solely upon my authority or
direction. Before their release, the purpose of their use shall be
explained to me confidentially.
Done in the City of Manila,
this 21st day of June, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and
eighty-two.
