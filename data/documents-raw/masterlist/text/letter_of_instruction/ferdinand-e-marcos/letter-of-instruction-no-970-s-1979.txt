LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 970
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 970
TO   
:      The Minister of Human Settlements or
her representative
The Minister of Energy or his representative
The Minister of Tourism or his representative
A representative from the Office of the City Mayor
of Iligan City
You are hereby constituted into a Committee with the Minister of Human
Settlements or her representative as Chairman and the rest as Members
to study ways and means of restoring the Maria Christina Falls.
You shall submit your report to the President of the Philippines not
later than March 1, 1980.
Done in the City of Manila,
this 19th day of December, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred
and seventy-nine.
