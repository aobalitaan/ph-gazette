LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 1072
LETTER OF INSTRUCTIONS NO. 1072
TO   
:      The Chairman
Philippine Medical Care Commission
The Philippine Medical Care Commission is hereby authorized to transfer
the functions of the Executive Director which is concurrently performed
by the Vice-Chairman, to a career official within that organization who
shall receive the same emoluments as the original Executive Director.
Done in the City of Manila,
this 13th day of October, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and
eighty.
