ACT NO. 2856
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 2856 - AN ACT

AMENDING A CERTAIN ITEM OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED TWENTY HUNDRED

NINETY-THREE, ENTITLED "AN ACT APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR PUBLIC WORKS AND

FOR OTHER PURPOSES"
Section 1.    On

page 250 of Republic Act Numbered Twenty hundred ninety-three, item

(g), under Group II for the Province of Negros Oriental, and under

Title M — Short Term Projects, is hereby amended to read as

follows:
"(g)   

Cangmalalag-Cabulihan feeder road,
Larena — Repair and/or

construction    P10,000.00"
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Enacted without Executive

approval, June 19, 1960.
