ACT NO. 3316
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 3316 - AN ACT

FURTHER AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED NINETEEN HUNDRED, ENTITLED "AN

ACT APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR PUBLIC WORKS," AS AMENDED
Section 1.    Item ( f -1), page 352, Group II, for the Province

of Negros Oriental, Section two, entitled "Short Term Projects," of

Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred, as amended by Republic Act

Numbered Twenty-eight hundred fifty-eight, is hereby further amended to

read as follows:
"( f -1)    Larena-Basac-Maria Road Repair and/or
improvement    P10,000.00."
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Enacted, without Executive

approval, June 18, 1961.
