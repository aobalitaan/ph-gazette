ACT NO. 1107
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 1107 - AN ACT

CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF BUAYAN, IN THE PROVINCE OF

COTABATO, TO GENERAL SANTOS
Section 1.     The

name of the Municipality of Buayan, in the Province of Cotabato, is

changed to General Santos.
cralaw
Sec. 2.     This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Approved:
June 15, 1954
