ACT NO. 1246
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 1246  - AN

ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF CONCEPCION, PROVINCE OF

ILOCOS SUR, TO GREGORIO DEL PILAR
Section 1.    The

name of the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Ilocos Sur, is

hereby changed to Gregorio del Pilar.
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
cralaw
Approved:
June 10, 1955
