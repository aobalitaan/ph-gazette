ACT NO. 1035
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 1035 - AN ACT

CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF DULAWAN IN THE PROVINCE OF

COTABATO TO DATU PIANG
Section 1.     The

name of the Municipality of Dulawan in the Province of Cotabato is

hereby changed to Datu Piang.
cralaw
Sec. 2.     This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
cralaw
Approved:
June 12, 1954
