ACT NO. 1034
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 1034 - AN ACT

CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO OF BANAWA, IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF KABAGAN,

PROVINCE OF COTABATO, TO BANAWAG
Section 1.     The

name of Barrio of Banawa, in the Municipality of Kabagan, Province of

Cotabato, is hereby changed to Banawag.
cralaw
Sec. 2.     This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Approved:
June 12, 1954
