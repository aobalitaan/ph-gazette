[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7290, March 24, 1992 ]
AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DAGOHOY MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF INABANGA, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, TO FRANCISCO DAGOHOY MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
1�шphi1
Section 1.
The name of the Dagohoy Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Inabanga, Province of Bohol, is hereby changed to Francisco Dagohoy Municipal Hospital.
Section 2.
This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
Approved,
(Sgd.)
NEPTALI A. GONZALES
President of the Senate
(Sgd.)
RAMON V. MITRA
Speaker of the House of Representatives
This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on January 23, 1991 and January 23, 1992, respectively.
