ACT NO. 6844
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6844  - AN

ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MARCOS BRIDGE IN BARANGAY LAUIS,

MUNICIPALITY OF CANDELARIA, PROVINCE OF ZAMBALES, TO LAUIS BRIDGE
Section 1.    The

name of the Marcos Bridge in Barangay Lauis, Municipality of

Candelaria, Province of Zambales, is hereby changed to Lauis Bridge.
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Enacted without executive

approval, January 18, 1990.
