Congress of the Philippines

REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6654
May 20, 1988
AN ACT REPEALING SECTION FORTY OF PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NUMBERED EIGHT HUNDRED SEVEN, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE CIVIL SERVICE DECREE
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
Section 1.
Sec. Forty of Presidential Decree Numbered Eight Hundred Seven, otherwise known as the Civil Service Decree, is hereby repealed.
