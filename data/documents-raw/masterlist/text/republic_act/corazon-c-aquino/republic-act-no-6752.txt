ACT NO. 6752
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6752 - AN ACT

CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN MIGUEL MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN SAN

MIGUEL, LEYTE, TO SAN MIGUEL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL
Section 1.    The

name of the San Miguel Municipal High School in San Miguel, Leyte, is

hereby changed to San Miguel National High School.
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Approved:
August 11, 1989
