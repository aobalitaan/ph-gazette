ACT NO. 6840
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6840  - AN

ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PAARALANG ELEMENTARYA NG BADING IN BUTUAN

CITY TO PAARALANG ELEMENTARYA NG OBRERO
Section 1.    The

name of the Paaralang Elementarya ng Bading in Butuan City is hereby

changed to Paaralang Elementarya ng Obrero.
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Approved:
January 5, 1990
