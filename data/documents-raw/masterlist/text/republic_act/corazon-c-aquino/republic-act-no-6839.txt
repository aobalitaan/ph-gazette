ACT NO. 6839
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6839  - AN

ACT RENAMING THE ILIJAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL TO PEDRO S. TOLENTINO

MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
Section 1.    The

Ilijan Elementary School located in Barangay Ilijan, City of Batangas,

is hereby renamed as the Pedro S. Tolentino Memorial Elementary School.
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Approved:
January 5, 1990
