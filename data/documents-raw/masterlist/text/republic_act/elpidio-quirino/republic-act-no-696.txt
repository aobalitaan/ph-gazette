ACT NO. 696
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 696 - AN ACT

TRANSFERRING THE BARRIO OF STA. MONICA (TUBABAO), MUNICIPALITY OF ORAS

TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN POLICARPIO ALL IN THE PROVINCE OF SAMAR
Section 1.    The

barrio of Sta. Monica (Tubabao), Municipality of Oras, Province of

Samar is hereby separated from its mother municipality and included in

the Municipality of San Policarpio in the same province.
Sec. 2.    This Act shall take effect upon its

approval.
Approved:
May 9, 1952
