AN ACT DIVIDING BARANGAY TANZA IN THE CITY
OF NAVOTAS INTO TWO (2) DISTINCT AND
INDEPENDENT BARANGAYS TO BE KNOWN AS
BARANGAY TANZA 1 AND BARANGAY TANZA 2

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Division of Barangay Tanza. — Barangay
Tanza in the City of Navotas is hereby divided into
two (2) distinct and independent barangays to be known as
Barangay Tanza 1 and Barangay Tanza 2.

SEC. 3. Conduct and Supervision of Plebiscite. — The
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) shall conduct and
supervise the plebiscite to be held in Barangay Tanza in
the City of Navotas not later than one hundred twenty (120)
days from the effectivity of this Act.


The expenses for such plebiscite shall be borne by the
City of Navotas.

SEC. 4. Special Election of Barangay Officials. — The
first set of officials of barangays created under Section 1
hereof shall be elected not later than ninety (90) days from
approval by majority votes cast in a plebiscite held
for the purpose.

The COMELEC shall conduct and supervise the special
elections to be held in Barangays Tanza 1 and Tanza 2 in
the City of Navotas within sixty (60) days after the

ratification of this Act in a plebiscite.

The expenses for such special elections shall be borne
by the City of Navotas.

Sec. 5. Incumbent Barangay Officials. — The incumbent
barangay officials of Barangay Tanza shall continue to
hold office and administer Barangays Tanza 1 and Tanza 2
until such time that the officials of the newly created
barangays have been duly elected and qualified.

Src. 6. Public Infrastructure and Facilities. — All
existing public infrastructure and facilities for public
use within the designated ‘territories of the new barangays
at the time of the approval of this Act are hereby
transferred without cost or compensation to and shall be
administered by them.

SEC. 7. Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA) Shares. —
Barangay Tanza 1 and Barangay Tanza 2 in the
City of Navotas shall be entitled to Internal Revenue
Allotment (RA) shares pursuant to Section 285 of Republic
Act No. 7160, as amended, otherwise known as the "Local
Government Code of 1991".

 

SEC. 8. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen
(15) days after its publication in the Official Gazetie or
in any newspaper of general circulation.
