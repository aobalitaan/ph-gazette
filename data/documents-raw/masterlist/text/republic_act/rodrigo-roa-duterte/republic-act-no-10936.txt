AN ACT CREATING A BARANGAY TO BE KNOWN AS
BARANGAY COMAWAS IN THE CITY OF BISLIG,

PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DELSUR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the

Philippines in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Creation of Barangay Comawas. — There is
hereby created a barangay to be known as Barangay Comawas in

the City of Bislig, Province of Surigao del Sur.

The territorial boundaries of Barangay Comawas are

Sec. 2. Conduct and Supervision of Plebiscite. — The
plebiscite conducted and supervised by the Commission
on Elections (COMELEC) pursuant to Provincial Ordinance
No. 103-94 of the Sangguniang Panlalawigan of the Province of
Surigao del Sur, which ratified the creation of Barangay Comawas
as proclaimed by the Plebiscite Board of Canvassers on
September 9, 1996, shall serve as a substantial compliance with
the plebiscite requirement under Section 10 of Republic Act
No. 7160, as amended, otherwise known as the “Local Government
Code of 1991”.

SEC. 3. Incumbent Barangay Officials. — The incumbent
barangay officials of Barangay Comawas, City of Bislig, Province
of Surigao del Sur shall continue to hold office until such time
that their successors have been duly elected and qualified.

Sec. 4. Public Infrastructure and Facilities. — All public
infrastructure and facilities for public use existing therein at the
time of the approval of this Act are hereby transferred without
cost or compensation to and shall be administered by the new
barangay.

 

SEC. 5. internal Revenue Allotment Shares. — Barangay
Comawas shall be entitled to Internal Revenue Allotment shares
pursuant to Section 285 of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended,
otherwise known as the “Local Government Code of 1991”.

Suc. 6. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen
(15) days after its publication in the Official Gazetie or in any
newspaper of general circulation.
