MALACANAN PALACE. .
MANILA 2

SPECIAL ORDER NO. 420

DESIGNATING THE EXECUTIVE SECRETARY AS OFFICER-IN-
CHARGE ON 24 TO 28 APRIL 2019

WHEREAS, the President will be in Beijing, People’s Republic of China to
participate the 2" Belt and Road Forum for International Cooperation on 24 to 28 April
2019; and

WHEREAS, to ensure continuity of government service, it is necessary to
designate an Officer-in-Charge to take care of the day-to-day operations in the Office of
the President and oversee the general administration of the Executive Department;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, RODRIGO ROA DUTERTE, President of the Republic
of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by law, do hereby order:

1. The Executive Secretary shall act as Officer-in-Charge to take care of the
day-to-day operations in the Office of the President and to oversee the general
administration of the Executive Department, and he shall, if necessary, act for and on
behalf of the President, except on matters that the President is required, by the
Constitution to act in person, during the time that the President is outside the Republic
of the Philippines from 24 to 28 April 2019.

2. All departments, agencies, and instrumentalities of the Government shall
assist the Executive Secretary in the discharge of his functions as stated herein.

3. All acts of the Executive Secretary for and on behalf of the President
pursuant to this Order shall be deemed acts of the President unless disapproved or
reprobated by the President.

DONE, in the City of Manila, this 17!" day of April, in the year of Our Lord, Two
Thousand and Nineteen.
