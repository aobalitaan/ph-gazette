MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 396, September 29, 1970 ]
EXCUSING FROM OFFICE GOVERNMENT PERSONNEL BEGINNING
3:30 P.M., SEPTEMBER 29, 1970
In view of the fact that public transportation services in the Greater Manila Area are disrupted due to the strike staged by jeepney drivers, government officials and employees holding office in the Greater Manila Areaa are hereby excused from work this afternoon only, beginning 3:30 p.m.
By authority of the President
(SGD.)
ROBERTO V. REYES
Acting Assistant Executive Secretary
