AMENDING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 138 OF THIS OFFICE, SERIES OF 1967.
Memorandum Circular No. 138 of this Office dated December 15, 1967, prescribing the policy regarding the grant of representation allowances to certain local government officials, is hereby amended so as to include Provincial Board and City Board/Council Secretaries among the officials entitled to representation allowances provided that the rates for them shall be as follows:
P200.00
for those in the Cities of Manila, Quezon, Baguio, Pasay and all First and Second Class Provinces and Cities.
