MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 1294
MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 1294 -
REINSTATING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 1266, SERIES OF 1983
In the interest of the speedy
administration of justice, Memorandum Circular No. 1266 dated November
4, 1983 which provides certain guidelines and procedural requirements
before cases are appealed to the Office of the President, is hereby
reinstated. Accordingly, Memorandum Circular No. 1268 dated November
18, 1983 is hereby revoked.
This Circular shall take effect immediately.
October 31, 1985
