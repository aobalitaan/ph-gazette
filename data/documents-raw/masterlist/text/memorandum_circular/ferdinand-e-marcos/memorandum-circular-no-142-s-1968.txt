MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 142, January 2, 1968 ]
AMENDING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 140 DATED DECEMBER 21, 1967.
Memorandum Circular No. 140 of this Office dated December 21, 1967, urging attendance of certain government employees in the Second National Convention of the Philippine Association of Occupational Medicine on February 2-4, 1968, is hereby amended so as to change the convention site from Manila to Baguio City, subject to the same terms and conditions set forth therein.
