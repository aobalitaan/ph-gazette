BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 578, June 5, 1972 ]
AMENDING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 570 DATED MAY 10, 1972.
Memorandum Circular No. 570 dated May 10, 1972, authorizing attendance in the Fourth General Assembly of the Kaisahan Ng Mga Kawani Ng Pamahalaan (KKP), Philippine Government Employees Union, is hereby amended so as to indicate the date of holding the Assembly as August 17-18, 1972, instead of June 8-9, 1972.
