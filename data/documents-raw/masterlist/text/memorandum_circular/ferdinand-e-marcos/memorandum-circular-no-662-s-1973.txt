MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 662, September 5, 1973 ]
AMENDING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 655 DATED AUGUST 15, 1973.
Memorandum Circular No. 655 dated August 15, 1973, authorizing the attendance of municipal judges in the Third Annual Convention-Seminar of the Municipal Judges League of the Philippines to be held in Manila from November 10 to 17, 1973, is hereby amended so as to reschedule the convention on October 22-25, 1973.
