MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 423, January 8, 1971 ]
EXCUSING FROM OFFICE GOVERNMENT PERSONNEL BEGINNING 3:00 P.M., JANUARY 8, 1971.
In view of the fact that public transportation services in the Greater Manila Area are disrupted due to the strike staged by jeepney drivers, government officials and employees holding office in the Greater Manila Area are hereby excused from work this afternoon only, beginning 3:00 p.m.
