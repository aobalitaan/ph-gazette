MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 366, June 26, 1970 ]
AMENDING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 364, SERIES OF 1970
Memorandum Circular No. 364, series of 1970, creating a committee to study and implement the recommendation of the Epifanio Delos Santos Committee (EDSA) to ease traffic congestion along Epifanio Delos Santos Avenue, is hereby amended so as to include the mayor of Mandaluyong, Manila, Rizal, as an additional member of the said committee.
By the authority of the President:
(SGD.)
PONCIANO G.A. MATHAY
Acting Executive Secretary
