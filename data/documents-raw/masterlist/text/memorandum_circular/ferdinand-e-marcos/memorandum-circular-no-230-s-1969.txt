MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 230, February 6, 1969 ]
EXCUSING MANILA PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS FROM CLASSESS FROM JANUARY 20 TO 24, 1969.
In view of the fact that the period from January 20 to 24, 1969, was devoted to discussing and solving problems related to the service in the Bureau of Public Schools, public school teachers in the City of Manila affected said period of discussion are hereby excused from their classes for the above period.
By authority of the President
(SGD.)
RAFAEL M. SALAS
Executive Secretary
