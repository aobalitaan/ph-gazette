BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 308, December 23, 1969 ]
PROHIBITING THE GRANT OF THE PREFERRED EXCHANGE RATE OF ?2 TO $1 FOR TRAVEL PURPOSES.
No agency or instrumentality of the Government shall henceforth authorize, direct or allow the dollar exchange for travel purposes at the rate of ?2 to $1 or any preferential or preferred rate other than the prevailing rate of exchange for normal commercial banking transactions.
By authority of the President:
(SGD.)
ERNESTO M. MACEDA
Executive Secretary
