MALACAÑAN PALACE
MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 259, May 13, 1969 ]
DIRECTING THAT ALL INDIVIDUAL COMPLAINTS OF SMALL LOT BUYERS BE REFERRED TO THE PRESIDENTIAL ASSISTANT ON HOUSING AND RESETTLEMENT AGENCY.
To facilitate the solution of the problems of small lot buyers in various real estate subdivisions, it is hereby directed that all individual complaints of small lot buyers be referred to the Presidential assistant on Housing and Resettlement Agency for appropriate action.
