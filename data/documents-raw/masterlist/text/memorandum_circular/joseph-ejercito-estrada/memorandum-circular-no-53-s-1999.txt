MANILA
BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
[ Memorandum Circular No. 53, December 23, 1999 ]
All officials and employees of the government, including government owned and controlled corporations, may take the afternoon off effective 12:01 p.m. of December 24, 1999, to enable them to celebrate the traditional Christmas Eve with their families and loved ones. Government agencies performing vital functions shall, however, maintain a skeletal work force.
