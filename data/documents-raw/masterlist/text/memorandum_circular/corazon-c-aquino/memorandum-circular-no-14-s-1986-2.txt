MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 14
MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 14 -
SUSPENDING REGULAR WORKING HOURS, INCLUDING REGULAR CLASSES, BOTH
PUBLIC AND PRIVATE, ON JULY 10, 1986
In view of inclement weather brought about
by typhoon "Gading", regular working hours in government and private
offices in Metropolitan Manila, including classes in all levels in both
public and private schools on July 10, 1986 are hereby
suspended.
Manila, July 10, 1986.
