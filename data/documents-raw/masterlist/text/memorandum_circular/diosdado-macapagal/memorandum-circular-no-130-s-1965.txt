AMENDING MEMORANDUM CIRCULAR NO. 128 DATED JUNE 28, 1965, URGING ATTENDANCE IN THE 2ND NATIONAL CONVENTION OF GOVERNMENT CARTOGRAPHIC, PHOTOGRAMMETRIC AND GEODETIC ENGINEERS AND SURVEYORS.
Memorandum Circular No. 128 of this Office dated June 28, 1965, urging attendance in the 2nd Convention of Cartographic , Photogrammetric and Geodetic Engineers and Surveyors to be held on July 26-31, 1965, is hereby amended, so as to change the convention site from Manila to Quezon City.
