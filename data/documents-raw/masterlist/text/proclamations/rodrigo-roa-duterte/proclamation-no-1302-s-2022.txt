BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES
PROCLAMATION NO. 1302

RESERVING A PARCEL OF PATRIMONIAL LAND WITHIN THE ALABANG
STOCK FARM SITUATED IN BARANGAY ALABANG, MUNTINLUPA CITY,
FOR RESEARCH INSTITUTE FOR TROPICAL MEDICINE (RITM) BUILDING
SITE PURPOSES, AND TRANSFERRING OWNERSHIP THEREOF IN
FAVOR OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH) - RITM

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, and upon the recommendation of
the Secretary of Environment and Natural Resources, I], RODRIGO ROA DUTERTE,
President of the Republic of the Philippines, do withdraw from entry, sale or other
forms of disposition a certain parcel of patrimonial land within the Alabang Stock Farm,
Barangay Alabang, Muntinlupa City, reserve the same for the RITM Building Site, and
transfer ownership thereof in favor of the DOH - RITM. This parcel of land is more
particularly described as follows:
