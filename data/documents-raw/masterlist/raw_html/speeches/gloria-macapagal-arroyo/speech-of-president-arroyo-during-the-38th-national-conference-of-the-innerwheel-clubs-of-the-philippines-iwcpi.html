Speech of President Arroyo during the 38th National Conference of the Innerwheel Clubs of the Philippines (IWCPI)

February 16, 2004

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the 38th National Conference of the Innerwheel Clubs of the Philippines (IWCPI)

[Delivered at the Fiesta Pavilion, Manila Hotel, February 16, 2004]

Thank you.

Thank you very much. Thank you very much, baby for your very nice introduction. And its campaign time so it was not wrong of you to give me an endorsement. Thank you for your endorsement.

I would like to greet and congratulate all the officers and members of the National Innerwheel headed by Aida Vera Cruz. Thank you for your beautiful report and I take this opportunity to greet her husband, my former presidential assistant for Western Mindanao, Boyet Vera Cruz.

And of course, speaking of VIP’s on the floor, past president and now congresswoman, Congresswoman Nining, thank you very much, Nining Nicolas, very nice to see you here.

I would like to also greet the president of the Innerwheel foundation, Mrs. Carmen Santillan. Thank you for your donation to our health program. And of course, the only Filipina who has become president of Innerwheel Rotary, Ms. Ester Vibal.

How nice to be here among empowered and empowering women. You are empowered because you are leaders but you also empower because of your concern for the communities and the families in the communities. Congratulations for your good work.

I didn’t hesitate to say yes when baby invited me to be with you today. Notwithstanding, that for us national candidates during this time, our schedule is usually dictated by the campaign manager. But I ask my campaign managers, make sure that I am in manila when the Innerwheel has its convention.

Because being a rotary arm, I know how Filipino Rotarians and Rotary arms, I know how the Innerwheel clubs have initiated all the good things that Aida told us about over the last 38 years and I am especially very appreciative of the programs that you have initiated for peace, for the alleviation of hunger for literacy for the youth and volunteerism. You do this because you want to be agents of change. And you are women leaders in the community, you have the power to be agents of change and you use that power.

Yes, we all want change and speaking woman-to-woman, I know that the change, that we women want are those that will make life better for the average Filipino family. We are women, we are family-oriented, and even if we are community-oriented, even if we are nation-oriented, it is still the family in the community, it is the family in the nation that is what we want to help make life better for. That is why you are working on things like alleviation of poverty especially, therefore, food on every table, because that is the bottom line as far as poverty is concerned. That is why you are working for peace because our families want to have safety and security. That’s why you’re working for youth projects, for education projects, for literacy, because we know that education is the key to the improvement of the life of the ordinary Filipino.

Well, on the one hand, I thought that since the campaign was gonna start before your convention, there was a need to make sure that my schedule didn’t clash with the campaign. But on the other hand, I think it is also God-given that I would be able to speak with you during the campaign period so that I can take this opportunity to say and to ask you to tell all of the many people you influence, in all of the many communities in which you work that among the candidates, Gloria Arroyo is in the best position to achieve the changes we want. Why? Because I have gained the clout and the experience to turn the economy around.

In a short time I have made a strong start. I have turned the economy around and stabilized the mess we inherited. I guess the best way, the shortest way to describe the mess that we inherited is that in the three years before EDSA 2 made me president, our economy was only able to create one million jobs. That’s why when I became president at EDSA 2, I inherited more than four million unemployed Filipinos. But in a short time, we have created now, in my short administration more than three million jobs.

Indeed, the most important thing, if we want to have food on every table, if we want to alleviate hunger is to create jobs, to create investments and to have people like the Innerwheel helping take care of the marginalized families and the poorest of the poor.

Literacy and youth, indeed, we have in our short administration worked for the betterment of our education system. We are in the 21st century and there are challenges of competitiveness in the 21st century that our educational system must be able to meet and that is why during the short period of my presidency we have improved math and science and English in order to make our young more competitive in the 21st century. And I thank the Innerwheel because again you helped the government, not necessarily on a government-to-Innerwheel kind of partnership but because you helped the young and you helped to increase literacy in the communities, and I thank you for that because what you do is volunteerism.

I thank you in your work, in your programs for peace because I know that you know, we all know as mothers, as family members that we want safety and security for the family. And in my administration, being a mother and now a grandmother who wants to have our families live in peace in the community, I have done unprecedented things in order to fight terrorism, fight kidnapping, fight the scourge of drugs, fight the corruption that makes public officials and law enforcement officers tied up with the syndicates that perpetrate these crimes.

Ang dapat nasa preso ay yung mga kidnappers at saka yung mga druglords. At yan ang ginawa natin kasama si Ning Nicholas doon sa paggawa ng matigas na batas kontra sa droga. Kaya ngayon, doon nga sa inyong distrito, ning, kakahuli lang natin ng 1.5 billion pesos worth of shabu. Kasama na yon sa napakaraming na-raid na mga drug laboratories, drug syndicates, yung mga drug smuggling pati na rin yung mga druglords na noong araw nakakaiwas sa batas, dahil mahina yung batas nakakapagpiyansa. Ngayon, ang dami-dami nating mga druglords na nasa preso, including the number one drug lord of them all, William Gan, at yung nahuli doon sa Meycauayan sa Bulacan, nahuli pati yung kasabwat na sindikato sa China. Dahil dito sa kalakasan ng ating foreign policy, preso sila sa China, preso yung mga nandito sa Pilipinas at dahil sa malakas na batas na napagawa sa ating administrasyon, kung ikaw ay druglord, panghabangbuhay kang nasa preso. Gaya nang sabi ko, ang dapat nasa preso, siksik na siksik na yung ating mga preso dahil sa dami ng druglords na nahuhuli at kidnappers. Dapat ang nasa preso druglord at kidnapper, at hindi iyung ating matatandang more than 70 years old.

Kaya noong yung Innerwheel Club ay nilakad itong ating dalawang lola, matatanda doon sa Women’s Correctional, more than 70 years old, iyan ang pangunahing dahilan na ako ay pumayag na mabigyan sila ng patawad sa kanilang kasalanan. Kasi ang ating paniniwala, ang preso sa 21st century ay hindi para sa benggansa, ito ay para sa rehabilitation. Siguro naman, by the time they are 70 years old, and so many years in the Women’s Correctional, rehabilitated na sila. So, its time for them, more than… Oh, its already overdue for them to enjoy the comfort and company of their family. Kaya patakaran ko ito, basta hindi sila druglord at hindi sila kidnapper, pag more than 70 years old, mag-recommend kayo at ipa-pardon ko rin sila.

Nagpapasalamat ako sa inyong mga programa tungkol sa kalusugan, kaya nandito si Undersecretary Galon, tinanggap yung inyong kontribusyon, 5,000 pesos yon bawat lalagyan. Thank you for your support. At mabuti na rin na si Baby Golez, ang National Conference Chairman, gayundin ang kaniyang anak na si Tony Golez, na aking inaanak, ay siya ang head executive assistant ng Secretary of Health. So, bagay na bagay ang partnership ng Innerwheel Club at Department of Health.

Alam mo si Tony Golez una ko nakilala, dahil siya ay isang lider nung tinatawag na lingkod E.R. estudyante pa siya, medical student at nagtutulong sila bilang intern doon sa emergency room ng U.S.T. tapos nakita nila ang mga pangangailangan, mga kakulangan, kaya nagtayo sila ng lingkod E.R. para makakuha ng kontribusyon para matakpan yung mga pangangailangan sa emergency room. Pero yung mga bagay na yan ay puwede nating mapagtulungan pa sa isa’t-isa.

Noong ako ay senador, may batas na ipinasa — National Health Insurance. Ibig sabihin noon, supposed to be lahat ng pamilya ay matatakpan, masasakop sa health insurance. Kanya lamang noong akoy naging pangulo, nakita ko na hindi pala natutupad yon. yung may health insurance lamang pala halos ay yung mga GSIS at SSS members. In other words, yung mga employed formally. yung mga unemployed or informal workers wala. Sa batas pag sila ay mahihirap dapat yung local government ang nagko-contribute ng premium nila, pero maraming local government, siyempre lalo na yung mga mahihirap na local government, mayroon silang ibang mga priorities hindi nila kaya bayaran yung mga premium na yon. Kaya noong akoy naging pangulo, para naman yung mga emergency room kagaya ng U.S.T. lalo na kung mako-confine sila sa hospital ay hindi na kailangan maghahanap-hanap pa ng kontribusyon, puwedeng bayaran ng National Health Insurance. Ang ginawa ko ay pinalawak yung programa ng health insurance. Iyung mga mahihirap, tapos yung local government ay mahirap, ay nag-partner ang PCSO at saka ang local government para bayaran ang kanilang premium. Milyung-milyong Pilipino ang naipasok natin dito, kaya ngayon ay majority na ng pamilyang pilipino ay sakop na ng National Health Insurance.

Pero down payment lamang yan. Kasi kung nagawa nating majority ay sakop ng health insurance, our next ambition, lahat masakop ng health insurance. Kaya sabi ko sa PCSO, pag yung local government ay hindi kaya iyung kaniyang counterpart — sinabi ko rin sa DBM dahil mayroon din siyang subsidy portion para sa health insurance — akuin na ninyo yung buong premium para lahat ay maipasok.

Kaya ito naman ang aking hiling sa ating mga Innerwheel dahil ang dami nyong mga pinaglilingkuran na mga community, pamayanan, kung doon sa mga pinaglilingkuran ninyo ay meron kayong matagpuan na mahihirap na wala pa silang Philhealth — nandiyan naman si Baby eh, nandiyan naman si Tony — siguro, Tony, Baby, lets have a program whereby the Innerwheel can submit the application forms of the indigents who are not yet covered by health insurance to the DOH through Tony para sa ganun pag nahospital yung mga mahihirap na pinaglilingkuran ninyo, hindi na kayo ang maglalabas sa inyong bulsa para mabigyan sila ng bayad sa kanilang hospitalization, yung health insurance na ang babayad.

Ang dami-dami nating puwedeng magawa na magkasama sa isa’t-isa. At ako ay nasisiyahan na talagang sinabi ninyo na ako ang gusto ninyong guest of honor itong araw na ito. Salamat sa pagkakataon dahil tayo mga kababaihan, siyempre tayo ay dapat na magkasama para alagaan, hindi lamang ang ating mga pamilya dahil halos lahat ng mga innerwheel naman ay mayroon namang kakayahang pag alagaan ang inyong pamilya kaya pamilya ng pamayanan na pinipili ninyo ang inyong alagaan.

Magsama-sama tayo. Congratulations on your 38th years of serving the Filipino family.

Let us be together for the next six years and serve the Filipino family of the 21st century.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
