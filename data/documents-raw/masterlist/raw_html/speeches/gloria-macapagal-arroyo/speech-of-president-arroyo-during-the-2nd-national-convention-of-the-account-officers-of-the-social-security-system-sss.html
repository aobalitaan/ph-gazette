Speech of President Arroyo during the 2nd National Convention of the Account Officers of the Social Security System (SSS)

April 18, 2007

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the 2nd National Convention of the Account Officers of the Social Security System (SSS)

[Delivered at the Quezon Ballroom, Great Easter Hotel, Quezon City, April 18, 2007]

Thank you. Thank you.

Thank you very much, Chairman Cunanan. And I’d like to especially thank you because when last September I asked you to propose and work for the ten percent increase in benefits for the SSS members, right after that the SSS board approved it. Thank you very much for that.

Thank you also and congratulations! To Cora Dela Paz. Because under your very, very disciplined financial reforms in the SSS and making sure that investments of SSS are all sound, objective, rational, no behest investments, the actuarial life of SSS has improved from 2015 to 2038. Congratulations!

Vice Mayor Herbert Bautista, maraming salamat for welcoming us here in behalf of the big city of Quezon City which is one-half of Metro Manila and I’m sure many SSS members are residents of your city.

Gusto kong batiin ang ating mga commissioners, members of the board, yung kayong lahat mga account officers ng Social Security System. Congratulations! On your second national convention and congratulations! On the 50th anniversary of SSS.

Kanina tinatanong ko kay Cora yung specific job functions ng account officers. Of course, we are all aware of what account officers do. There are account officers in banks. There are account officers in insurance companies, real estate companies, sales companies, and I wanted to see how the general function of an account officer is translated into what SSS account officers do. And from her description and her answers to my probing questions about what you do, I can gather that this is a gathering of the best and brightest in the SSS. You are the committed and dedicated foot soldiers of the institution. You spearhead collections. You spearhead good governance. Thank you for your sterling services to the people.

Bakit sabi nating good collection and good governance. Kasi dahil sa pag-collect n’yo ng magaling even if we don’t have to increase contributions by 10 percent we can have increase contribution… Increase benefits by 10 percent dahil sa magandang koleksyon ninyo gumaganda ang fiscal position ng SSS. And therefore that translates into good governance.

Pag kailangan ng konting increase ng kontribusyon during our administration dahil gusto pa natin mag-recover ang ating working class sa mga nakaraang kahirapan ang nagsasagot sa employees contribution ay yung ating mga employer. Kaya maraming salamat sa ating Employers Confederation of the Philippines, the Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry. Sila ay may kinatawan sa Board of Commissioners ng SSS and these patriotic organizations spearheaded the move of the employers to shoulder whatever increase in contribution rates occurred in 2003 and this year. Thank you too to the employers.

Kung titignan natin kung ano na ang nangyari sa SSS in the past six years — and now that I have a chance to speak with you — I can’t help but remember baseline situation in 2001, Sus! SSS was faced by the threat of collapse by 2015. Steep challenges that took a heavy toll on its actuarial standing. Yun na nga hindi n’yo kasalanan, nagko-collect kayo ng magaling, ang problema yung collections napupunta sa mga investments na hindi karapat-dapat.

But Cora changed that. We embarked on a radical reform program and after six years your institution is more than back to its feet. It is stronger and more resilient than ever. What a great gift for the 50th anniversary of SSS.

And if we look at the world around us, your accomplishments are even more deserving of praise in the face of emergent global crisis in social security programs that are in dire straits in other countries, while ours, yours, and our peoples is sailing steady on an even keel.

The reform of the SSS that started greatly with Cora’s administration and added on in a couple of years by the policy leadership of Thelmo Cunanan and our board, these were part of the hard-fought economic reform battles through which the Philippines has finally broken loose from its historic legacy of lethargy. Everybody talks about the tax reforms. It’s time that everybody knows also about the SSS reforms. When we talk about new revenues, we include increases in contributions for SSS. But not only from those small increases in contribution rates that the employers have agreed to shoulder in behalf of the workers, but your better collection and overall in our country a crackdown on tax cheats, but in your case also a crackdown on those who are cheating on their remittances. And these together with the fiscal discipline, fiscal discipline in our national budget where the money goes to where it should be in a very directed focus, but also fiscal discipline on the part of SSS making sure that the investments of SSS are sound and rational. All these have put the Philippines on a path to permanent, sustainable economic growth and stability. Yun naman ang kaibahan ng kaunlaran sa ilalim ng ating administrasyon. Six years, 24 quarters of uninterrupted growth. Nung araw kasi boom and bust. Yung boom and bust every three years akyat-baba. Ngayon tayo akyat-akyat-akyat. At ang laki ng papel ng SSS, ang reforms at yung inyong mga collection. And the world is taking notice. The Philippines offers one of the best values in Asia for investors.

There has been a significant increase in investments, exports. Sa kabila na ang lakas ng piso natin tuloy pa rin tumataas ang ating exports and job creation. Our unemployment rate has gone done. And so I ask you, let’s continue to work to ensure that this positive momentum will be sustained. As part of that effort, we are committed to consolidating the gains in new revenue by making long overdue investments in human and physical infrastructure. This includes billions of pesos for education, health care and training — at marami dito ay tinutulungan yung mga beneficiaries ng SSS — along with additional billions of pesos for new bridges, roads and ports to upgrade the competitiveness of the Philippines.

I really take great pride in the discipline of the stalwarts and the soldiers, including yourselves, of our administration. We are very disciplined in our focus on the economy and our overall economic health. And I’m proud of the fact that because of the improving finances of our national government and our financial institutions like SSS, external debt is declining at a rapid clip. We have repaid… Prepaid so many of our loans. We’re committed to making sure we use the new resources these developments are generating to invest in vital social and economic needs. At kayo ang dami niyong ginagawa para doon. It’s been a tough road — I’m sure Cora had a hard time turning around the finances of SSS and lengthening its actuarial life from 2015 to 2038 — but together, all of us, I and the members of our administration long ago made a decision not to sacrifice long-term gains for short-term political expediency.

Yung nalalabi pang mga kahirapan eh kasama yun — nagka-opera nga ang aking asawa — kasama na yun sa pag malubha ang sakit ng isang katawan pag kailangan operahan siyempre magkakaron ng sakit. Halimbawa ngayon nilalagnat pa siya, bahagi yun sa post-operative complications. Nguni’t sa katagalan ang mangyayari ay sustained recovery. And that is what has been done with SSS, that is what has been done with the national government.

As a result of the SSS reform program, we’re really very happy and we must be proud that the actuarial life has been extended from 2015 to 2038. Malaking bagay yung inyong collections at I hope, inaasahan ko, na yung condonation program na dineklara natin nung May 1 nung isang taon ay nakatulong. I hope it enabled you to collect even more. In fact you’ve collected 1.7 billion pesos in delinquent accounts. Congratulations! To you for that. And because you were able to collect all of these together with our incentive that they would have some condonation or penalties, you have brought back, I understand, 200,000 SSS members to good standing. That is congratulations! To our account officers. And because of that the SSS last year, last September, was able to grant a 10 percent increase in pensions benefiting more than one million members.

Ngayon 50th anniversary ninyo. Kaya ang aking instructions muli, ang aking request sa ating board, ay maybe let’s gift the Filipino people with another 10 percent increase in benefits this year. This is social payback. Gaya nang sabi ni cora kanina, you the SSS are a fulcrum for social justice.

You are also the medium of industrial peace. Narito ang ating kinatawan ng mga manggagawa. SSS is a part of a mechanism that mitigates the financial burden of workers — basta in good standing, nakakahiram, SSS salary loans — encourages the overall productivity in the workplace and forges harmony in the economy.

But even as we celebrate our 50 years and the six years of hard-fought reforms within the institution that are now paying off for our people, we still realize that new social security schemes are imperative as we modernize our economy.

As we enter the 20th century, as SSS enters its second 50 years and so I ask SSS, the account officers, the different market developers to focus on the coverage for instance of our OFWs. Kaya sabi sa akin kanina ni Chairman Thelmo Cunanan, we are now going to focus on our overseas Filipinos in America as a trial or a pilot so that we can increase the coverage of SSS. Isa pa yung mga self-employed workers. Yun din kasi hindi naman automatic. Hindi kagaya ng korporasyon na ilalim sa batas kailangan kasama sa SSS. Self-employed workers are a very, very big source also for coverage. And we were discussing that with Cora while we were here, that’s one way by which you can increase the market when you go to the community, when you talk to people, when you do your collections you can also identify new SMEs or even micro-enterprises that may want to cover themselves with SSS, self-employed workers and those who belong to the underground economy. Parang talagang insurance salesman naghahanap ng mga bagong kliyente.

And SSS also must broaden its involvement in programs that have a direct impact on the lives and welfare of the poor, such as housing kasi yung housing loans napakalaking bagay yun para sa ating SSS members. And we are doing our best in order to finance the construction of housing, so that the SSS members can also finance their own amortizations and interest payments. Kaya yun naman isang tulong para sa housing sector ay… Yung housing industry they are now… And the economy, the whole economy is now enjoying the lowest interest rates in history, ‘di ba, single digit. Meron pa ngang mga prime accounts diyan three percent lang ang kinukubra sa kanila. And so you have the real estate firms putting up housing and you have SSS financing the servicing or the take out of the members of the SSS.

And therefore we have these continuing challenges. But if you have gone through the challenge of improving collections and improving the investments — those were great challenges that were almost for awhile seemingly insurmountable — I’m sure these challenges now of widening your market, improving the benefits and getting into additional investment areas or more investment areas like microfinance that should be much easier than what we have done, what you have done over the last six years. So kung ano pa itong mga hamon ay hamon ng kung paano mas gumaling pa ang SSS. Hindi na hamon kung papaano mabubuhay ang SSS, buhay na buhay na ang SSS dahil sa inyo.

And so as far as the remaining challenges are concerned which are challenges of modernization and getting into the new century and spreading the benefits to a bigger population of our people, I am confident that you are up to the challenge because you have been up to even more difficult challenges in the last six years. And so on this your second national convention, on this year your 50th anniversary my message is: let us sustain our gains, the gains that you brought about in the last six years. And let us build on them in the next many years for the brighter future of the Filipino.

Mabuhay ang account officers ng SSS!

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
