Speech of President Arroyo during the meeting with the Filipino Community

August 31, 2009

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the meeting with the Filipino Community

[Delivered at the Golden Tent Hall, Tripoli, Libya, August 31, 2009]

Maraming salamat Secretary Roque sa iyong pagpapakilala sa akin.

At siguro kilala na rin niyo si Secretary Bert Romulo ang ating Secretary of Foreign Affairs; si Secretary Cerge Remonde ang ating Press Secretary; si Secretary Bert Gonzales ang ating National Security Adviser; saka si Ambassador Paeng Seguis na siya ang Chairman ng ating Peace Panel na nakikipag-usap sa MILF.

Gusto kong batiin si Pangulong Makabinta at ang buong Philippine community, pati na rin kay Ambassador Vicente — welcome to Libya from being Consul General in Hongkong — and Mr. Jamal, our friend from Libya who’s been taking good care of us since we arrived here today. Pwede na kayong maka-upo. Sa inyong lahat, maraming salamat.

Congratulations sa ating mga graduates! At doon sa iba maraming salamat sa inyong pagdalo dito. King kabalen kung Capampangan, dacal a salamat king pamanyatang yu ngening bengi. Sa mga kaigsuonan gikan sa Visayas ug Mindanao, daghan salamat sa pag-anhi. Dagiti kakabsat nga Ilocano, agyaman ti … tatta nga rabii. Sa mga kasimanwa, salamat gid nga ari kamo subong.

At gusto kong sabihin sa inyo, siguro alam naman ninyo ito, sa nadidinig ko sa mga tiga rito, saludo sa inyo ang lahat ng mga taga dito sa Libya. Congratulations!

I’m happy to learn that companies in Libya compete with the Filipinos working for them whether as nurses in Libyan hospital — congratulations! sa mga nasa Medical Center — or engineers building the towers of Tripoli, our mechanics manning the oil fields in the desert, at yung iba-iba niyong mga trabaho rito, my hats off to the Filipino workers in Libya.

Talagang hindi matatawaran ang sipag at kalidad ng inyong trabaho, gayundin ang mabubuting asal at katapatan ninyo sa tungkulin. Kaya kahit narito si Ambassador Vicente kayo ang mga tunay na embahador ng Pilipinas sa Libya. At hindi lamang ito kung hindi lalong matimbang kayo sa ating bansa — pinagdadarangal at pinapahalagahan ang inyong sakripisyo at katapatan sa inyong trabaho, sa inyong pamilya at sa ating bansa.

Gaya na nang sabi ni Ambassador Vicente parati kong sinasabi nasa hirap at ginhawa pinapatatag ninyong mga OFW ang ating bansa. Yung padala niyong 16 billion dollars mga OFWs sa buong mundo nung isang taon kahit na nagkaroon na ng economic crisis yun ay record sa kasaysayan ng ating bansa. Pero itong taon mas mataas pa ang pinapadala ninyo kaya maraming salamat.

At alam ko na ito ay nanggagaling sa sakripisyo. It is not a sacrifice joyfully borne, it is lonely work, very hard work. Although here in Libya, I hear it is not as lonely for some of you especially those who are working in the oil fields compared to other countries because many of you have been allowed to bring your family with you, hindi ba? Isang malaking bentaha yan hambing sa mga OFW ng ibang bansa. And this way you’re even able to look after your family members yourselves.

Hindi ako nag-aatubiling bisitahin ang ating taong bayan at inyong mga host sa buong mundo mula sa Middle East hanggang sa American Midwest gaya ng Chicago, mula Libya hanggang London nakikinig sa inyong mga problema at pangangailangan. Inaalam kung papano kayo matulungan ng ating pamahalaan by working out better policies on migrant labor or by saving lives and restoring liberties.

And I’m so glad that here in Libya there are not too many problems with the law. And I don’t have to come here to ask for salvation from the death penalty for any Filipino worker. My visit here this time, kasi meron sa inyo nakilala ko na nung nag state visit ako ng 2006, but this time my visit has given me the opportunity, to renew the opportunity to engage the leaders of Africa who are important to our country. We were invited by Libya, by the chairman of the African Union to come to their summit and also to the 40th Anniversary tomorrow of the Libyan Revolution.

Africa is becoming more important, more and more important to the Philippines. We are directly affected, our energy security is directly affected by the peace and order in Africa. But even more than that the safety and welfare of many of our Overseas Filipinos are also affected by the peace and order of Africa especially our seafarers. Malapit nang matapos ang East African monsoon, so we feel a surge of pirate attacks of the coast of Somalia where many of our seafarers work on board the ships that pass through the Suez Canal and the Gulf of Aden.

Bagamat marami na ang pinakawalaan mahigit dalawang daan Filipino seamen ang nagdusa sa kamay ng mga pirata kaya masigasig tayong nagsisikap para proteksyunan ang ating mga seafarer. This is one issue where it is crucial for Africa to work together to bring peace and order in the affected areas of the Coast of Somalia.

Earlier today I had the opportunity to speak with the President of Somalia. He is also worried, he is also concerned about the terrorrists who harass the boats that pass through the Suez Canal and the Gulf of Aden of the Coast of Somalia. And he realizes how important it is to us because we are the number one seafaring people in the world.

So, one way by which we can help to address the problem of peace and order in Somalia is by institution-building. Halimbawa, palakasin yung kanilang civil service, palakasin yung kanilang coast guard, at alam naman ninyo ang mga Pilipino eksperto sa mga ganung bagay, yung mga human resource development. Kaya dahil mahalaga ito sa ating mga seafarer ang pinag-usapan namin ng pangulo ng Somalia kanina ay tutulong ang mga Pilipino sa pag training sa kanilang mga civil servants. Ikabubuti ng Somalia at ikabubuti din ng ating mga Pilipino.

On the part of Libya, Libya has always been standing by us. During my administration, Libya’s help has been so big in bringing peace and hope to the Southern Philippines. And also, in helping to strengthen and form a deeper relation with the other Muslim countries. (applause) Together with Brunei and Japan, Libya monitors our ceasefires with the MILF.

And I am glad to see some of the students who are studying in the Islamic Call Society because that is a very important institution for us to teach the real Islam, the Islam that loves peace and abhors hatred. And it is our honor that during our administration we were able to accord the Libyan Islamic Call Society diplomatic status (applause) to make it easier for us to work together for peace and development.

Libya’s role in our pecae is also important because of the connection of peace to development. As we improve the security of the parts of the country that have known some degree of violence — and we are doing this together with the help of the international community, kasama na nga ang Libya — mas mabilis tayong kumilos para magdala ng kaunlaran sa mga lugar na yun na nung araw ay marami ang labanan. Marahil yun din ang isang dahilan kung bakit isa tayo sa iilan lamang mga bansa sa buong mundo na nakakaraos sa kasalukuyang world economic recession at tuloy pa rin na positive ang ating growth rate.

Itong pandaigdigang krisis ng ekonomiya ang isang naipakita ay yung ating ekonomiya ay matatag at malakas, mababa ang inflation, at handa na tayo para sa economic rebound. Yung ating mga business outsourcing at call centers tuloy-tuloy ang kanilang boom. Yung tourism, kausap ko lang si Secretary Durano kanina sa telepono, umaakyat yung ating manufacturing sector, umaangat na naman muli pagkatapos nang bumaba nung panahon ng world economic crisis. Yung mga bagong construction tuloy-tuloy walang tigil. Yung ating mga hotel punong-puno ng mga negosyante mula sa buong mundo.

Kaya tayo ay bullish para sa ating prospects for a solid recovery. At ito ay importante dahil nagsisikap tayong lumikha sa ating bansa ng mga trabahong maganda ang kita para sa ganun kayo overseas work will just be a career choice and not the only option for a hardworking Filipino.

Samantala, sa ating mga nag graduate ngayon at sa mga organisasyon na tumutulong dito sa programa gusto kong magpasalamat sa inyong lahat na mataas ang tingin sa Pilipinas dito sa bansang Libya dahil pinapakita ninyo ang excellence ng great Filipino worker.

Kaya sa inyong lahat, congratulations! More power to you!

Maraming, maraming salamat.
