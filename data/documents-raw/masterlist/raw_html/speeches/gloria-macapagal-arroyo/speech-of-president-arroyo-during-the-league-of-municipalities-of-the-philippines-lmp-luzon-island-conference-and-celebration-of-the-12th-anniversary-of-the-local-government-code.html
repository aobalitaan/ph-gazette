Speech of President Arroyo during the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP) Luzon Island Conference and celebration of the 12th anniversary of the Local Government Code

October 9, 2003

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP) Luzon Island Conference and celebration of the 12th anniversary of the Local Government Code

[Delivered at the Grand Ballroom, Westin Philippine Plaza, Roxas Blvd., Pasay City, October 9, 2003]

Maraming salamat.

Maraming salamat sa inyong napakainit na pagbati sa akin, mga punongbayan ng buong Luzon. Maraming salamat, Secretary Joey Lina sa iyung magandang pagpakilala sa akin na naalala sana ng ating mga punongbayan ng Luzon kung papaano natin kamahal ang mga punongbayan ng Pilipinas.

Dios ti agngina, apo Mayor Ramon Guico ken ti naduma nga mayors ti amianan. Agyamanak ti… Ay, nagpapasalamat ako hindi lamang sa mga mayors ng norte, sa mayors ng buong Pilipinas, ang mayors league, sa napakagandang resolusyon ninyo na nagsusuporta sa aking desisyon na sumali sa halalan sa susunod na taon.

Yes, I thank the league of municipalities of the Philippines for that resolution. Maraming salamat din Governor Dolfo del Rosario na sa ngalan ng buong ULAP ganun din sinusuportahan ninyo ang aking desisyon. And thank you for pointing out the twelve gains of the union of local authorities of the Philippines, but these twelve gains happened in the two and a half years of my administration.

Thank you very much, mayor mayor — I always call him that, mayor Mayor — for that resolution that you have attested to. I am very grateful for that. And I want to greet also our other officers of ulap who are here such as Governor Louie Villafuerte. Matagal na niyang pinipilit akong gumawa ng desisyon. Minsan, nagdinner kami sabi, mag-decide ka na. Ako silent, speaks like silent. Nakikita ko yung frustration ni Louie. Kaya kanina nung binati ko siya, sabi ko, ano, Louie masaya ka na? Sabi niya, masaya na raw siya.

Salamat rin kay Mayor Francis Tolentino kumakatawan ng League of Cities. Agad-agad nag-text siya sa akin na sinusuporta niya at ang mga cities yung aking desisyon. At kung nandito si James Marty Lim — ay, there you are. How could I miss such a handsome face — at salamat din, thank you for the support of the barangay officials. At lahat lahat ng dumalo dito sa Luzon island Conference — caring Cabalen cung Capampangan, mayap a bengi mga taga-Tagalog, mga taga-Bicol, marhay na banggi sa indo gabos, salamat sa inyong suporta.

Yung aking desisyon binase ko sa ating pambansang interes ganun na rin sa interes ng inyong mga constituencies. Kayong mga incumbents kayo, kayo alam ninyo ang halaga ng karanasan.

And in the more than two years of my presidency, I have gained the experience necessary to understand what we need to do in order to change society in a way that leads to economic development and the elimination of poverty.

Itong nakaraang anim na buwan, doon nga nagkukumpara kami ng growth rates sa ASEAN kung saan ako nanggaling. We achieved 4.8 percent GNP growth — this is the highest in our country since the 1997 Asian crisis. And siguro naman sa maraming bayan ninyo nararamdaman ninyo, in so short a time of two years, we accomplished one of the most massive housing programs undertaken by any administration. At siguro sa inyong mga palengke nakikita rin ninyo, we’ve been able to keep the supply and the price of food stable. We confronted our deep national divisions face to face and implemented painful court rulings like the arrest of Estrada and Misuari. We won victories in pinning down corrupt high-ranking officials, some of them on the basis of unprecedented lifestyle checks. Even my family is not above the law.

Nabanggit ni Monching yung ating all-out war sa illegal drugs. At doon sa nakaraang tatlong buwan, pagkatapos kami ni Joey gumawa ng particular na all-out war, yung mga nakumpiskang illegal drugs sa tatlong buwan, mga 8 billion pesos worth, spending less than 100 million of enforcement money — unprecedented achievements. Iyung bilang ng mga drug dealers, kaya punong-puno na ngayon ang ating preso, far outstrip those made in several years before. At yung mga big fish na nahuli diyan, yang mga triad, big fish na noong araw sila ay mga untouchable, touchable na ngayon.

We have sought a peaceful resolution to the Muslim insurgency, and we are well on our way to signing a permanent peace pact. We cut red tape to make sure crucial infrastructure are built. Halimbawa, yung roll-on/roll-off ferry na umaabot sa mindoro at lumalampas pa sa Mindoro. Dumadaan na ngayon iyung mga bakasyonista sa Boracay sa Mindoro island and other areas. New bridges — ilan sa inyo noong dumaan ako ay nagpasalamat dahil sa matagal nang hinihintay na tulay na ngayon ay nagawa. New highways, and also because among other things, your leagues passed formal resolutions for changes in the Constitution. I listen to you and we began meaningful debates on those changes in the constitution, particularly on the parliamentary and federal form of government.

Kagabi, halos hatinggabi, dumating ako sa Cebu mula sa ASEAN Summit sa bali. Doon sa bali pinirmahan ng mga leaders ng ASEAN ang Bali Concord II. Ipinangalan ito sa unang Bali Concord noong 1976 na siya ang nagtayo ng unang ASEAN. Eh ngayon, many years later, bago na ang mundo kaya gumawa ng bagong Bali Concord. At dito ay nagkasunduan na tutulakin ang economic integration, papalawakin ang pangangalakal at ipag-iibayo, we will consolidate among the various countries in ASEAN our vigilance against terrorism. Kagaya rin ng inyong kasunduan doon sa inyong peace, unity and democracy na lalabanan ninyo ang terorismo.

Bukod doon sa Bali Concord II, China and India also acceded to a treaty with ASEAN, a treaty of amity and cooperation in Southeast Asia. These are key developments and — nandito yung ating hybrid rice winner — ang China ang pinanggalingan ng hybrid rice na nakadoble nang ani ng marami sa inyong mga constituents dito — ayun, nagpapalakpak si Cito Lorenzo, siya ang nagdala ng hybrid rice dito.

Yung India naman, dalawang daang milyon na ngayon ang milyonaryo sa india in a population of one billion. Eh tayo sa marami sa inyong mga bayan mayroon kayong malaking indian community, and many of them still hold indian citizenship, at tayo bumibili ng gamot sa India. Yung mga programa ninyo na I hope ginagamit ninyo yung murang gamot na half-priced galing yon sa India. Kaya kailangan, sabi ko naman sa Prime Minister ng India, ang dami naming binibiling gamot sa inyo dapat we are in the radar screen of each other. Sabi naman ng Prime Minister of India, he is giving our airline, our designated national airline landing rights unilaterally in several key cities in India. Kaya yung mga indians sa inyong community, marami ding Indian citizens pa, alukin n’yo sila na ipalawak ang pangangalakal sa kanilang mother country dahil mayroon silang dalawang daang milyong milyonaryo doon, mas malaki pa kaysa sa buong populasyon ng Pilipinas. Because in the end, whatever I negotiate it will be you, the frontliners, who will translate the gains we make in foreign policy into the momentum and effectiveness in our common battle against poverty, against terrorism, against criminality.

We are all in the executive branch. You are in that sense extensions of presidential authority in the communities. As I am chief executive of our nation; you are the chief executives of your municipalities. Together we share a determination to deliver the best of governance. But also together, we must overcome the obstacles to our mission.

Our historic problem has been the need for economic development to fight poverty.

Historically, our economic development has been slow compared to our neighbors. Mabuti na lamang na sa nakaraang dalawang taon at kalahati ng ating administrasyon, during the last two years and a half of our administration, we did better than our neighbors. Because our neighbors suffered very much in the aftermath of the Asian crisis but as an economist I knew that if I don’t build up domestic demand we will suffer too. How did I build up domestic demand? I build up demand in the provinces, and now you know why I released all that IRA even the debts of the previous administration.

Kaya, oo, dahil mahal ko kayo, pero mahal din kayo ng ating bansa, and it worked. Yung mga ginamit n’yong utang na binayaran ko, utang ng nakaraan, ginamit n’yo para sa kaunlaran sa inyong mga bayan that’s why we performed better than our neighbors in the last two and a half years. Kaya lang ngayon nagsisimula nang mag-recover ang ating rehiyon. Naku! Nakakatakot, nagko-compare kami ng growth rates. Our neighbors are starting to accelerate their growth again, and this time we cannot afford to be left behind.

Our historic economic problems cannot be blamed on any single president, because the problems are fundamental, caused by our economic and political system. Kaya nasisiyahan ako doon sa sinabi ni Monching na naintindihan n’yo, naunawaan n’yo nung sinabi ko yung aking desisyon. I said and you agreed in your resolution that to really develop our economy and create a stronger republic, we need to change our system. We need to institute true reforms in our politics and true reforms in our economy. And I am glad that in your covenant you have peace, democracy and unity, because we also need unity as a people.

We need to heal the deep divisions within our society. We must have justice and we have gotten much justice in this administration. Many cases waiting for decades have been resolved in our administration. But we must also have reconciliation. We must put a closure to our national divisions. Because in the final analysis, as you have said in your resolution, it will be reform and reconciliation that will heal the wounds of our divisiveness.

You’ve always been on my mind since the day I assumed the presidency. And because even then I had that idea that we need reconciliation, napatatag lang ang aking paniniwala sa aking karanasan. I also looked for the causes of division between the national government and the local government. At ang nakikita ko noon, Senador pa ako, na pinanggagalingan ng paghihiwalay sa pananaw ng national at local government ay may tendency ang national government na pakialaman ang autonomy ng local government. Kaya naman binanggit ni Dolfo, binanggit ni Joey, isa sa unang ginawa ko sa aking administrasyon was the recall of the executive orders which placed a yoke on your fiscal autonomy.

Labindalawa yon, hindi ko na ililista lahat. Nilista na ni Dolfo at inilaborate ang ilan ni Joey. But lately, I talked about the first, I’ll just talked about the last major one.

I have released all your last local government service equalization fund. Bayad na ako sa utang ng national government sa inyo. At gaya nang sabi ng dalawa, walang bawas at lumaki pa ang inyong shares ng internal revenue allotment. Ang hinihingi ko lamang sa inyo dahil napakaraming nawalang trabaho sa nakaraang El Nino at mga bagyo, sana sa inyong LGSEF release gamitin ninyo ang isang milyong piso bawat bayan para sa microfinance ng inyong bayan. Sa ganun mabibigyan natin ng ginhawa ang maraming walang trabaho sa ating kanayunan.

Sad to say, the political season is here.

But because our neighbors are starting to accelerate their growth, and we can’t afford to be left behind, we must together give — even as the political season is on — we must together give greater certainty to business and investors. We must together show them that our programs are devoted to strengthening the economy, creating more jobs, and encouraging business activity unhampered by corruption and red tape in government.

Sa inyong mga meeting over these last two and a half years sa mga league meetings ninyo mayroon kayong mga best practices sharing ng pagbawas ng red tape — yung mga one-stop shop, yung mga… ang dami, ang dami n’yong mga ginagawa yung mga mabilis na proseso. And I ask you to stay on track with that. I ask you to stay on track in the task of governance as I am keeping a steady hand over the concerns of the state. Let us also ask our constituents, your constituents to keep their eyes focused on the challenges we face daily. Sa pang-araw-araw kung minsan may krimen, sa pang-araw-araw mayroon diyang mga drug lords pa rin, mga pang-araw-araw na mga hamon. O kaya nagkaroon ng baha at hindi nakaani, daily challenges we have to face them daily. And let us not allow ruthless politics to distract us all from the all important charge of nation-building.

And in this all important charge of nation-building, I would like to assure you that as your president, I will always be — as the twelve accomplishments have shown — I will always be your champion for local autonomy.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
