Speech of President Arroyo during the National Nutrition and Gawad Saka Awarding Ceremonies

October 15, 2009

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the National Nutrition and Gawad Saka Awarding Ceremonies

[Delivered at the Reception Hall, PICC, Pasay City, October 15, 2009]

Thank you very much maraming salamat Secretary Pingkoy Duque, Chairman ng National Nutrition Council at host nitong ating gawain ngayon. I’d like also to greet the members of the Diplomatic Corps and congratulate Secretary Artee Yap, also the officials of the National Nutrition Council and the agriculture department as well as the Landbank of the Philippines; our recipients of the different awards congratulations sa inyong lahat! Mainit na pagbati sa mga tumanggap ng Gawad Saka at Nutrition awards.

Hiniling ko na makasama itong dalawang pangyayari ang gawad saka at nutrition awards dahil sila ay kapwa importante para sa pagkain ng ating bayan.

At sa pamamagitan ng mga parangal na binibigay ngayon, ipinapakita na maraming mahuhusay na Pilipinong opisyal, volunteer, magsasaka, nutrition worker na dapat tularan sa kanilang kahandaang tumulong upang maiwasan natin ang gutom at mapalakas ang ating laban sa kahirapan.

At isa pa yung kaninang gagawain sana pero iba yung nasa TV napakaimportante baka nanunuod kayo ng video iba naman ang ginagawa dito ngayon ay gagawain ko na rin. Hiningi kong isama sa ating selebrasyon ang isa pang pangatlong pangyayari—the P150 billionth na pautang ng Land Bank of the Philippines sa ating mga magsasaka at mangingisda sa loob ng ating administrasyon. So, now I would like to ask the Land Bank and to call on our recipient of the 150 billionth peso loaned out during our administration. So ito ay 50 million pesos para sa Cooperative Rural Bank of Bulacan. You are receiving the 150 billion peso of loans from the Land Bank during our administration, congratulations!

Tapos nun itong 50 million na pinahiram natin sa Cooperative Rural Bank ng Bulacan ay ipapahiram naman sa ating mga magsasakang humihiram sa Rural Bank eto na ang tseke sa kanila. So congratulations sa Land Bank congratulations sa Cooperative Rural Bank of Baulacan at congratulations sa mga magsasakang tumanggap ng lending! 150 billion pesos ganun na karami ang naitulong ng Land Bank, sa ating admninstrasyon at ako ay nasisiyahan na ganito kalaki ang naitutulong ng Land Bank dahil and Land Bank ay parang kapatid ko dahil sa inilkha sa panahon ng aking ama, ang dating Pangulong Diosdado Macapagal, na siya ang ama ng land reform.

My father, President Diosdado Macapagal, wrote in his memoirs his conviction that the basis for achieving equitable prosperity is: to release the impoverished tenant farmers from bondage. He fought an uphill battle against several landlords to push his land reform programs, walong special sessions ang ginawa niya dahil hindi maipasa pasa yung programa tinitigil.. pumasa sa Congress, tinitigil ng mga landlords doon sa senado, but after eight special sessions, nakuha rin niya yung Republic Act 3844 or the Agricultural Land Reform Code and that included the creation of the Land Bank, the primary conduit of agricultural loans for our farmer sector.

Matagal na yung land reform 1964, 1963 siguro nakalilimutan na nain yung kalagayan ng mga magsasaka bago nagkaron ng land reform. Bago nagkaroon ng reporma sa lupa, ang umiiral na sistema ay share tenancy. Yung mag magsasaka ang tawag ay kasama– they share may kasama yung landlord sa kanilang ani. Malaki ang share ang landlord sa kanilang ani may porsyento malaking porsyento kaya tawag sa kanila kasama. At yung landlord din ang pinanggagagalingan ng pautang na mataas ang interes, tapos babayaran pagdating ng ani kaya pagdating ng ani halos wala nang naiiwan sa magsasaka except for his own subsistence. Kaya walang insentibo o kakayahan ang kasama na mamuhunan upang tumaas ang kaniyang produktibidad.

Ngunit sa Republic Act 3844, tinanggal ang share tenancy. Sa ganoon, nagkaroon ng dalwang programa yung isa– bilhin ng pamahalan ang sakahan para ibenta sa magsasaka ng mga magaang mga terms and conditions o kaya yung share tenancy magiging lease tenancy babayad na lang ng fixed rent para lahat ng kita ay doon sa mgasasakamismo, hindi magsheshare bawat tumaas ng produktibidad me share parati yung landlord. At para hindi na kailangan parating sa landlord umaasa ng pahiram doon nga tinayo ang Land Bank para mapahiram sa ating mga magsasaka.

The ultimate goal of land reform is to fight poverty. By providing the incentive and the means for the farmers to be more productive, kaya malaki rin ang kanyang papel sa food security at hunger mitigation na importanteng ipinagdidiriwang natin dito. Yung mga nabigyan ng premyo dahil yun ang pangunahing trabaho ngayon special task ng Nutrition Council labanan ang kahirapan sa pamamagitan ng labanan ang pagkagutom.

And as I have said since teo years ago when we gave this task to the Nutrition Council we approached the problem of hunger and food security from to sides– demand and supply.

On the supply side nga, malaki yung papel ng mga magsasaka at saka yung land reform– increase the production of food; bawasan ang wastage at spoilage; kagaya nung binanggit ni Artee Yap sa kanyang diskurso meron tayong mag binigyan ng premyo yung barangay bagsakan mga ganung bagay that’s to provide effective delivery systems for farm products to the consumers, pati yung RORO yung ating mayor ng Roxas ay nandito at malaki ang inangat ng ekonomiya ng Roxas dahil sa ating RORO. At saka yung mga driers … para hindi nilalagay sa kalsada yung drying at yun ang isang malaking programa na ginagawa ni Artee ngayon bilang rehabilitation ng ating mga magsasaka yung driers dahil maraming nabasa nga mga produkto.

On the demand side, we aim to increase the ability to buy food by increasing incomes; by educating our people on proper nutrition, kaya trabaho ng Nutrition Council yun nuon pa including I want to remind Bernie information on breastfeeding; because up to six months breastfeeding is the best kind of nutrition, and including managing our population through natural family planning.

Sa demand side at sa supply side, importante ang magsasaka nasa kamay ng magsasaka na iangat ang produksyon at kung ang magsasaka ay maganda ang kanyang kita siya rin ay consumer magkakaron ng mas pagkakataon na makabili ng mga kakanin. Kasi hindi parating pareho yun parati nating pinag aaralan yung ten poorest provinces and ten most food poor provinces. Meron halimbawa Kalinga hindi siya mahirap hindi bahagi ng ten poorest provinces tapos siya pa ring isa sa mga exporters ng rice sa ibang probinsya pero kasama sa ten most food poor provinces. Hindi ko maintindihan bakit kasi yungiabng pagkain ang kulang doon hindi yung bigas so malaki talaga ang farmer mismo ay consumer din at dapat magkaron ng income sa kanyang kamay para mabili niya yung hindi lamang yung kanyang sariling produkto kungid yung iabng produktong kailangan niya para makakain siya.

Ang land reform ay importante sa produktibidad, and during our administration, I am very gratified that we have distributed almost a million hectares of privately owned land under the comprehensive agrarian reform program, over 800,000 hectares of public land for agricultural use, and almost 700,000 hectares of ancestral domain land. All in all 2.5 million hectares more than any other administration. So We have distributed much more land with our record of over 2.5 million hectares of private, public and ancestral domain lands for agriculture. At talagang nalampasan na natin yung ating target dahil yung isang sinabi ko sa SONA we will distribute a 100,000 hectares of land reform per year walong taon lang tayo nandito dapat 800,000 hectares yun nakaabot na ng 2.5 million so that is a SONA commitment that is over complied with.

Ang gusto natin narinig ko kanina the term agribusiness we want to turn our farmers into agribusinessmen. Kaya napakaimportante ang Land Bank pero napakaimportante rin yung panukalang batas na sana magamit ang agricultural land para sa collateral para sa pautang. Because that way our farmers can join the formal credit mainstream and not rely on loan shark or long for the days when they still had to count on their landlords even if they have to harvest to hock their harvest for them.

Naalala ko kasi nandito yung ating mga kaibigan sa Bacolod Talisay yung arian ng pamilya ng aking asawa niland reform natin maaga pa sa ating administrasyon a couple of years ago sa isang pagbisita ko sa Bacolod nakita ko sila di akala ko ang saya saya na nila nadistribute na yung lupa sa kanila … ay hindi kasi ang problema nga nila wala na wala na kami wala nang magpapahiram at hindi naman sila makahiram sa bangko dahil hindi naman magagamit ang kanilang lupa sa collateral. So you see that is very important … a new land reform we must allow land to be used as collateral.

This is part of our efforts in poverty reduction and also in hunger mitigation and I am very happy that these efforts are producing results. Kanina nung pumasok ako yung atingmga magagandang binibini ng Nutrition Council na maganda ang kanilang kimona at patadyong ay binati ako at nalaman ko na dito sa nakaraang bagyo nabaha ang Nutrition Council hanggang sa bewang sabi ko, I’m sorry to hear about that pero congratulations pa rin kasi kahit na nababaha kayo nababa niyo yung incidents of hunger from 20 percent three months ago to 17.5% today kaya congratulations sa inyo!

But speaking of the typhoons napag usapan na ni Artee given the destruction the two recent typhoons in the North Luzon Agribusiness Quadrangle, na marami sa atin nakatanggap ng premyo belong to the agribusiness North Quadrangle nandiyan si governor ng Cagayan, nandyan si governor ng Quirino, nandyan si Governor ng Ilocos Sur they are all producing our food. So we have to guard against food shortage and an increase in hunger. Kaya malaking bagay yun nasusubaybayan na naman ng Nutrition Council yung supply side thru the Department of Agriculture para tiyakin na meron kaagad na produksyon kasi nga maraming nasayang pero magtatanim muli sa Nobyember di ba? Kaya sabi ko kay Artee Yap tiyakin mo na bago All Saint’s Day yung seeds na kailangan paramagtanim muli ay nasa kamay na ng ating magsasaka.

Sabi ni Artee, bastat hindi nabaha ang lugar magmumudmud na kami ng mga seeds. So uunahin yung mga hindi nabaha kasi syempre kung baha pa eh bakit magbibigay ka ng seeds sayang lang hindi pa magagamit so yun ang gagawing timing yun ang gagawing sequence ni Artee yung mas nawalaan ng baha yun ang uunahin na bibigyan ng tulong for seeds.

And I hope that with your support our outstanding farmers we will continue with our reduction in hunger. And I hope that with our outstanding nutrition workers and nutrition government officials we will be able to continue to make sure that on the demand side your constituents will know how to spend their hard earned money to buy the right products for their food and the good of their family.

So I am very happy about the awardees and their accomplishments we thank the hardwork of many of our government agencies and officials. We thank the hardwork of the many selfless individuals like today’s awardees who work hard—you are working hard not for yourselves but for others. You are working to give food to the people of the Philippines.

My late father said: unless the government acted decisively and speedily this is with regard to land reform– the goal of land for the landless would not become a reality.

We continue with our reforms, especially now that we pass thru a global crisis in 2008, a global economic crisis in between 2007 a global food crisis, 2009 a global climate change crisis. We must become more decisive and daring because we know that the world economy will recover and we hope it will be soon. We must be daring because we know that climate does not assure as of food supply that is sufficient every year. And we must be daring we know because as far as climate and weather is concerned, uncertainty is the rule and no longer the season that we can always rely on.

So we must combine the political will of the citizenry and the government and work on our goals together. Only then can we hope to realize our bigger goals of land for the landless, food security, and emancipation from poverty. At yung mga nakatanggap ng mga parangal itong araw na ito nagpapatunay kayo na kaya ng Pilipino magkaisa sa kanyang pamayanan para tiyakin na sapat ang pagkain at umangat ang kita ng ating mga magsasaka at lahat ng mamamayan. Congratulations to all of you!

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
