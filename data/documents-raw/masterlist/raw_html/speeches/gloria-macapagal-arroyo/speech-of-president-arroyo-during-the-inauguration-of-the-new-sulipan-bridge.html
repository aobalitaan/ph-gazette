Speech of President Arroyo during the inauguration of the new Sulipan Bridge

March 17, 2007

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the inauguration of the new Sulipan Bridge

[Delivered in Brgy. Sulipan, Apalit, Pampanga, March 17, 2007]

Maraming salamat.

Maraming salamat, Secretary Manny Bonoan. At congratulations! Sa iyo at sa lahat ng mga DPWH na nagtrabaho rito dahil technically napakahirap gawin itong tulay na ito pero nagawa niyong maganda at matatag.

Ambassador Yamazaki, we thank you and the Japanese government for giving us the loan that we used to fund the construction of this very, very important infrastructure; Congresswoman Anna York Bondoc queng caring kegana-ganang Congressmen Naning fourth district ning Pampanga, peka maragul a proyektong dinalang keti quing distrito na; kaya ara caring nga tao keti qing fourth district keting qing Pampanga iyana kanu ing eldest child ku aleya Mikee. Anyway, I greet also my biological eldest child, Mikey.

And I would like to greet board member Dong Gonzales; our various mayors who are here headed by Apalit Mayor Tirso Lacanilao; the two mayor Floreses of Macabebe and Masantol; Mayor Sagum, also the first gentleman of the district.

And I would like to greet also our Cabinet officers for regional development here in Central Luzon: our chairman of the Clark-Subic alliance, Ed Pamintuan; our Presidential Assistant for Central Luzon, Rene Diaz; let me also greet Hanjin, our principal contractor here, not only because you have built this beautiful bridge, Mr. Shim, but also because you opt your investment on the shipbuilding in Subic which is about already over 250 million dollars and going up to one billion U.S. dollars; caring kabalen kung kapampangan, mayap a oras kekayu uling iko ing.. Iko ing mag-benefit kaniting Sulipan bridge.

And Ambassador Yamazaki, may I tell you something about Sulipan. Pampanga is very well known for its cuisine. But Sulipan has a very special cuisine, it’s called Sulipan cuisine. And why do we have a special cuisine here in Sulipan. You see this beautiful river which incidentally used to be dirty but it’s very clean now, maayap a-oras qing kekatang Mayor Lacanilao. This river goes out into Manila bay. And in the olden times, the galleon ships between Mexico and the Philippines used to enter this river from manila bay and have their drydock and shipyard here in Sulipan. And so therefore in the many months that the galleon ships will be undergoing repair, rehabilitation, maintenance, the chefs, the Spanish and Mexican chefs of the galleon ships would be here and exchange in culinary culture with our own Filipino Kapampangan cooks. And so we have a unique blend of Sulipan cooking made up a combination of Kapampangan, Spanish and Mexican cuisine, in fact, propagated in some of the fine restaurants in Metro Manila by the most famous Filipino chef coming from Sulipan, his name is Gene Gonzalez.

So, just as Sulipan was a very important infrastructure area in the Sspanish period. Today, Sulipan is very important infrastructure area in today’s economy. At alimo kaning… It’s not only the Kkapampangans who are benefiting from this bridge. It is from everybody who will have to travel from Manila to the provinces in the north, yung mga magbabiyahe na hindi kaya magbayad doon sa toll road. Mas mura ito pero maganda, at yung mga truck, sabi nga ni Manny Bonoan, kayang-kaya yung mga mabibigat na karga. Kaya nung araw hindi sila makasakay dito diyan sa yung tulay na yan na maliit, hindi yan kaya nung malalaking truck kaya lahat dadaan sa toll road. Ngayon malalaking truck kaya na ito, hindi na magbabayad ng toll, magiging mas mura tuloy ang mga produktong at pagkain na dumadaan dito.

Kaya congratulations! Sa DPWH dahil nilalagay niyo ang mga mahahalagang imprastraktura naging bunga ng ating mga reporma sa buwis. Itong inagurasyon nating ito ay kasunod ng groundbreaking kahapon lamang doon sa isabela, sa isang malayong barangay para matulungan yung ating mga magsasaka. Itong groundbreaking na ito kasunod din… This also follows the groundbreaking the other day when Ambassador Yamazaki and I presided over a five billion-peso groundbreaking of a Toyota transmission plant. Na ang kanyang mga produkto ibebenta hindi lamang sa Pilipinas kung hindi na rin sa ibang mga bansa kasama ang Japan mismo. At yung groundbreaking na yun kasunod naman doon sa inagurasyon nung isang araw naman ng isang… Yung pinakamodernong pagawaan ng gamot. Yung pagpalawak ng Unilab na magiging pinakamodernong pagawaan ng gamot sa buong rehiyon. Hindi lang yon, yun ang unang magiging pabrika ng gamot na magiging FDA accredited. Ibig sabihin nuon ang Pilipinas makakabenta na ng gamot sa Amerika mismo.

Lahat itong mga investments na ito — Toyota, foreign investment; Hanjin, foreign investment; Unilab, giant Filipino investment — ay dahil sa kumpiyansa sa ating ekonomiya dahil sa ating pagmaneho ng ating budget. The closing budget gap is fueling the confidence that creates jobs. We are speaking of well-paying jobs gaya ng mga welders doon sa Hanjin shipyard. Noong araw mangingisda, subsistence fishermen, ngayon highly-paid welders. Gaya ng mga trabaho sa Toyota transmission, ang daming mga enhinyero, mga technical engineers at iba pang mga technical factory workers. Sa Unilab ang daming mga chemist. All of that is evident… All these high-paying jobs are evident in the finding, na loob ng peryodiko today, that Philippine salaries growth is the third fastest in the Asia-Pacific. And we are proving that good salaries basta nanggagaling sa desisyon ng management at employee, good salaries are not a bar to investment but a guarantee of more profits to the firms that are putting a stake in our country.

No one can beat the Philippines when it comes to fiscal discipline. And the Filipino people are world-class when it comes to productivity and excellence. But economic strength is more than fiscal stability and a good payback to our human resources. We have to pump prime the system. Ano’ng ibig sabihin ng pump priming the system? Hindi ba yung mga lugar kung saan mahina yung pressure ng tubig naglalagay tayo ng pump, binobomba natin. Yun ang ibig sabihin ng pump priming. And we have to pump prime the system and guarantee that its returns are felt in the countryside and grassroots level through better infrastructure gaya ng tulay para sa magsasaka sa isabela at gaya nitong tulay na ito sa sulipan na nasa Mac Arthur highway na nagdadala ng ating mga kababayan mula sa Manila hanggang probinsiya, ating mga produkto mula probinsya hanggang Maynila.

We also have to provide payback in quality education and a healthier people gaya nga ng mga gamot na ginagawa ngayon sa world class, most modern laboratory in the region doon sa Unilab. At yung kahapon naman may apat na kasunduan tungkol sa herbal medicine na binuo sa silver anniversary ng Health Research Council.

At the same time this flow of economic gains must be reinforced by plugging revenue leaks, prosecuting tax cheats and smugglers, and nipping graft in the bud through less red tape and stricter controls on wayward spending. We are keeping pace with the world in all aspects of economic and social stability, shoring up the welfare of the people, expanding democratic space, and hitting hard on the threat of terror.

Itong tulay na ito hindi lang halimbawa ng ating importanteng imprastraktura para sa ekonomiya, halimbawa rin ito ng pagkakalinga sa mahihirap at saka sa demokrasya. Kasi isang dahilan kung bakit matagal na matagal hindi natin mapalitan yang tulay na yan ay maraming mga squaters dito. Eh hindi naman pwede nating basta tabuyin lamang sila. Pero sa ilang mga nakaraang taon nagkaroon tayo ng pera dahil sa ating tax reforms na natulungan silang lumipat. Kaya nung nakalipat sila dahil sa tulong na rin ng ating budget nasimulaan natin itong construction nitong tulay na ito.

Kaya… Kaya, mayap a oras pasibayu caring mahal kung kabalen. At congratulations! Muli sa lahat ng gumawa at lahat ng pilipinong makikinabang sa Sulipan bridge at MacArthur highway.

Dacal a salamat. Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
