Speech of President Arroyo during the closing ceremonies of the Mindanao Island Cluster Conference of the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP)

September 3, 2009

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the closing ceremonies of the Mindanao Island Cluster Conference of the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP)

[Delivered at Hotel Stotsenberg, Clark Freeport Zone, Pampanga, September 3, 2009]

Maraming salamat Mayor Monching sa iyong pagpakilala sa akin at saka sa magandang idea mong nagkakaroon itong mga ibat-ibang mga cluster meetings.

At dakal a salamat naman Mayor Tarzan Lazatin king masanting a pag magwelcome yu kiring kekatang a kaluguran manibat king a Mindanao. And I’d like to congratulate also Mayor Dennis for hosting some of the affairs here, and of course Mayor Abalos and all of the mayors of Mindanao. According to Mayor Abalos some of you it’s the first time you come to Clark, so welcome to my home province of Pampanga.

We are meeting at a very important time in the history of our country when the world has been shaken by global economic crisis. But thank God, we are one of the few nations, out of the two-thirds of the world that went into recession, we are one of the one-third that have withstood the onslaught and kept our growth positive.

Part of the reason is that the bitter and unpopular medicine of economic reform that we did years ago — thanks to the help of our congressman like Congressman Tarzan Lazatin — helped to create a firewall that softened the pressures arising from the global economic downturn. Hindi ba madalas may sunog sa mga probinsya pati sa Manila, kung dikit-dikit yung mga barong-barong lahat nasusuno, pero kung meron kayong firewall hindi umaabot sa kabila ng pader yung sunog. And our economic reforms caused that firewall. That is why we were able to maintain positive economic growth not only during this global economic downturn when the world two-thirds went into recession but for 34 uninterrupted quarters of our administration.

Yung sinasabi natin noon na palalakasin ang ating economy nung mga tax reforms natin napatunayan dito sa recession because the world recession showed our economy is stable and strong. Yung growth natin sa second quarter of this year mas mabuti pa keysa sa growth ng first quarter. At yung ating inflation isa sa pinakamababa, halos zero ang ating inflation ngayon, sa buong kasaysayan ng ating bansa. So, we are ready for the economic rebound. And our macroeconomic stability is a major strength that will put us in a stronger position as an attractive investment and business destination.

Halimbawa, kahit na sa Mindanao umabot na yung ating mga business processing and call centers, doon sa Davao meron na, sa Cagayan de Oro. Our business process outsourcing and call center industry continues to boom. Our tourism is way up. Our manufacturing sector na bumaba nung recession it’s picking up again. New construction continues unabated, kung dumaan kayo sa Metro Manila panay makikita niyo mga cranes, construction cranes, and our businessmen… our hotels are full of businessmen from around the world. So, overall I am quite bullish about our prospects for solid recovery.

Imagine two-thirds of the world went into a recession, tayo hindi, hindi lang ito nagkaroon pa tayo ng credit upgrade. At siguro dapat maalala natin ang situation ngayon compared to 2001 nung nagsimula lang tayo. Kasi ngayon kapaligiran natin marami nang naghihirap na bansa tayo umaangat. Nung 2001, Asia was surging but the Philippines was on the verge of bankruptcy. And do you remember before our administration you could not even get your IRA paid in full.

But since then, what did we do? What are those economic reforms? We put new tax revenues in place so that we could pay for better healthcare — nandiyan si Secretary Pingkoy Duque inimbita ko para kasama natin ngayon — so that we could pay for a strong education system, so that we could pay for more roads gaya nang mga sinabi ni Congressman Tarzan Lazatin na nawitness sa kanyang distrito, so we could pay for more roads and ports. Binanggit ko yung ports kasi yung ating RORO system that was for the very purpose of bringing Mindanao products to Luzon at a faster and lower cost, hindi ba, Mayor? Nakatulong sa inyo.

Doon naman sa Mindanao andami nating kalsadang mga itinayo o nakumpleto, isang halimbawa lamang, yung Pagadian-Zamboanga Road, kamusta na siguro tapos na by now. It used to be called the longest road because it took the previous administration so long to build so little pero nakumpleto natin. Tapos yung Cagayan de Oro-Surigao Road, we’ve also improved that and it has cut travel time and it has revived commerce in Agusan.

We designed with our tax reforms housing policies to lift up our poorest citizens so they can live and raise a family with dignity. Our tax reforms also even if agriculture is devolved, our tax reforms funded agricultural investments, and that is a lot of it went to Mindanao which is our food basket. They strengthened our farmers as the backbone of the nation, and they gave our farmers the modern tools to feed our nation and feed their own family. At higit sa lahat, siguro para sa inyo ngayon buo na ang binabayad na IRA ninyo, ‘di ba? Hindi lang yun we even paid the IRA that past administrations owed you. At hindi lang yun dahil sa Value Added Tax kung tumataas yung Value Added Tax tumataas din yung inyong IRA. So, this year you participated in the biggest VAT increase in history of 40 billion pesos for the local government.

And I want to thank you for using your IRA bonanza to contribute to our country’s economic resiliency. At dagdag pa sa inyong IRA tinanong ko kay Monching kung binriefing na kayo tungkol doon sa mga foreign assisted projects na nahahanap ng League of Mayors, League of Municipalities, so congratulations to Monching and the leadership of LMP because they found through their global engagement creative ways of having on top of your IRA additional projects funded by various development institutions. Tinanong ko kay Monching kung pinresent niya yung Canadian Executive Service Organization — so it was presented on the first day — kasama yun ng ating Commission on ICT, our own Commission on Information and Communication Technology. I understand that the Canadian Executive Service Organization and CICT helped to computerize the tax system of 43 towns in Luzon, so sana yung mga bayan ng Mindanao ay makinabang din.

Tapos tinanong ko rin kay Monching kung pinresent rin niya yung ILO program at — yes, he did — to enable mayors, the ILO-sponsored programs to enable mayors to share best practices on livelihood projects for your workers in your constituents. So para sa mga pandagdag sa IRA na programang ganito, congratulations sa League of Municipalities!

So the LMP shares the lesson that I have been imparting that global engagement is what has been an essential component of what has kept our country from getting pulled under the raging economic waves sweeping most countries. At bahagi nitong global engagement kadadating ko lang galing sa Libya. Ang Libya ay ay isang key ally na nagpapalakas ng ating paradigm for peace in Mindanao.

Ang Libya ay laging pinanindigan tayo. Nung una pang nagkaroon ng terrorists crisis tayo nung 2001, yung Dos Palmas, nakausap ko si President Khadafi at tinatanong nga niya kung anong tulong ang kailangan natin. At sinabi ko ang tulong na gusto ko labanan yung mga terorista dahil ayaw ko magbigay ng ransom. At sabi niya, “sige, tutulong ako,” and he has been helping. Malaki ang tulong ng Libya sa pagdala ng kapayapaan at pag-asa sa Mindanao, at hindi lang yun dahil si Khadafi ay isang leader sa buong Africa na napakaraming mga Muslim countries doon. Malaki rin ang kanyang tinutulong sa pagbuo ng ating mas malalim na relasyon sa mga bansang Muslim. At kasama ng Brunei at Hapon, ang Libya, silang tatlo ang ating international monitoring team ng ceasefire natin kasama sa MILF.

Bukod doon sa pagpapalakas ng relasyon natin sa kanila ay kausap ko rin yung Khadafi Foundation, na gustong tumulong sa pamamagitan ng investment sa Mindanao. Ang pinag-usapan namin, kagabi lang yun — ay, hindi makalawang kagabi pala — ay bago ako umalis ay yung maglagay ng investments doon sa mga paligid ng Liguasan Marsh at saka Agusan Marsh. Tulungan natin silang gumawa ng magandang proyekto.

That is a very important part of our poverty alleviation program which we want to do throughout the Philippines but especially in Mindanao where six of our poorest provinces are located, namely: Tawi-Tawi, welcome! sa ating mga mayors ng Tawi- Tawi; Zamboanga del Norte; Maguindanao, diyan welcome! nakita ko yung mga mayors doon; Surigao del Norte and Dinagat Islands; and Lanao del Sur. Likewise six of our most food-poor provinces, namely: Zamboanga del Norte, Tawi-Tawi, Agusan del Sur — nakita ko yung isang mayor ng Agusan del Sur diyan — Maguindanao, Surigao del Norte and Dinagat Islands. They’re also in Mindanao. So, we are working to use the larger part, the biggest part of our resources go to our mission of alleviating poverty.

So, once again I ask you, mayors, let us work hard to fulfil our Philippine Reform Agenda until our terms end.

Let us fight for the Economy, Education, the Environment.

Bahagi ng ating poverty alleviation, we have a National Anti-Hunger Task Force — yun ang nasa ilalim ng panguluhan ni Secretary Pingkoy Duque kaya I ask him to come here today because six of the most food-poor provinces are in Mindanao. And I asked the National Nutrition Council to conduct more… no, the Anti-Poverty Task Force to conduct more frequent visits to the most food-poor provinces to identify the problems first-hand and to do what is necessary to address them as quickly as possible.

Ngayon dahil hindi naman makapunta kaagad ang National Nutrition Council sa lahat ng bahagi ng ating bansa, I asked them to create local Anti-Hunger Task Forces in the provinces to assist in locating the problems and resolving them as soon as possible. Gusto natin na yung sistema ng national and local Anti-Hunger Task Force ay palalakasin ang cooperation ng national government at local government para harapin ang problema ng kagutuman sa ating bansa sa paraang mas mabilis at mas mabisa.

There are already Regional Anti-Hunger Task Forces organized by the National Nutrition Council in such provinces as Sarangani, Zamboanga del Norte, Zamboanga Sibugay, Agusan del Sur, Maguindanao, Surigao del Norte at yung unang-una yung Lanao del Norte. Pinuntahan ko nga yung Lanao del Norte at sa interview pinaliwanag ni Governor Dimaporo, Kali Dimaporo kung ano ang ginagawa nung Anti-Hunger Task Force ng Lanao del Norte at sana magawa ito sa lahat ng mga probinsya ng Mindanao lalo na yung anim na most food-poor provinces ng Mindanao. And I hope that our mayors will be active in those councils.

Kaya tinawag ko si Secretary Duque dito para tiyakin na in those provinces that I just mentioned now we will organize local Anti-Hunger Task Forces kaagad. So meron kayong output nitong meeting ninyo dito on top of the ones that Mayor Guico was able to brief you about.

Dear Mayors, you have laid your own plans to make your respective constituents more productive and to bring your towns nearer to your growth targets. And it’s good that you’re the ones making your plans, it’s good that the league is working together to help you make your plans better and implement them better. Because it is you rather than the national government that know best how to strengthen your respective local units.

Our part was to reform the internal revenue to assure you of funds to finance your projects. All of us are doing this, national and local, for our people. We are elected officials. They have placed us where we are so we cannot but give them the best of our efforts. And as you have enjoyed your two nights and three days of I hope pleasurable meetings here in Clark, I hope that you will go back home refreshed to continue to give your constituents the best of your efforts.

Welcome once again! Daghan salamat. Thank you.
