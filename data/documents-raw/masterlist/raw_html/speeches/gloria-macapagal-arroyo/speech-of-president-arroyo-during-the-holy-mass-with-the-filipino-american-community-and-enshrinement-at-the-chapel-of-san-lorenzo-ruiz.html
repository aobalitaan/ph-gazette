Speech of President Arroyo during the Holy Mass with the Filipino-American Community and Enshrinement at the Chapel of San Lorenzo Ruiz

September 15, 2005

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the Holy Mass with the Filipino-American Community and Enshrinement at the Chapel of San Lorenzo Ruiz 

[Delivered at the San Lorenzo Ruiz Chapel, 378 Broom St. New York City, USA, September 15, 2005]

Speaker and Mrs. De Venecia and the congressmen, governors and city mayors who comprise the Philippine delegation to our United Nations activities; Secretary and Mrs. Romulo; and all of the appointive officials in the Philippine government who are here today; members of the Filipino community.

Ang dami-daming El Shaddai na narito, purihin ang Panginoon!

Most reverend Cantillas who takes care of the overseas Filipinos; Reverend Monsignor Kevin Nelan, representing the archdiocese of New York, thank you for giving this wonderful pastoral center to the Filipino community; mga minamahal kong kababayan:

Malaking karangalan na tayo ay narito. Malaking karangalan para sa akin na narito para ma-dedicate natin itong simbahan kay San Lorenzo Ruiz.

Si San Lorenzo Ruiz ay isang overseas Filipino din nung kanyang panahon. Kaya tamang-tama na siya ay hindi lamang unang santong Pilipino kung hindi na rin pwede nating isipin bilang patron saint ng overseas Filipinos.

Ipinagmamalaki natin ang overseas Filipinos pag tinatanong sa akin ng mga negosyante sa buong mundo bakit dapat kami mag-invest sa Pilipinas. Ang unang sinasagot ko, kilala naman ninyo kung gaano kagaling ang ating mga Filipino dahil meron tayong galing bilang Filipino.

There are eight million Filipinos all over the world. Kaya yung ating isang pinakaimportanteng kontribusyon sa ating outcome document ng general assembly doon sa United Nations ay yung mga paragraphs tungkol sa migration. Congratulations! Ambassador Baja for having that part of the outcome document. At talagang kayo ay ambassador ng kagalingan ng Pilipinas sa buong mundo. At hindi lamang yon, habang tinutulungan niyo ang ekonomiya ng inyong adopted land like the Untied States tuloy pa rin ang tulong niyo sa ating bansang Pilipinas.

Of the eight million Filipinos all over the world, the Filipino-Americans are about three and a half million, less than one-half of our overseas Filipinos but you send two-thirds of the remittances to the Philippines. At dahil sa inyong mga remittance ang dami-daming mga kapamilya ninyo na nagkakaroon ng magandang edukasyon, puhunan para sa sariling maliliit na negosyo, pabahay. At nagpapasalamat ako dahil ayon sa ating mga datos in our latest statistics, your remittances to the Philippines have increased by 19 percent. Thank you very much for your continuing support.

Dahil sa inyo ang kagalingang pinapakita niyo sa Amerika at sa buong mundo ginagalang tayo ng mundo. At isa sa mga pinakasimbolo ng paggalang ng mundo sa ating bansang Pilipinas ay yung pangyayari kahapon — yung pulong ng U.N. Security Council. Ang pinakamakapangyarihang institusyon ng United Nations kung saan yung mga pinakamakapangyarihang mga bansa ay mga kasapi, ang presiding officer ay walang iba hindi ang kinatawan at pangulo ng republika ng Pilipinas.

Ng paggalang ng mundo sa Pilipinas na sila ay tinanggap nila ang alok ng Pilipinas na dito sa 60th Jubilee Summit ng United Nations magkaroon din ng Summit ng U.N. Security Council. This is only the third time in the history of the U.N. na nagkaroon ng Summit ang U.N. Security Council.

Dahil ang New York ay ang financial capital ng buong mundo, ginamit na rin natin itong pagkakataon na makipagpulong sa ilang mga investors sa Pilipinas, after care ng mga kasalukuyang nag-iinvest, at promotion sa mga baka sakali malaki ang tsansang mag-invest sa Pilipinas. At inaasahan natin na itong paggalang ng buong mundo sa Pilipinas ay ma-translate sa dagdag pang mamumuhunan sa ating bansa para magkaron pa ng higit na trabaho ang ating kapwa Pilipino doon sa Pilipinas.

Today, I received a text message from the Philippines that our unemployment rate has gone down to 7.7 percent from above 8 percent in the last _____. Pero kulang pa rin yon. Kailangan pa rin natin ang mas maraming trabaho dahil milyung-milyon pa rin ang ating mga kapatid ay wala pang trabaho.

Kaya kinakailangan ko hindi lamang yung remittances that you have been so generously sending but also your prayers that we will be able to bring about the conditions in the Philippines that will be able to solve our problem of poverty and divisiveness once and for all.

Yung problema ng divisiveness ng ating bansa umabot nga na para — ngayon kasi hindi ba nung tayo ay nag-aaral nabasa natin yung “A Tale of Two Cities” by Charles Dickens — para bang ang ating Pilipinas is a tale of two countries. Para bang may dalawang bansang nakatira sa Pilipinas na pareho ang pangalan, ang tawag ay Pilipinas. Yung isa ay yung Pilipinas ng ating ekonomiya. Your remittances have helped for instance to give our country a very respectable growth rate.

Last year, 6.1 percent, the highest growth rate in the history of our country. At ngayon kahit na may oil crisis, 4.8 percent pa rin. Napakadisente. And even if we are an oil-importing country and our import bill for oil has become bigger, your remittances have helped to keep our peso stable. And we are also one of the 26 countries with the lowest price increase in the price of fuel.

At saka yung housing ang laking tulong ninyo doon. Our housing industry is bustling. Our deficit is below the ceiling by 35 billion pesos. Our revenue collection are above target by 7 billion pesos. Kaya our interest rate is lower compared to many years in the past.

Our tourism business is growing up double digit. Ito ang Pilipinas ng ating ekonomiya, ready to take off, not because of what I have done but because of what you do and because of that cumulative improvements in our economic policies over these years.

But just as our economic policies have been improving over these years, yung ating political system naman over the years has been deteriorating to the point that now it is so difficult to survive in this system and yet keep yourself ‘Lily White’ as everybody would want us to be. Somehow it paints us some shades of gray, some shades lighter or darker but it is so difficult to survive pure white. And that’s why we say, “who is without sin, let him cast the first stone.”

The system must be changed. Fundamental change is needed. We have our legislators. We have our local government officials. They must be the main instruments for change. But not only them, the people too must be the instruments for change. At tayo ay instrumento lamang. Ang talagang makakaganap ng pagbabago sa ating bansa ay yung Panginoon. And therefore, we have to dedicate our country to the divine will.

Kapwa ko Pilipino dito sa Nueva York, dito sa Amerika, tulungan ninyo ako sa inyong remittances, sa inyong panalangin. Tulungan niyo ako na makamit natin ang isang Pilipinas, hindi dalawang Pilipinas. The economy that works and the political system that doesn’t seem to be working anymore, but one Philippines. Under our patron saint, San Lorenzo Ruiz, and under the love and care of Mama Mary, Santa Maria de Dolores, that we celebrate today, the mother of the Philippines, Pilipinas Amante ti Maria.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
