Speech of President Arroyo during the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP) General Assembly

October 28, 2009

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP) General Assembly

[Delivered at the Fiesta Pavilion, Manila Hotel, City of Manila, October 28, 2009]

Maraming salamat.

Maraming salamat Secretary Claudio sa iyong pagpapakilala sa akin. Maraming salamat at congratulations Mayor Monching Guico sa iyong napakagandang mga ginawa para sa League of Municipalities of the Philippines! Don Emilio Yap, maraming salamat din dahil ang Manila Hotel ay parating matulungin para sa ating LMP tuwing meron silang gawain dito sa Maynila. Mayor Escalante, Mayor Tiu, other officials of the LMP, local chief executives, guests, ladies and gentlemen.

Una sa lahat, pagkatapos ang congratulate ko kay Mayor Guico pinupuri ko naman kayong lahat sa tulong na inihandog ninyo sa mga nasalanta ng mga nakaraang bagyo. Maraming salamat sa inyo at sa inyong mga mamamayan.

Tama yung sinabi ni Mayor Guico, sa loob ng walo o halos siyam na taong nagkasama tayo — kayo bilang punong-bayan, ako naman bilang punong-bansa — sa tuwing idinaraos natin itong General Assembly may nakikita akong bago na dapat purihin sa LMP. Napaka consistent kayo doon.

And through all of these, I am thankful that through all these years, a great majority of our mayors have always been by my side. Together, we have been seeking ways to improve the quality of governance and the delivery of services down to the grassroots. And the LMP has been, as Monching said, a key instrument in the effective performance of our municipal local government executives. Congratulations LMP!

Mayor Guico enumerated in his report the many accomplishments in these years of his watch. Most impressive has been the single-mindedness of the mayors and the LMP leadership to gain credibility as an organization devoted to good governance, and not merely a social club of politicians. Kanina doon sa ating exhibit, exhibit ‘A’ talaga, that tells us no nonsense performance has been your key to earning the support of various international organizations, among others — and many of them have exhibits to show how they had been working with you — the UN, the ILO, the USAID, the Asia Foundation, CIDA, ADB, the European Union, AusAID, them and many others. They support and their support continues to fund your development, capability-building and livelihood training programs for your towns as well as the LMP itself.

Kaya naman lalong ginaganahan ang pamunuang nasyonal na hanapan ng paraan parati para magkasama tayo. Noon ngang unang LMP meeting nung 2001 maraming long list, pero sabi ko kay Monching, antay muna ang pera ng gobyernong namana natin ay wala at… pero kaya nga nung mga nakaraang pamahalaan ni hindi kayo nakakakuha ng buo ng IRA ninyo. So sabi ko, Monching, maghanap kayo ng paraan, magsariling sikap pero ang masasabi ko yung IRA ninyo ay tatanggapin ninyong buong-buo, yun ang isang repormang ginawa natin. At hindi lang yun na nagkaroon tayo ng mga konting pera binayaran ko pa ang utang sa inyo ng mga nakaraang administrasyon. At nung nagkaroon tayo ng mas malaking pondo dahil sa VAT, aba kasama kayo doon, 40 percent ay pumupunta sa inyo three years later, and since it was implemented in 2006, yung three years later is 2009, kaya itong taon palagay ko naramdaman niyo ang pinakamalaking taas ng inyong IRA — 40 billion pesos para sa mga LGUs.

At dito mula sa mga reporma ng VAT nagkakaroon kayo ng mas malaking pondo. At sa ibabaw pa ng mga sinasabi ni Monching na nagawa ng Liga mismo, inaasahan kong gagamitin ng mga mayors yung malaking dagdag ng inyong pondo para makipagbalikat pa rin tayo sa mga programa laban sa kahirapan.

Halimbawa, sa microfinance. In the last eight years, a P150 billion pesos has been unleashed for microcredit. Much of this amount came from the government and reached the microentrepreneurs in your jurisdictions through cooperatives, accredited NGOs and rural banks. Ngunit ang mga LGU ay korporasyon din, meron kayong corporate powers. Kayo rin pwede kayong mag microfinance sa inyong… yung inyong development fund.

Isa pang panlaban sa kahirapan ay yung conditional cash transfer na tinatawag nating Pantawid Pamilya. Our firewall against economic recession includes this emergency protection for the poorest of our poor. Itong conditional cash transfer program sa kasalukuyan tumutulong sa 700,000 families among the poorest of the poor. At bago matapos ang taon, we will expand the coverage of this Pantawid Pamilya to one million families by the end of the year. Pero ang listahan natin ng poorest of the poor ay two million families. Baka naman ang mga munisipyo ay makapag-angkin ng ilan doon sa mga over and above the one million na hindi pa nakakatanggap ng conditional cash transfer.

Isa pang importanteng panlaban sa kahirapan ay ang reporma sa lupa. Sa pamamahagi ng milyun-milyong ektarya mahigit isang milyon sa inyong mga constituents na magsasaka ay naging CARP beneficiaries at 700,000 sa mga katutubo ng inyong mga upland municipalities ay may-ari na ng sariling lupa. Malaki ang maitutulong ng mga LGU para alalayan sila dahil ang Department of Agriculture ay devolved sa inyo. So, yung mga dating tenant na wala ng landlord, nag-iisa na lang sila, naninibago, baka yung Municipal Agricultural Officers ninyo ay maka-alalay sa kanila para magpatuloy ang kanilang pagsasakang maganda.

At dahil sa Philhealth naman, sakop na sa health insurance ang 86% ng ating populasyon na sila ay mga constituents ninyo ngunit gusto kong ipaalala na ayon sa batas ng Philhealth ang obligasyong na bayaran ang premium ng mga pinakamahihirap ay nasa LGU at hindi nasa national government. Kaya lang para ipakita natin kung ano ang mga biyaya ng Philhealth nagbigay ang national government ng mga premium. Pero ito ay dapat obligasyon ng local government, kaya ngayon ay malaki na ang inyong IRA dahil sa ating VAT starting this year, sana naman sa mga susunod na taon ay saluhin ng mga LGU yung inyong obligasyon na bayaran yung premium ng pinakamahihirap sa inyong mga ibat-ibang bayan.

These are some of the things that I hope that the LGUs, the municpal governments will do in our fight against poverty and of course all the programs that Monching mentioned. We could go on and on about the numerous programs that allow you to effectively cascade basic services to the most vulnerable of your constituents. And indeed, as you have been applauding as Monching mentioned your different accomplishments, you deserve the accolades because the harvest of mayors we have had over these past years has raised the bar of local governance. Kung gagamitin natin ang jargon ng mga kabataan, municipal governance has leveled-up. And much of this has come together with the reforms of the League of Municipalities of the Philippines.

Inaasahan natin that you will be able to institutionalize your best programs and methods, improve on any systems that have rendered less than satisfactory results, and seek new methods for the new challenges we will face, and Monching has identified several of them.

And so, my wish is for LMP to go forward as a stronger, more confident and more effective organization.

Let us continue to work aggressively to ensure that the growth that has been generating jobs and revenue to invest in critical social programs both of the national and municipal level to fight poverty will be sustained. Through government support at all levels and the hard work of our people, let us not be deterred by any calamity, man-made or natural, in our effort to remain growing as an economy.

Once again, Monching, maraming salamat for your exemplary leadership of the LMP. And once again, to our mayors thank you for your unwavering support.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
