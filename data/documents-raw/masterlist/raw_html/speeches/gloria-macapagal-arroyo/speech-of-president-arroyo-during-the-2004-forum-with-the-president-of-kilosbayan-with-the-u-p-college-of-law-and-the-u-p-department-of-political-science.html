Speech of President Arroyo during the 2004 Forum with the President of Kilosbayan with the U.P. College of Law and the U.P. Department of Political Science

January 9, 2004

Speech
of
Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
President of the Philippines
During the 2004 Forum with the President of Kilosbayan with the U.P. College of Law and the U.P. Department of Political Science

[Delivered at the NISMED Bldg., U.P. Diliman, Quezon City, January 9, 2004]

Thank you very much, Dean Pangalanga.

I just like to take this opportunity to tell you in public. Dean, you’re still young and I think in the fullness of God’s time, you’re going to become a justice of the Supreme Court.

Chancellor Roman, thank you for the very kind words that you said of welcome and I am indeed grateful to have a chance to be here again in my old haunts in U.P.

Former Senate President Jovito Salonga, isang iginagalang nating leader ng ating bansa at ating lipunan. Sa mga nakaraang nagpunta ako sa Kilosbayan, kinekwento ko na bago ako naging topnotcher sa Senate, siya ang naunang topnotcher. Minsan kinewento ko rin sa isang pabibisita ko sa Kilosbayan na si Senate President salonga bagama’t hindi naging Pangulo, ang tadhana niya ay maging libre ang ating bansa sa huling senyas ng kolonisasyon, at iyon ang mga base militar ng Amerika. At ngayon, gusto ko ring bigyan nang pagkikilala na may isang magandang kontibusyon. At least isa, na binigay in Senate President Salonga sa aking administrasyon. Siya ho ang napanukala na gawin kong PCGG Chairman si Haydee Yorac.

Executive Secretary Bert Romulo whom I always like to call the conscience of the Cabinet. Professor Malaya Ronas, Director Merly Tan, Attorney Capulong, at nandito rin si Justice Ordoñez sa ating listahan dito. Ayun! Kaya sabi ko, saan ‘yung pangalan ni Justice Ordoñez ang namuno ng commission na nag-investigate noong pagtakas ni Al-Ghozi. At maganda ang mga panukalang ibinigay at tinutupad yuon. At pagkatapos noon hiningi ko na lawakin niya ang papel ng Al-Ghozi ivestigative commission at gawing PNPReform Commission na kasalukuyang ginagawa niya ang mga panukala.

Marami talagang pagbabagong kailangan kagaya ng PNP reform. Talagang gusto ng taumbayang ang pagbabago. Sa aking nauunawaan sa pakikwento sa mga karaniwang Pilipino sa buong Pilipinas. Sa buong Metro Manila, ang nauunawaan ko sa kanila ay ang pagbabagong hangad nila ay tungkol sa mas mabuting buhay. Lalo na ang bagay sa pangkabuhayan gaya ng trabaho, presyo, hanapbuhay, pagkaing sa bawat mesa, kalusugan, edukasyon, tiyak na tahanan, malinis na tubig. Sa mga kandidato masasabi ko siguro. Ako ang nasa pinakamabuting kalagayan makait itong mga pagbabago dahil maryoon na akong kapangyarihan at karanasang mapipaikot ang ekonomya at lumikha ng trabaho na nakakataas ng suweldo.

Indeed, while historically our economic development has been slow compared to that of our neighbors. This once basket case of Asia posted this past year that just finished. A growth that is number five or number six among the 21 economies of Asia Pacific. It is also the highest growth since the Asian crunch of 1997.

Sabi nga nila, marami, ay kulang ‘yon. Kaya lang a question that could be asked is: ‘could anyone have done better considering the world crisis, the regional crisis and also the chaotic situation that I inherited?’

Sa maikling panahon na ako ay Pangulo, napatatag ko ang kaguluhang namana natin. Nakamit ang wala pang kapantay na pagsulong sa palupa at pabahay, kalinga sa kalusugan, at laban sa katiwalian. Sa Palupa, ngayon lamang nangyari na halos tatlong daang libong squatter ay hindi na squatter dahil nabigyan ng karpatang bilhin ang lupang tinitirkan. Matagal na hindi natatayo ng pabahay na mura ang mga developers dahil masyadong mahirap ang patakran. Pinalitan natin ‘yon. Nireporma natin’yon at ngayon nakikita na natin ang dami-daming housing projects muli. Nagkaroon ng pabahay ang higit apat na daang libong maralita at manggagawang pamilya.

Sa kalusigan naman, noong ako’y Senador, kami ni Senator Bert Romulo noon naipasa ‘yung Health Insurance Law. Ngunit halos naging Dead Letter Law kasi konti lang ang ini-enroll. Ngayon, binigyan ko nang malaking pansin sa aking pagkapangulo, ngayon nakararami na sa ating pamilya ay sakop na ng Health insurance. At sinimulan ko ‘yung programa na pinalawak ang gamot na karniwang binibili ng mahihirap na nabentang kalahati ang presyo.

We were able to finance these and many other unprecedented scales of basic services for the ordinary Filipino by our successes on the Macroeconomic front, Our growth rate is steady, our inflation has been kept under control, kaya yung pagkain na karaniwang kinakain ng mahihirap, yung kanilang bigas na regular milled rice, “yung isda na karaniwang kinakain ng mahihirap lalo na pinalawak natin ang tilapia, eto ang presyo at supply ay matatag sa duration ng aking administration.

The budget has been kept within manageable levels, fiscal performance has improved, kaya ang interest rate ay mababa hambing sa kasaysayan at maraming small enterprises, mga 20 billion pesos worth ang nakakahiram sa isang taon. Bandwidth has become cheaper and more accessible during my administration. This has accelerated our competitiveness in information technology, the international community recognize our vigilant advances in fighting corruption especially through our innovative but meaningful lifestyle checks and the procurement reforms that we were able to legislate after many, many years.

Ang mag ebidensiyang nanggaling sa lifestyle checks ay nakatulong sa mga kaso kontra sa maraming opisyal sa lebel ng Presidential appointee.

Sinampahan ng kaso ang mag huwes, prosecutor, mga opisyal halimbawa, higit sandaang opisyal ng BIR, at higit pitong daang Presidential Appointees ay na-prosecute sa ilalim ng administrasyon marami dahil sa ebedensiyang nakuha sa lifestyle check.

Etong E-Procurement law na pinasa pagkatapos ng napakaraming taong hinintay ay nakabawas sa katiwalian sa pagbili ng mga gamit ng gobyerno.

The people’s dissatisfaction about frontline government services are being address, too, there had been dramatic changes in the land transportation office, for instance,

And last year, at this time, noong ako ay lumibot, doon ko nalaman —naglibot ako sa anibersayon ng EDSA-Doon ko nalaman na ‘yong marami ng barangay pala ay walang tubig, at sa ating pag-aaral over this past year, na identify natin sa Metro Manila 300 ang waterless communities . Ngayon more than half of this 300 have been addressed either through connection, water tank, mobile tank of public faucets and other means. At sinisikap na mabuo pagdating uli sa EDSA anniversary.

Ang mga ito ay down payment, hindi ako kontento: higit pa ang dapat gawin, kailangan pa ang dagdag na installment, ngunit ang aking maialok ditto sa inyo eh ” Nothing beats experience” That’s what we need.

Our historic economic problems cannot be blamed on my single President, because the problem is fundamental. They are caused by the economic and political systems.

Si Senate President Salonga sa kanyang senate Presidency, siya ay na address niya ang ilan sa batayang problema ng ating sistema, but there are so much more that we still have to still have to change in the system.

We must change the system and usher in deep and far reaching reforms that will lead to political and social stability, and sustained economic development including constitutional reforms leading to a parliamentary and federal form of government.

Ipaglalaban ko ang walang pang kapantay na pagbabago na gagawin tayong higit na ligtas, papalakasin ang ating mga pamilya, at tatanggalin ang katiwalian, hayaan nyong pag-usapan ko itong tatlo.

Una, yung pagbabagong gagawin tayong higit na ligtas, ipaglalaban kong matigil ang epidemya ng droga at maubos ang terorisma,ngayon pa lang unprecedented na ang mga nahuli nating shabu factory, na-confiscate nating shabu at yan ay sinusunog every week, at yung drug lords, unprecedented din ang nahuli, pati yung number one drug lord na matagal nang hinahabol ng pamahalaan ay nakuha na rin, but down payment lang yan.

We need to reform our protective institutions because of the imperative of peace and order, that’s why I asked Justice Ordoñez. Secretary Ordoñezto help out there.

Yes, we increased the salaries of policemen and military personnel to make them equal to the salaries of teachers, Yes we increased the salaries of the judges but we need to increase the salaries of the prosecutors as well and we need to support the judicial reforms being done by Chief Justice Davide, and we need to reform the military and the police towards graft-free and dedicated organization with the tools to enforce peace and order throughout the land to be true protector of the people.

Detalye ng reforma sa PNP, “yon na nga po aasahan natin ang lider ng Kilosbayan, si Secretary Ordoñez as I said earlier, we increased the salaries of the judges, now we need to increase the salaries of the prosecutors because there is a serious shortage in the prosecution Service. Iyan ang pinakaimportanteng senyas na kulang ang suweldo because even the market shows that there is disparity between demand and supply at the present salary levels of prosecutors.

Ngayon punta tayo sa pagbabagong papalakasin ang pamilya. Siyempre ang pamilya ano ang hangad? Trabaho, pagkain, edukasyon, kalusugan, tahanan. Nakalikha tao ng tatlong milyong trabaho, mahigit, sa aking panguluhan. At in the last three years, bago ako nagging pangulo, isang milyon lamang ang nalikha, kaya three times ang nalikha kong trabaho. Tingnan mo nga yung ano, kinuha ko yung text message kay Pat Sto. Tomas para siguraduhin ang statistics.

But we need to create enough jobs for the average Filipino. Marami pa irn na sinasabing wala silang trabaho, and to do this, we must build the long-term underpinnings of infrastructure, telecommunications, electric power, expanded credit. We must broaden education to sharpen the intellectual competitiveness of our country. I believe in investing in education because I believe that the Philippines’ greatest resource is the Filipino skill and the Filipino mind.

But to afford this infrastructure, to afford these telecommunications, to afford this investment in education. We need structural reforms for the government to broaden its fiscal controls and capabilities. And as I said, therefore, to provide the infrastructure needed to leverage the country’s competitive advantages and promote in investment-friendly environment based on good governance and a strong, deep financial and banking system.

We must strengthen the average Filipino with reforms in agriculture where the keenest struggle for survival takes place – pagkain sa bawat mesa. No Filipino who works hard for family and country be deprived of a decent meal, and therefore, we must modernize agriculture, which we have been doing in my administration by spending at least 20 billion pesos a year. Again, an unprecendented amount inspite of our budget challenges, and because we have been spending on productivity and modernization, rice productivity has reached a new bounty with the biggest irrigation program in the history of our country and also with the propagation of a rice hybrid that doubles or even triples the harvest of the farmers who use it and use it properly. We have found new markets for tuna, carrageenan, tropical fruits. And as a boost to agriculture, the Roll-on/Roll-Off highway system, the ferry and highway system, is now reducing the cost of food transport from Mindanao, which is our food basket, to Luzon where most of our consumers reside.

Pagkain sa bawat mesa, a duty to every Filipino, a commitment that cannot be compromised no matter what.

In my administration this year, we were able to…kasi nakuha natin itong hybrid rice noong aking state visit sa China noong November of 2001. Tapos inayos ‘yung technology, dinevelop ng Philrice para maging tama para sa ating lupa sa Pilipinas, this past year, nakatanim tuloy ng 100,000 hectares sa hybrid rice, at gaya nang sinabi ko, doble at triple yung ani ng mga magsasakang gumamit nito, but we must plant 500,000 hectares more to make it 800,00 to be able to be self-sufficient in rice. And we must put real wealth in the hands of our farmers by legislating the acceptability of their farm land as collateral.

Kasi ngayon okay, na land reform ang lupa. Dati-dati ‘yung magsasaka nakakahiram sa landlord. Ngayon na lang reform siyempre hindi na magpapahiram and landlord. Hindi naman makahiram sa bangko kasi hindi nagagamit yung kanyang lupa bilang colalteral. Kaya kailangan ayusin natin ang batas para meron na siyang sariling pinanggagalingan ng kanyang puhunan.

Yes, speaking of land reform, we must do not just land reform but asset reform. Our assets are land, credit, minumim basic needs.

Kanina binanggit ko na sa aking administrasyon nakamit na natin ang mayorya ng pamilya ay sakop na ng health insurance. Gagawin nating universal ang health insurance dahil ganun ang batas noong kami ni Bert Romulo ay senador – universal health insurance. At yung mga gamut na kalahati ang presyo na nabebenta sa mga national and regional government hospitals, papalawakin natin ang abot itong gamot na kalahating presyo doon sa mga hanggang munisipyo at kung kaya hanggang barangay.

And while we have given an unprecendented number of the poor the right to buy the land they occupy, ang dami pang pwedeng napalawak diyan sa karapatang panlupa. Siguro sisikapin nating umabot sa labindalawang milyong tao at magtatayo ng tatlong milyong pabahay. Para magawa ito, gusto kong dalhin sa Pilipinas ang mabisang reporma na ginawa sa ayon sa mga ideya ni Hernando de Soto, na siya ay isa ditto sa ating mga importanteng mag-iisip sa ating bagong siglo na nata-transform ang lupa sa kapital. Mismo ang Thailand, Haiti, Mexico, El Salvador, marami sa South America ay ginagawa na itong sistema niya at nakakabigay ng kapangyarihan sa mga maralitang taga-lungsod.

Microcredit is another major reform that the Philippines has become exemplary for during our administration. We’ve been able to provide not directly by the government but by working through NGO conduits, private conduits – microcredit to one million assetless rural women. Were being praised for this system by no less than the author of the Grameen Bank model himself, Dr. Yunos. But I would like to see at least one million pesos worth of microfinance in every barangay as proven successful again in countries like Thailand.

When we talk about asset reform, we also talk about stable prices for the food for the poor. Kaya doon ko nilalagay parati, dapat pagkaing mahihirap, yung ordinaryong bigas as saka yung isda. Pinalawak ang tilapia. We have achieved this, eto rin ay asset reform. Magkakaroon tayo para sa kalakasan ng pamilya. Basic services nga yon, yung pagkain na kaya at malinis na tubig. Magkakaroon tayo ng malinis na tubig at malinis na pamahalaan. Dalawang simbolo ng basic services ang moral reforms o pagbabago, pagababagong makatanggal sa katiwalian.

As an economist, alam nyo nung ako ay nasa U.P. School of Economics, mataas talaga ang tingin ng gobyerno ng ibat-ibang ahensiya sa mga ekonomista ng U.P. ako ay naging consultant ng PIDS ng NEDA at saka nagging consultant ng Tariff Commission. So from the time as an economist, as a professional economist, as a trade official pagkatapos noon, as a senator, as vice-president and now as president. I’ve learned the complexities of the bureaucracy. I’ve learned too that fighting corruption is not like Saint Michael slaying the dragon with one swoop of his sword.

Sinikap gawin yon nung aking sinuportahan lubusan na BIR Commissioner Rene Bañez, pero hindi ganun umuobra and panglaban sa katiwalian pag burukrasya ang ating kailangan na harapin.

Fighting corruption is like healing an infected wound that must be constantly cleansed and disinfected. Kaya yung lifestyle check, talagang kailangan ang homework doon. Yung procurement reforms, kailangan ng homework doon. At hindi lamang yon, ang kultura mismo, ipaglalaban kong mapalitan ang kultura ng katiwalian para mawala na nga ito.

The down payment that we have made on anti-corruption – those hundreds or maybe almost a thousand presidential appointees prosecuted. The E-Procurement and other systemic reforms can graduate into fundamental reforms if we work hard enough on governance reform. We must use our fiscal reforms at the same time. As we are doing governance reforms to have a better compensated civil service. Doon sa Hong Kong, nagtayo sila nitong Independent Commission Against Corruption. Alam nyo sa Hong Kong, isang siyudad lang yon at malaki ang budget nila. Mayamang bansa, city state pero pitong taon bago rin nila nasabi na nagsugpo na nila ang corruption. Yung mismong nagsimula ng ICAC sa Hong Kong ay consultant ko ngayon dito sa atin laban sa katiwalian. At siya ay naniniwala sa ating sinseridad. At siguro naman hindi naman aabot ng pitong taon dahil natuto na tayo sa karanasan ng ibang mga bansa. At natuto na rin tayo sa ating sariling karanasan. Once and for all, we must end graft with a mighty hand of political will and a change of values among our citizenry.

Nakasulong tayo ngunit kailangan ko ang mandato ng sambayanan para sa batayang pagbabago sa sistema ng ating pulitika. At higit sa lahat…kanina nga nagsimba ako – araw-araw kasi nagsisimba ako – kanina nagsimba ako doon sa Nazareno dahil fiesto ngayon, sabi nga nung pari sa kanyang sermon, “we may change people that we vote for or we may not change people we vote for, but we have to change our own moral values.” At yan ay isang dhailan kung bakit mga lider ng Kilosbayan, bagama’t alam ko na pag nag-imbita kayo sa kin, isang silid ang ating audience hindi’yon libu-libong mga nakikinig ay binibigyan ko ng galang ang Kilosbayan dahil alam ko na ang pakay ng Kilosbayan ay gawin kung ano ang makakaya para nga maipag-ibayo ang mga asal, ang values ng ating bansa at ng ating lipunan.

Kaya ako’y nasisiyahan, at natutuwa na nagpapasalamat na sa bagong taon ako’y inimbita nyo para ipabahagi ang aking vision and program and platform of government. At inaasahan ko na itong vision na ito ay sang-ayon din sa mga vision ng Kilosbayan. At inaasahan ko na ang Kilosbayan ay gabay para sa mga pagbabago ng moral values na kailangan natin sa ating lipunan mismo at sa taong-bayan mismo.

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
