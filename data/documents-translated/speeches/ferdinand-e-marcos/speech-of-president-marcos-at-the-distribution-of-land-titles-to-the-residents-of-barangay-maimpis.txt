Address of

I am happy to be with the people of Barangay Maimpis, San Fernando, Pampanga. On this occasion, we once again celebrate the redemption of the people from slavery to the land. You, the people of Maimpis, form a magnificent example of the fruit of the efforts of the government and the citizens. The redemption of people like you is the main principle of our New Republic. Long live the people of Maimpis! Long live the New Republic.

The New Republic is ready to help the poor. Today, let us refresh our minds with the efforts of our poor brothers and sisters. The success achieved by Barangay Maimpis is an example of what kind of government and citizens are needed to succeed in the principle. On this occasion, we witness the redemption from the poverty of the common citizen. We all worked wholeheartedly to achieve the lofty goals. The example of Barangay Maimpis is a sign of our success against the hardship that has long oppressed the small people.

Poverty is not a natural occurrence. It is a condition not decreed by divine power. Poverty can be cured. Poverty in any society is an illness that can be cured. Suffering is not a condition imposed by any place; rather, it is a disease of society, where there is suffering, there is also a society in need of change. Breaking the chains of suffering that bind the small people is the main goal of our government; because these people, after all, are the reason why our government was established.

The government prioritizes the problem of the poor. We have already implemented many programs to fulfill the dreams of our impoverished countrymen. In this endeavor, food, clothing, and shelter were given the most attention. We have allocated a significantly larger amount to the construction of affordable housing for the common people.

The government's housing program, although varied in approach, addresses the essential needs of the poor. Land they can call their own; a home for them and their loved ones; a barangay and environment with sufficient comfort in life; and a livelihood, which is an important component for a person to live with dignity and rise out of poverty. These can be seen in Barangay Maimpis, a former estate inhabited by the poor. Maimpis is our inheritance from the past when our ancestors were shackled by poverty and bound to the land they tilled. But now? We are writing a new chapter in history that will end the sad chapter of the past. Today, our farmers are not only owners of the land they till, but also owners of the land where their homes stand. On this day, the distribution of land titles to the people of Maimpis is proof of the new republic's continued effort to restore the inherent dignity of every Filipino.

For many years, our national leaders have promised land to the landless. Every administration and every president has promised land to the landless. However, these dreams remained just dreams, unfulfilled and frustrated. But when the New Society and the New Republic were established, the land reform program was implemented vigorously and genuinely, surpassing all the achievements of previous administrations. The American Government attempted to distribute the former military lands; through laws enacted in 1954 and 1955 distributing land to former HMBs, guerrillas, and poor residents of slum areas, as well as the so-called land reform program of 1963, all of which failed due to lack of support from national leaders indebted to the owners of large estates. In the face of these obstacles, the 1972 land reform remained steadfast because it symbolized an unprecedented desire for radical change in our society; and above all, millions of farmers had the opportunity to taste the grace of social justice and prosperity that had been denied to them for centuries. The most important and invaluable success of the 1972 land reform is the fact that millions of our farmers who were formerly only tenants are now owners of the land they till.

There is no doubt that in almost ten years, the accomplishments of our land reform have exceeded our expectations. However, our work has not yet been completed; we must continue to act so that our countrymen can taste and achieve their long-cherished dreams. Because of this, land reform must be considered a continuing program until all that we want to do is fully accomplished. The slavery of our countrymen to the land they till for many years has been cured by us in less than ten years. Our land problem is complex and not easy to cure and will not be solved by a leader who does not have a far-sighted vision.

Therefore, on January 9, our government launched a more comprehensive land reform program to grant the land where their houses stand to those residing in the former haciendas. Our objective in this program is to address the problem of those who have long resided in the haciendas. This will help transform the former haciendas into havens of prosperity and ideal communities instead of nests of poverty. Once again, the problem here is co-existence and land ownership, which is the root of centuries of problems in Philippine society.

From now on, we will call this program the National Bliss Program for Communities in the haciendas. The letters of instruction that will launch this program will have important foundational policies.

First, it will be the policy of the government to protect the right to the land where the houses of those who have genuinely resided in the haciendas for many years stand.

Second, the objective of this policy is to resolve the problem and grant land to those residing in the haciendas who have long built houses in the aforementioned areas.

Thirdly, large estate owners are prohibited from evicting residents from their lands without the permission of the National Housing Authority.

Fourthly, the NHA will create this program in coordination with Local Governments. The implementation of the National Bliss Program for large estates will be funded through a program based on the annual policy.

Fifthly, the necessary funds for this program annually will be determined by the NHA and included in the national budget. Furthermore, the NHA will be authorized to issue housing bonds under Decree 757, which will be its source of funds.

Sixthly, local governments, with the assistance of the NHA, will provide assistance to these farmers and citizens in accordance with the Slum Improvement and Resettlement Program guidelines as outlined in LOI 555, 557 and 686.

Seventh, the NHA will assist and provide the necessary funds to local governments as outlined in this program.

And eighth, the NHA will select the estate or portion of the estate included in this program. This land will be divided and awarded to eligible families who will make monthly payments.

The national Bliss housing program for home sites is just one of our efforts to address our deeply rooted land problem through specific measures. What we want to solve is nothing less than the centuries-old root of discontent and unrest in our country. What we will do is a solution that will give farmers the opportunity to one day truly own the land on which they build their homes without infringing on the rights and welfare of others. Moreover, this program will further invigorate our countrymen to fully trust the changes they can make through their own efforts and perseverance. It will also further strengthen the Filipino's faith in himself, in his ability to achieve dreams that once seemed sky-high and unattainable.

Like other housing programs, the Bliss communities in the estates are also involved in our livelihood and development movement. We will continue the policy that it is not enough to simply provide people with a roof over their heads to say that they are in a home. These housing programs are about a thriving area where people have a livelihood and rely on their own intelligence and abilities because every citizen has their own opportunity for employment and everyone is a useful part and component of the area in which they live.

Our platform of change is not only focused on the goal of solving problems that have been rooted for centuries; its purpose is none other than to instill in the heart and mind of every citizen their civic duties as part of society and the place where they live. Only freely created entities with a spirit of accountability to nurture and strengthen the framework of stability that we are building can achieve this. Only freely created entities can truly carry out the struggle that we have launched and are currently undertaking.
