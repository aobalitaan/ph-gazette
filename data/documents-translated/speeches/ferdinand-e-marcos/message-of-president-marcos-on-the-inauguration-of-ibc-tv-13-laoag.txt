Message of – 13 Laoag

On this day, as the broadcast of IBC TV-13 Laoag is launched, on this occasion the First Lady and I extend our joy to all our Ilokano brothers and sisters with our heartfelt greetings. This event in the media would not have been possible were it not for the dedicated public service that guides the Intercontinental Broadcasting Corporation, it is its right to be honored and greeted.

We hope that in the coming months, the value of the service that this communication provides to the provinces and the people of the region will be strengthened. Aside from the benefits provided by the programs, I hope that this station will also provide an effective instrument so that the people may know the various developments in the region and in national progress. Indeed, this facility will greatly help not only in improving the lives of our countrymen in Ilocos but also in the service of national unity.
