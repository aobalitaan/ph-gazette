A CALL FOR UNITY

The President expresses the belief that in every Filipino flows a sense of patriotism no matter where he is or what social level he has attained.

I want to thank you for the warm welcome you have given me and my delegation. I am here to share a special moment of recollection with you, my countrymen, whose prayers and expressions of support sustained our struggle and helped us consolidate our victories.

You Filipinos residing abroad have a role to play in our future. An important chapter of our history is unfolding. Your participation is expected by our beloved countrymen – countrymen who are once again hoping for a bright future.

I ask you today. Are you still part of our nation that has experienced the rebirth of freedom and honor? Do you still unite with all Filipino citizens to achieve the ideal guided by the principles of democracy? In these questions, I know your answers will be in agreement because I believe that the patriotism of every Filipino still runs in your veins and cannot be suppressed by the temporary comforts offered by the country you now reside in.

In this mass offered for the aspirations of our countrymen, I earnestly prayed that every Filipino may awaken so that true unity may once again exist in our country. I also asked the Almighty God that all our countrymen may discover their natural abilities and importance towards the democratic principles of our nation. You possess the ability to help and participate. God has given you intelligence and diligence. Our beloved countrymen await the fulfillment of your desire to unite towards the prosperity and progress of our nation. Thank you very much.
