FULFILLMENT OF DREAMS

The dream of the farmers in the municipality of Jalajala, Rizal has finally come true. Meralco has distributed their approximately 390 lots in Jalajala to the farmers, along with various programs launched that can help the beneficiaries.

The ownership of the land they till is a long-held dream prayed for and fought for by our farmer countrymen. For them, this will bring relief from the hardships they have long endured. And this is also the key that will open the door to their progress.

This dream has come true for some of our farmer countrymen from the small town of Jalajala in the province of Rizal. You are now receiving your respective emancipation patents as proof of ownership of the land you till.

While the proposed law regarding the Comprehensive Agrarian Reform program is being discussed by our legislators in Congress and the Senate, the Meralco Foundation and Meralco are undertaking their own land reform. They have already distributed their more than 390 lots in Jalajala to farmers. And they didn't stop at just giving land, they are now implementing various programs for their beneficiaries to further improve their situation and ensure their benefit from the land they received.

Our farmers deserve our help to improve their lives. Cooperation and assistance to small and poor people is not only the duty of your government. It is also the duty of people and citizens who can be said to have more than enough for their needs. It is our duty as people as a good citizen and responsible caretaker of our fellowmen. Many of our problems as a nation can be remedied if everyone shares and works together.

The programs being implemented by the Meralco Foundation and other companies and citizens to help farmers and other people suffering in our rural areas should serve as an example to others so that we can accelerate the progress of our nation.
