Speech of the Honorable Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, President of the Philippines, at the Launch of the National Supplemental Feeding Program

A strong and healthy child is equivalent to a strong and healthy nation. Every child needs a healthy start, or a healthy beginning to their day, a healthy beginning to life.

So, Assistant Secretary Santos, what we will call this program is “Malusog na Simula” or “Healthy Start”.

This healthy start in this nutrition program aims to fill our children with hope, as well as a healthy breakfast.

We must nourish the hearts and minds of our children through the blessing of education. But before this happens, they first need full stomachs. Our children should start each day happy, healthy, and full of hope. They should only look forward to knowledge and not the basic need of food on the table during breakfast.

Providing nutritious breakfast to children, who would otherwise be hungry, is the most important thing our country can do. It is good manners, a social necessity, and economic well-being.
