When I became president, I stated my four fundamental beliefs, first of all, we need to be courageous in our national ambition. So our challenge should be that within this decade we will win the fight against poverty. Secondly, we need to raise the standard of morality in government and society in order to have a strong foundation for good governance. Our politics of personality and money should give way to a new politics of party programs and processes of dialogue with the people. And finally, I believe in leadership by example. We must uphold strong qualities such as diligence, a lifestyle of dignity, proving words with deeds, acting rather than just posing.

What we are doing now is part of my belief in the process of dialogue with the people, and the belief that we must eradicate poverty within the decade.
