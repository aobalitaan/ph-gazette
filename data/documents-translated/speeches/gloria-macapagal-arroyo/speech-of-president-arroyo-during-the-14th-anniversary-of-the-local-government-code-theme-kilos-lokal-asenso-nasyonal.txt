Speech of Her Excellency Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo President of the Philippines During the 14th Anniversary of the Local Government Code Theme: “Kilos Lokal, Asenso Nasyonal”

[Delivered at the Centennial Hall, Manila Hotel, October 10, 2005]

Thank you.

Thank you very much, Secretary Angelo Reyes, secretary of DILG.

Congratulations! Governor Aumentado sa imo nga maayong presentasyon sa “Kilos Asenso,” sa imong liderato, sa ULAP ug sa… Ubos sa imo nga liderato ang suporta ninyo kanako kanunay, salamat; Mayor Lito Atienza, ang ating host mayor at pangulo ng Metro Manila Mayors League, maraming salamat din, at congratulations sa pagpanalo ni Bobby Pacquiao. Eto mga Pacquiao brothers ang unang training nila ay ginanap dito sa City of Maynila kaya malapit na malapit sila kay Mayor Atienza; Mayor Ramon Guico and all the Municipal Mayors of the Philippines who are here today; Mayor Jerry Treñas and all the City Mayors who are here today; Vice Governor Julius Herrera and all the Vice Governors who are here today; vice Mayor Jessie Cruz and all of the vice mayors who are here today; Councilor Carlo Fortuna and all the councilors who are here today; James Marty Lim and all of the barangay officials who are here today; board member Piccio –saan yung ating Santa Claus? Saan? — and all of the board members who are here today; our Cabinet members who are here aside from Secretary Reyes, we have Secretary Neri, Secretary Favila, Secretary Ampatuan I think, Acting Secretary Pangandaman. I don’t know who else are here but thank you to all of you; Mayor Sarmiento, Secretary General, thank you for emceeing this occasion; Mayor Oca Rodriguez; other officials and members of the ULAP and local governments; guests; ladies and gentlemen.

Una sa lahat, mabuhay ang pamahalaang lokal!

And as Angie Reyes described me, I am dead serious in taking the power from the center of governance, from Metro Manila to the countryside that feeds it. Kaya naman hindi ko dinadamutan ang inyong IRA. Buo kong binibigay taun-taon sa sapat na panahon. Binabayaran ko pa yung utang sa inyo ng mga nakaraang administrasyon. And now, we will return to our LGUs through programmed monetization the unprogrammed funds that should have been part of your 2000 and 2001 IRA.

Congratulations! To the league of cities for what you presented today, your new partnership with the world bank for strategic planning, governance and resource mobilization. Keep the flame burning dahil ang national government may deficit, sinisikap na bawasan ang utang, ang mga local governments may surplus so kayo na ang gumamit ng mga credit facilities available to the national government.

At higit sa lahat, mabuhay!! Ang mga LGU sa pag-unlad, paglunsad ng “Kilos Asenso: Building Prosperity from Community to Community.” Thank you for aligning your programs through “Kilos Asenso” with our Medium- Term Philippine Development plan highlighted by our 10-point program. Thank you for keeping faith with our common vision. And because we appreciate you so much, as Governor Aumentado acknowledged earlier, we have put our national counterpart in the national budget for 2006.

Yon yung sinasabi nilang media na pork barrel raw ng local government. Hindi yon pork, ang problema naman eh pag maglalagay ng infrastructure at social services ang tawag pork barrel tuloy. Anong gusto nila, panay suweldo na lang ang ating budget? Kung suweldo lamang ang ating budget aasenso ba ang ating bansa? Kailangan programmed funds. And we thank the LGUs for activating “Kilos Asenso.” We thank you for your leadership.

Gamitin natin ang “Kilos Asenso” upang ipairal ang mga pamuhunan para lumikha ng sampung milyong trabaho. Kasama na dito microfinance. This year, almost one billion pesos of microfinance has been released to various regional and provincial conduits that will relend money to micro-entrepreneurs. Gamitin din natin ang “Kilos Asenso” upang suportahan ito upang makasuporta tayo sa tatlong milyong maliliit na pansariling negosyo at saka upang makaabot tayo sa dalawang milyong ektarya ng agri-business. Yon nga ang nilagay sa programa yung mga farm-to-market roads, yung mga bagay na yon ay para makabukas tayo ng dalawang milyong ektaryang lupa sa agri-business.

I would also like to thank you not only for the support that you have expressed earlier, halimbawa, salamat sa inyong suporta sa EO 464 — this is constitutional and it promotes the healthy separation and equality of powers among branches in government.

But not only that, even in the past, you have supported the national government. I remember in 2004 when I announced an austerity program to avert a fiscal meltdown you the local governments immediately responded with various proposals to cut your own spending and to raise your own revenues. I appreciate your spirit of sacrifice and volunteerism. Salamat sa inyo.

Salamat na rin sa mga batas na napasa, naiwasan natin ang krisis sa pananalapi at nagawa natin ang unang yugto ng ating reporma sa ekonomiya. This first phase is revenue build-up. And as we are implementing the revenue build-up, I ask you once more to be in the frontline to explain to the people the sacrifice we have to make in the short run with regard to paying more taxes. Sana tulungan niyo ako magpaliwanag sa kanila na ang dagdag na buwis ang siyang makakabigay ng eskwela, tubig, kuryente sa barangay, pautang sa maliliit na negosyo, imprastrukturang mag-eengganyo sa namumuhanang magbibigay ng trabaho. Ang dagdag na buwis ang siyang nakakagawa ng paraan para makalagay tayo ng national government counterpart sa budget sa “Kilos Asenso.”

And now the world suffers from many things including high oil prices. There is a new round of oil price hikes. And we should be proud and thankful that so far our country has been one of the 26 countries, out of 200 countries in the world, with the lowest fuel price increases even if we import oil. Thus, it is important because we are heavily dependent on imported oil. It is important to realize that even if we’re one of the 26 lowest countries with an oil price increase more oil prices may come.

And you once again are at the vanguard of price stabilization and I acknowledge your contribution in managing the threshold of affordability at the level of mass consumers.

I ask you to help us not only in price stabilization but also in our initiatives to lessen dependence on imported fuel oil by helping us develop ethanol, biomass, wind power, hydro power, geothermal power and other alternative fuels, and to facilitate oil and gas exploration in your jurisdictions.

I also call on you and all sectors to strengthen our energy conservation against the backdrop of strict implementation of the Government EnerCon Program. Alam niyo doon sa Malacañang tuwing pumapasok ako sa isang silid at giniginaw ako sabi ko, hindi yata 25 degrees ang ating air conditioning. Kaya dapat istrikto ang lahat. And I call on the LGUs once again to take the leadership role there.

And as for our legislators, I reiterate our call to them to approve the indigenous fuel bill.

Kayo mga local governments, meron na kayong special na trabaho para labanan ang terorismo and watch over the threat of avian flu. Sa terorismo when we developed a 14-point program to fight terrorism in the country and in the Asean region, that was way back in late 2001, you were among the frontliners. Indeed, in the avian flu, in the terrorism fight, in price stabilization, in the fiscal reforms, in everything, the LGU phenomenon is emerging as the saving grace of our burdened nation, and I thank you for that.

And that is why I reaffirm our commitment to reward LGUs with greater powers that are due to you via revolutionary constitutional change.

Salamat sa inyo na kayo ay napakaaktibo sa ating Consultative Constitutional Commission. At inaasahan ko na bagamat meron diyang mga vested interest na sila ay sinisikap na huwag magtagumpay ang ating systemic change, dahil nandiyan kayo, tayo ay magtatagumpay.

Mabuhay ang pamahalaang lokal!

Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat.
