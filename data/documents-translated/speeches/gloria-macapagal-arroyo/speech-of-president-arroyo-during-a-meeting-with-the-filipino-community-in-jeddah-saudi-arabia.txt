Therefore, I have directed the DOLE to implement the reforms we have made because the majority with problems are household workers. So I told the DOLE, I reminded them, to strictly enforce our reforms in order to provide household service workers with greater protection.

Second, no placement fee.

Third, mandatory skills certification to ensure that they are capable when they come here for the household job.
