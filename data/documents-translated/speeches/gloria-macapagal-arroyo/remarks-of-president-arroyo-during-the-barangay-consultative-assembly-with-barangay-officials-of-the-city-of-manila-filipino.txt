Statement of the Honorable Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, President of the Philippines, to the Barangay Consultative Assembly with the officials of the barangays of the City of Manila.

Thank you very much.

Thank you very much to Mayor Lito Atienza for your continuing support for me, Lito, thank you very much.

Thank you very much to Madame Beng Atienza. Thank you very much to Congressman Miles Roces. Thank you very much to Kim Atienza. Thank you very much to our president Caloy Castañeda. Ninety percent of all officials of Metro Manila are here, all the barangay captains and barangay officials. Thank you very much for your large attendance.

I thank Lito, Beng, all of you, because Lito and Beng love me very much, and you love those whom Lito and Beng love, so thank you all very much.

Even though we have a lot of noise in politics, I want to assure you that we are not stopping our reforms. Reforms to improve governance, reforms to uplift the lives of the poor. I will give you a scoop — because of course, you are candidates, and COMELEC is important to you — I want to announce to you, report to you that I asked the presiding justice of the Court of Appeals, Justice Brawner, to be the next Commissioner in COMELEC.

This is the statement that… yesterday, I wanted to let you know that at Land Bank and at the National Development Company there is a new six hundred million pesos for microfinance. Many of you, don't you engage in microfinance? There are ten conduits with the PCFC, are you using those ten conduits? Or the DSWD, Lito’s social worker, because there is… the DSWD can be a conduit, the DSWD of Lito can be used as a conduit so that the DSWD can borrow from the Land Bank and the National Development Company, six hundred million pesos. I’m sure the poor will feel this large amount, so get ready on how to borrow from this fund for the betterment of your livelihoods.

Manila youth, thank you very much. You have been supporting me for a long time. Thank you for your continued support. Everything I do is for the good of your future.

So thank you, Lito, for the opportunity. Thank you for this meeting. May I expect the barangays of Manila?

Thank you very much to all of you.
